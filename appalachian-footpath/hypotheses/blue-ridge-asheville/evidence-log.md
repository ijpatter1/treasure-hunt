**Source:** Postscript pages 1 & 2
**Finding:** Author confirms key constraints that favor Blue Ridge/Asheville area
**Impact:** Supports hypothesis viability
**Notes:**

Relevant constraints from Postscript:

- Not buried: Natural rock features and forest hiding spots available

The Blue Ridge Parkway corridor provides exceptional road access to the AT compared to many other trail sections, making the "3 miles from road" constraint easy to satisfy.
