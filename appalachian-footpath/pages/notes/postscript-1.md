## Key Text

> "There is no subterfuge in what I am going to say to you now. No double meanings. No misdirection. There is no subtext. No clues. No code. You should take what I say here as literally as you possibly can."

> "I have hidden none of our five treasure boxes in a dangerous place. By this, I mean it is not hazardous to arrive at any box nor does any box reside in a perilous spot."

> "No box is hidden under any body of water. You do not need to get into a raft, or canoe, or a water vessel of any kind to come to or find any of our treasure boxes."

> "No box is precariously close to a swift current or a high or dangerous ledge. And you will not need to scale a cliff or rock face to find a box."

> "There is no treasure hidden more than three miles from any road."

> "No box is hidden on private property of any kind, and there is no need to walk through private property to arrive at the location of any box."

## Key Points - Safety Rules (LITERAL, NO CODES)

**What Boxes Are NOT:**
- Not in dangerous places
- Not hazardous to reach
- Not in perilous spots
- Not under water
- Not requiring water vessels
- Not near swift currents
- Not near dangerous ledges
- Not requiring cliff/rock face climbing
- Not on private property
- Not more than 3 miles from a road

**Search Guidelines:**
- Use sound judgment
- Don't overexert
- Avoid high heat/humidity
- Bring 1 liter water per 2 hours of hiking
- Don't search in winter/snow conditions
- Be aware of hunting seasons
- Wear bright clothes during hunting season
- Search with a companion
- Always bring a phone
- Know your location and return route
