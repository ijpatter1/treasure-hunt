# Treasure Box Image

## Content Type
Full-page photograph of the Appalachian Footpath treasure box

## Image Description
The treasure box sits on a black background, showcasing its ornate design. It's a rectangular box with blue steel side panels and elaborate gold/brass decorative metalwork.

**Box Details:**
- **Top/Lid**: Silver/steel surface with gold corner brackets and a central medallion containing what appears to be a blue stone
- **Front Panel**: Deep blue steel panel with elaborate gold filigree scroll pattern in the corner
- **Construction**: Steel, carbon steel, stainless steel, and brass
- **Style**: Victorian/ornate treasure chest aesthetic
- **Size**: Appears to be approximately 6-8 inches in length

## Key Points
- Author describes this as "definitely the most ornate of all our treasures boxes"
- Blue steel created using Seth Gould's special blueing technique
- Only box that is both a combination lock AND a puzzle box
- The blue color was Seth Gould's suggestion, inspired by a blue bolt he had made
