## Top Columbus Park Candidates

### 1. Highbanks Metro Park (BEST OVERALL MATCH)

**Location**: 9466 Columbus Pike, New Albany, OH 43054

**Cryptogram Matches**:
| Clue | Feature |
|------|---------|
| ROCK | 100-foot shale bluffs over Olentangy River |
| FIELD | Extensive meadows throughout |

### 2. Quarry Trails Metro Park (BEST QUARRY MATCH)

**Location**: 2600 Dublin Road, Hilliard, OH 43228
**Size**: 182 acres

**History**: Former Marble Cliff Quarry - once the **largest contiguous quarry in the United States**

**Cryptogram Matches**:
| Clue | Feature |
|------|---------|
| ROCK | Massive limestone cliffs, quarry walls |
| STREAM | Millikin Falls (25-foot waterfall), Roberts Millikin Ditch |
| POSTS | Observation decks, Via Ferrata anchors |
| FOREST GREEN | Beech-maple forest |

**Notable Features**:
- Provided limestone for Ohio Statehouse and Columbus roads
- Turtle Cove and Swan Lake (former quarry pits)
- Via Ferrata climbing route (1,040 feet of cabled climbing)
- 6 miles of trails

**Weakness**: No clear FIELD or STRONG connection
