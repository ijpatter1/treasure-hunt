## Counter-Evidence

### No "STRONG" Match - CRITICAL
**Finding:** No Columbus park has "Strong" in the name. No obvious STRONG interpretation found.
**Impact:** Challenges (major)
**Notes:** Searched all Columbus Metro Parks - no Armstrong, Strong, Stronghold, or similar. This is the hypothesis's biggest weakness.

### Middletown Research
**Finding:** Halliday's hometown (Middletown, OH) also has no STRONG match
**Impact:** Challenges
**Notes:** Even RPO-connected locations in Ohio don't have STRONG matches. Only Strongsville does.

### RPO Mentions No Parks
**Finding:** Ready Player One does not mention any real-world outdoor parks or nature areas
**Impact:** Challenges
**Notes:** The book is set in virtual reality and dystopian urban environments. There's no geographic clue in RPO pointing to any specific park.

---

## Assessment

Columbus remains **secondary hypothesis** because:
1. Strong thematic RPO connection
2. Multiple parks match most cryptogram elements
3. **BUT: No explanation for "STRONG"** - this is fatal unless we're misinterpreting the cryptogram
