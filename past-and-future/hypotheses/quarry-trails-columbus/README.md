## Core Evidence

### 1. Ready Player One Connection (STRONGEST)

The "Past and Future Box" is explicitly themed around Ready Player One:
- Chapter XXVI extensively references Ernest Cline, Wade Watts, OASIS, James Halliday
- The box's futuristic steel/brass aesthetic mirrors the book's themes
- 1980s gaming nostalgia (Pacman, Zork, Adventure, D&D) referenced

**Ready Player One is set in Columbus, Ohio:**
- Ernest Cline worked for CompuServe in Columbus (1990s)
- The novel places Wade Watts in Columbus (2045)
- GSS (OASIS creator) headquarters: Columbus
- IOI (villain company) headquarters: Columbus

**This thematic alignment is extremely strong evidence.**

### 2. "STRONG TO STREAM" = Millikin Falls

Cryptogram: "FROM ROCK TO FIELD FROM STRONG TO STREAM"

- **STRONG** = Strong waterfall (25 feet, cascading with force)
- **STREAM** = Roberts Millikin Ditch/stream below the falls

This is a literal description of waterfall flow, not geographic wordplay.

### 3. QUARRY = Marble Cliff Quarry

The word QUARRY appears prominently in the word search.

Quarry Trails sits on the former **Marble Cliff Quarry**:
- Largest contiguous quarry in United States history
- Operated for over a century
- Provided limestone for Ohio Statehouse
- Now a public Metro Park (182 acres)

### 4. "UNDER, OVER, IN BETWEEN" = Via Ferrata

The park's Via Ferrata climbing course literally involves:
- Going OVER a 90-foot suspension bridge
- Climbing UNDER aerial walkways
- Traversing IN BETWEEN rock formations
- 1,040 feet of cabled routes with metal rungs and ladders

### 5. "WHERE BEAUTY SHEENS" = Cascading Waterfall

Millikin Falls is described as:
- "Graceful, gently cascading"
- "Stunning 25-foot waterfall"
- Water sheens as it cascades over limestone

## Park Features

### Millikin Falls Area
- 25-foot waterfall
- Upper trail (easy, short, paved)
- Lower trail (steep, unpaved, rock steps)
- Observation deck with panoramic views
- Can approach base of falls via limestone steps

### Via Ferrata
- 1,040 feet of cabled climbing
- 90-foot suspension bridge (105 ft elevation)
- Two aerial walkways
- 54-foot steel staircase
- Registration required, 90-minute average

### Trail System
| Trail | Distance | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Millikin Falls Upper | 0.05 mi | Easy, paved |
| Millikin Falls Lower | 0.8 mi | Easy, paved |
| Tall Wall Trail | 0.8 mi | Limestone cliff views |
| Lake View Trail | 0.3 mi | Easy, paved |
| Connector Trail | 0.4 mi | Swinging benches |
| Flat Rocks Trail | 0.3 mi | Paved |

### Water Features
- Millikin Falls (waterfall)
- Swan Lake (paddling, fishing)
- Turtle Cove (wildlife viewing)
- Roberts Millikin Ditch (stream)

## Search Strategy

### Priority 1: Millikin Falls Area
- "Strong to stream" + "beauty sheens"
- Check rock formations at base and top of falls
- Look "under, over, in between" limestone steps
- Examine observation deck area

### Priority 2: Via Ferrata Vicinity
- May not need to do the course
- Check around entrance/staging area
- Look for features visible from ground level
- "Under, over, in between" structures

### Priority 3: Rock/Field Transition
- Where quarry walls meet open areas
- "From rock to field" transition points
- Observation decks along Tall Wall Trail

### Priority 4: Trail Posts/Markers
- Note any numbered posts or markers
- Check observation deck posts
- Trail intersection markers

## Logistics

### Parking
- Main lots at 2600 Dublin Road
- Smaller lot at 2158 Old Dublin Road (near waterfall, fills fast)

### Hours
- 6:30 AM to dusk, year-round

### Safety Notes
- Lower falls trail is steep with rock steps
- Via ferrata requires registration and equipment
- Public Metro Parks land (no private property concerns)
- Within 3 miles of road (satisfies author's guidelines)
