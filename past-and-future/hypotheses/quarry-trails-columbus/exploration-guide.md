## Priority Search Areas

### 1. Millikin Falls (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
**Why:** "STRONG TO STREAM" + "BEAUTY SHEENS"

- **Upper Falls Trail** (0.05 mi, easy, paved)
  - Observation deck at top
  - Check around viewing area posts

- **Lower Falls Trail** (0.8 mi, steep rock steps)
  - Limestone steps approach base of falls
  - **"UNDER, OVER, IN BETWEEN"** - check rock formations at base
  - Look between/under/over limestone blocks

- **Key Features:**
  - 25-foot waterfall
  - Cascading water = "beauty sheens"
  - Rock steps provide under/over/between opportunities

### 2. Via Ferrata Vicinity
**Why:** Literal "UNDER, OVER, IN BETWEEN"

- 1,040 feet of cabled climbing routes
- 90-foot suspension bridge
- 54-foot steel staircase
- Multiple posts and anchors

**Note:** May not need to do the course - check ground-level features around:
- Staging area
- Base of climbing walls
- Posts/anchors visible from trails

### 3. Tall Wall Trail (0.8 mi)
**Why:** "FROM ROCK TO FIELD" + "POSTS"

- Views of limestone cliff walls
- Multiple observation decks
- Transition from rock walls to open areas

### 4. Lake View Trail (0.3 mi)
**Why:** "BEAUTY SHEENS" (reflective water)

- Views of Swan Lake
- Turtle Cove wildlife area
- Scenic overlooks

## Search Protocol

### Phase 1: Millikin Falls Upper
1. Park at 2158 Old Dublin Road (arrive early)
2. Take Upper Falls Trail to observation deck
3. Note all posts, markers, structural features
4. Look for hiding spots around deck area

### Phase 2: Millikin Falls Lower
1. Descend via limestone steps
2. Count steps (numbers clue?)
3. At base, look UNDER rocks, OVER ledges, BETWEEN formations
4. Check where water meets stream ("strong to stream")

### Phase 3: Via Ferrata Area
1. Walk to via ferrata staging area
2. Look at ground-level posts and anchors
3. Check base of climbing walls
4. Note any accessible "under/over/between" features

### Phase 4: Connecting Trails
1. Tall Wall Trail - rock formations and observation decks
2. Connector Trail - noted for "swinging benches"
3. Lake View Trail - water views

## Logistics

### Parking
- **Main:** 2600 Dublin Road (larger, more amenities)
- **Waterfall:** 2158 Old Dublin Road (smaller, closer to falls - fills fast)

### Best Times
- Early morning (waterfall parking fills)
- Weekdays less crowded
- Spring-fall for best conditions

### What to Bring
- Water
- Phone (charged)
- Good footwear (rock steps are steep)
- Camera (document search areas)

### Safety
- Lower falls trail has steep rock steps
- Via ferrata requires registration (if you do it)
- Public Metro Parks land
- Within 3 miles of road

**"FROM STRONG TO STREAM"** = Millikin Falls waterfall flowing to Roberts Millikin Ditch

The 25-foot waterfall is literally "strong" water becoming a "stream" below. Combined with:

- Via ferrata = literal "under, over, in between"

This creates a compelling case for Quarry Trails as the target location.
