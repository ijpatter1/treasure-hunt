## Hocking Hills: Unique Strengths

### 1. Trail Blaze Color Match (Strongest Evidence)
The words SAPPHIRE, LEMON, and LILAC appear in the word search. At Hocking Hills State Park:
- **SAPPHIRE** (blue) = Buckeye Trail / Grandma Gatewood Trail
- **LEMON** (yellow) = Whispering Cave Loop Trail
- **LILAC** (purple) = Old Man's Cave to Whispering Cave Trail

No other location has been identified where these three specific colors match actual trail markers.

### 2. "Under, Over, In Between" = Recess Caves
Hocking Hills is famous for its recess caves (rock shelters):
- **Ash Cave**: 700 feet wide, 100 feet deep, 90 feet high
- **Old Man's Cave**: Multi-level recess cave
- **Whispering Cave**: 300-foot recess cave

These formations literally allow something to be "under" the overhang, "over" the ground, and "in between" rock walls.

### 3. NINETEEN = Hocking Canal Lock 19
A specific historic landmark that matches the number word.

### 4. Cryptogram Key Spells OHIO
The O=H, I=O mapping in the cryptogram key spells out OHIO, reinforcing the state identification.

## The "STRONG TO STREAM" Debate

Three interpretations:

### 1. Hocking Hills (New)
"FROM STRONG TO STREAM" = From sturdy rock formations to creeks

### 2. Quarry Trails (Literal)
"FROM STRONG TO STREAM" = Water flowing from strong waterfall to stream
- Millikin Falls: 25-foot waterfall cascading into Roberts Millikin Ditch

"FROM STRONG TO STREAM" = Strongsville → Mill Stream Run
- Place names embedded in cryptogram
