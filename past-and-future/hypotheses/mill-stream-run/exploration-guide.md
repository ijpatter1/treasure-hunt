## Priority Search Areas

### 1. Physical Fitness Trail (18 Stations)
- Walk entire trail noting numbered posts
- Pay attention to stations 5, 9, 11, 12, 19 area (numbers from word search)
- Look for features "under, over, in between" posts

### 2. South Quarry Loop Trail (1.1 mi)
- Dirt trail along Rocky River floodplain
- Rock formations transitioning to meadow
- Interurban Streetcar remnants ("posts"?)

### 3. Baldwin Lake Overlook
- "Where beauty sheens" = reflective water
- Check scenic viewpoints
- Look between rocks/trees at overlook

### 4. Rock/Meadow Transition Zone
- Where sandstone meets grassy area
- "From rock to field" transition point
