### What is a Sugarhouse?
A sugarhouse (or sugar shack) is a building where maple sap is boiled down to make maple syrup. They are typically located in forests of **sugar maple trees** - hence "Sugar Bush Trail" nearby.

### Why This Matters
The cryptogram says: "PAST TREE AND POSTS TO FOREST GREEN"

This could literally mean:
- **PAST TREE** = Past the sugar maple trees
- **AND POSTS** = Past the sugarhouse foundation posts/chimney
- **TO FOREST GREEN** = Into the surrounding forest

## Historical Context

### Pre-1930 Land Use
- Old farms evolved into forests and meadows
- **Sugarhouse operations** (maple syrup production)
- 19th century communities: Albion, Sanderson's Corners, Slab Hollow
- Sawmills and grist mills on Rocky River's east branch

### American Indian History
- Artifacts indicate tribes frequented area **over 8,000 years ago**

### Industrial Heritage
- Baldwin and Wallace lakes = former grindstone quarries
- "Finest grindstones in the world"
