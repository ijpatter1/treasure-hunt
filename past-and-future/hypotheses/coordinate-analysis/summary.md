## NEW: Cryptogram Path Mapping (Hypothesis)

The decoded cryptogram may map to Van Sickle Trail terrain:

```
FROM ROCK TO FIELD FROM STRONG TO STREAM,
PAST TREE AND POSTS TO FOREST GREEN,
ARRIVE YOU NOW WHERE BEAUTY SHEENS.
IT'S UNDER, OVER, IN BETWEEN.
```

### Directional Analysis: Top-Down

The cryptogram reads as directions going **downhill** (high to low elevation):

| Cryptogram | Direction Evidence |
|------------|-------------------|
| ROCK TO FIELD | Rocks at high elevation → fields near lake shore (low) |
| STRONG TO STREAM | Waterfall (strong) above → stream flows down |
| FOREST GREEN | Descending through forest |
| General flow | High → Low matches starting from TRT junction |

**This suggests starting at the TRT junction (7,777 ft) and hiking DOWN toward Heavenly Village.**

### Proposed Interpretation (Top-Down)

| Cryptogram | Proposed Feature | Confidence |
|------------|------------------|------------|
| ROCK | Granite outcroppings near TRT junction | High |
| FIELD | Fields near lake shore (destination direction) | Medium |
| STRONG TO STREAM | Waterfall to stream (descending) | Medium |
| TREE | Trees on trail | High |
| POSTS | Ski lift poles (Heavenly) | **Speculative** |
| FOREST GREEN | Forest around tributaries | High |
| UNDER | First/highest crossing (near waterfall) | **Hypothesis** |
| IN BETWEEN | Middle crossing - **candidate location** | **Hypothesis** |
| OVER | Lowest crossing (near Heavenly Village) | **Hypothesis** |

### Three Creek Crossings (Top to Bottom)

Based on cryptogram direction (high → low), start from TRT and hike DOWN:

| Trailhead | Distance to Van Sickle Junction | Notes |
