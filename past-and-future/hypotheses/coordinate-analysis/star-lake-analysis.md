# Star Lake Area Analysis

*Last updated: 2026-01-13*

Analysis of Star Lake area features mapped to word search clues and cryptogram.

---

## Location Overview

**Star Lake** - 9,200 feet elevation
- Highest year-round lake in the Tahoe Basin
- Base of three peaks: **Job's Sister, Job's Peak, Freel Peak**
- Accessed via Tahoe Rim Trail from Armstrong Pass (3 miles)
- Public land, surrounded by National Forest

## Key Landmarks at Star Lake

### Natural Features
- **Crystal Peak** - large pile of white quartz above western edge of Star Lake
- **Piles of white quartz** near the trail to Freel Peak
- **Rock stairs** where TRT crosses Star Lake outfall
- **Cold Creek** - clear stream between Star Lake and Freel Peak saddle
- **Meadowy area** with plane crash debris

### Peaks Visible
1. **Job's Sister**
2. **Job's Peak**
3. **Freel Peak** (10,881') - highest in Tahoe Basin

### Forest
- Whitebark pines
- Western white pines
- "Enchanting forest" along trail

## Trail Description (Matching Cryptogram)

**Route from Armstrong Pass to Star Lake:**

1. **FROM ROCK** - Start at granite/rock formations near trailhead
2. **TO FIELD** - Cross meadow areas
3. **FROM STRONG** - Pass through Armstrong Pass (8,700')
4. **TO STREAM** - Trail crosses creek via footbridge; later passes Cold Creek
5. **PAST TREE AND POSTS** - Enter whitebark pine forest, pass TRT markers
6. **TO FOREST GREEN** - Continue through pine forest
7. **WHERE BEAUTY SHEENS** - Arrive at Star Lake (reflecting alpine lake)
8. **UNDER, OVER, IN BETWEEN** - Look for treasure among rock features
