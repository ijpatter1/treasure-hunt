## Theory Statement

The Past and Future Box is hidden in **Hocking Hills State Park** in **Hocking County, Ohio**, likely along the Whispering Cave trail where three color-blazed trails form a sequential chain: BLUE (Sapphire) → LILAC → YELLOW (Lemon) → LILAC → BLUE. ## Why JCB Would Choose This Location

### 1. Ready Player One Connection
- The novel is set in Columbus, Ohio (GSS and IOI headquarters)
- JCB explicitly cites Ready Player One as inspiration for this box
- Placing treasure in Ohio creates meta-connection to the book

### 2. "Past and Future" Theme Embodied
The location spans geological and human history:
- 330 million years ago: Blackhand sandstone formed
- 7,000+ years ago: Native American habitation
- 1790s-1840s: Pioneer settlement, canal construction
- 1840s-1890s: Hocking Canal operation (Lock 19)
- 1870s-present: Railroad/scenic railway
- Present: Ohio's most-visited state park

### 3. Grandparents Connection
- Cryptogram dedicated to JCB's grandparents
- Hocking Hills is a multi-generational family destination
- Park evokes nostalgic family vacation memories

### 4. Scenic Beauty
- Postscript says hunt should be "fun, invigorating, scenic, pleasurable"
- Hocking Hills is Ohio's most beautiful destination
- Waterfalls that "sheen" with reflected light

### 5. Perfect "Under, Over, In Between" Geometry
- Recess caves provide natural hiding spots
- Ash Cave: 700 feet wide, 100 feet deep, 90 feet high
- Box could be under overhang, over ground, between rock walls

### "Stay On Trail" Constraint Analysis

Hocking Hills State Park strictly requires: **"HIKERS MUST REMAIN ON THE MARKED TRAIL"**

This creates a practical problem:
1. How would JCB hide a treasure without going off-trail?
2. How would hunters retrieve it legally?

**Possible Resolutions:**
- The trail passes THROUGH Whispering Cave (under the overhang) - something could be reachable from the trail itself
- The "under, over, in between" spot could be at trail's edge
- A designated overlook or stopping point might have accessible hiding spots
- Park rules might be less strict at certain features (visitor center areas, etc.)
- **This constraint might indicate Hocking Hills is NOT the location**

This is a legitimate concern that requires boots-on-ground assessment.
