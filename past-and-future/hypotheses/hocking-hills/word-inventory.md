## Practical Constraints

### "Stay On Trail" Requirement

Hocking Hills State Park has strict rules: **"HIKERS MUST REMAIN ON THE MARKED TRAIL"**

This creates a practical problem for a treasure hunt:
- How would JCB hide a treasure without violating park rules?
- How would hunters retrieve it legally?

**Possible Resolutions:**
1. Trail passes THROUGH Whispering Cave - something reachable from trail
2. Hiding spot at trail's edge or designated stopping point
3. Visitor center areas may have different rules
4. **This constraint might indicate Hocking Hills is NOT the location**

This is a significant practical consideration separate from the word analysis.
