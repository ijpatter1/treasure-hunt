## 2026-01-13 - Trail Image Analysis

**Source:** https://thehockinghills.org/Whispering_Cave.htm (downloaded images)
**Analyst:** Claude

### Finding 10: Trail Map Shows All Three Colors in Sequence

**Finding:** The official Whispering Cave trail map (Whispering2020.jpg) shows:
- **Blue line** = Grandma Gatewood Trail (SAPPHIRE)
- **Magenta/Pink line** = OMC to Whispering Cave Loop (LILAC)
- **Yellow line** = Whispering Cave Loop (LEMON)

The three trails form a **sequential chain**, not an intersection:
- BLUE connects to LILAC at waypoint **OO**
- LILAC connects to YELLOW at waypoint **QQ**
- YELLOW leads to Whispering Cave at waypoint **VV**
- Return path: YELLOW→LILAC→BLUE (one-way system)

**Impact:** Strongly Supports
**Classification:** Direct
**Notes:** Visual confirmation that SAPPHIRE/LEMON/LILAC = actual trail blaze colors forming a chain: BLUE→LILAC→YELLOW→LILAC→BLUE. The sequence may represent the "five clues."
