## "SQUARE" and "THREE" Discovery

### The Old Man's Cave Burial Story

From the History page:
> The Old Man (Retzler) was found buried in a ground pocket that was **"four feet square"** and approximately **"three feet down"** to the box containing his mummified remains, cooking utensils, and flintlock gun.

**This directly connects TWO word search words:**
- **SQUARE** = The shape of the burial pit (4 feet square)
- **THREE** = The depth of burial (3 feet down)

This could indicate:
1. Old Man's Cave is significant to the treasure location
2. The numbers 4 and 3 have specific meaning
3. The burial story itself is a clue
