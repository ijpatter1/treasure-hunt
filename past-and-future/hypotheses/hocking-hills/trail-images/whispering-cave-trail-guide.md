## Trail Overview

- **Distance:** 5-mile one-way loop (3.7 to 5 miles depending on start point)
- **Type:** One-way system - NO turnaround option
- **Features:** Swinging bridge, one waterfall
- **Difficulty:** Strenuous - moderate to steep incline, uneven steps, cliff edges, steeper terrain

## Trail Color Chain (One-Way System)

**IMPORTANT:** The three colors do NOT intersect at a single point. They form a sequential chain:

```
BLUE (Sapphire) ──[OO]──► LILAC ──[QQ]──► YELLOW (Lemon)
     ▲                                          │
     │                                          ▼
     │                                    Whispering Cave (VV)
     │                                          │
     └────── LILAC ◄──[PP]──[RR]◄───────────────┘
        (at Visitor Center)
```

**Waypoints:**
| Waypoint | Location | Trail Transition |
|----------|----------|------------------|
| **OO** | Near Old Man's Cave | BLUE → LILAC |
| **RR** | Along return path | On LILAC |
| **QQ** | Junction | LILAC → YELLOW |
| **PP** | Along return path | On LILAC |
| **VV** | Whispering Cave | Destination |

**The complete sequence:** SAPPHIRE → LILAC → LEMON → LILAC → SAPPHIRE (5 segments = 5 clues?)

## Two Starting Options

### Option 1: Visitor Center Start
- Start at steps near kiosk at Visitor Center
- Halfway to Cedar Falls, catch the Purple Trail (marked with sign)

### Option 2: Upper Falls Start (Recommended)
- Start at kiosk at Upper Falls
- Follow **BLUE trail markers** 1.5 miles to Whispering Cave junction
- Take **PURPLE trail** (marked with sign)
- Loop one-way to Whispering Cave and back to Visitor Center

## Rules & Restrictions

- **HIKERS MUST REMAIN ON THE MARKED TRAIL**
- No wading or swimming in waterfalls or creeks
- Leashed pets permitted but NOT recommended (cliffs)
- Not suitable for young children
- Open year-round, dawn to dusk

---

## Key Observations for Treasure Hunt

1. **Trail blazes confirm hypothesis:** BLUE (SAPPHIRE) + PURPLE (LILAC) blazes
2. **"Note tree to the left"** - specific landmark mentioned at junction
3. **Sphinx Head** - distinctive rock formation along trail
4. **Swinging bridge** - landmark feature
