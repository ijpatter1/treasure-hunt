## Strongest Evidence

1. **Trail blaze colors match exactly** - SAPPHIRE (blue = Buckeye/Grandma Gatewood Trail), LEMON (yellow = Whispering Cave Loop), LILAC (purple = Old Man's Cave to Whispering Cave). These three specific colors are the actual trail marker colors at Hocking Hills State Park.

2. **Multiple geographic words converge** - QUARRY (Rohr Quarry + historic sandstone quarries), WATER (multiple waterfalls), PEAKS (150+ ft cliffs), RAIL (Hocking Valley Scenic Railway + historic canal) all describe Hocking Hills features.

3. **Cryptogram validates location** - "FROM ROCK TO FIELD FROM STRONG TO STREAM" matches sandstone formations to streams; "UNDER, OVER, IN BETWEEN" perfectly describes recess caves; "WHERE BEAUTY SHEENS" matches waterfalls.

4. **Ready Player One thematic connection** - Novel is set in Columbus, Ohio. JCB explicitly cites this book as inspiration for the box's theme, providing the "why" for an Ohio location.

5. **NINETEEN = Lock 19** - Hocking Canal Lock 19 is a historic landmark in the Hocking Valley, providing a specific number reference.

---

## Biggest Challenges

1. **NORTH + WEST ambiguity** - Could refer to northwest Ohio (different location) rather than directions within the park. However, northwest Ohio lacks the trail blaze color matches.

2. **Number sequence interpretation** - The numbers 19, 12, 11, 9, 5, 3, 0 beyond NINETEEN = Lock 19 are not yet explained. Could be step counts, coordinates, or trail markers.

3. **Exact location unknown** - While the park is identified, the specific hiding spot (Old Man's Cave area vs. Ash Cave vs. elsewhere) requires physical reconnaissance.
