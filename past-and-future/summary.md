# Past and Future Box - Summary

*Last updated: 2026-01-13*

## Goal

**Find the Past and Future treasure box** - one of five treasure boxes hidden somewhere in the United States by author Jon Collins-Black, as detailed in his book "There's Treasure Inside."

## Book Overview

- **Chapter:** XXVI
- **Title:** THE PAST AND FUTURE BOX
- **Structure:** Part Two box (one of four smaller boxes, separate from Lion's Share)
