## Why Colorado Wins Despite NC Personal Connection

1. **The poem is the primary clue vehicle.** The author confirms Joy's Serenade is "pretty important" for the Lion's Share. The poem describes the LOCATION, not the author's life story.

2. **Seven independent clue threads converge.** Pike, magic water, gold circles, Will, conductor, dancers, and oaks ALL point to the same 10-mile radius. No other US location matches more than 3.

3. **NC fails on the most specific clues.** The 4 most geographically distinctive poem elements (pike, magic water, circles of gold, Will) have NO credible NC interpretation.

4. **The author knows the Rockies.** He searched for Fenn's treasure in the Rocky Mountains — he's familiar with the Colorado/Wyoming/Montana region.

5. **"No personal connection" is assumed, not proven.** The author may have deep affection for Colorado Springs/Garden of the Gods without stating it in the book.
