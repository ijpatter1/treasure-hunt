## HIGH-SIGNIFICANCE FINDINGS

### 1. Page 18 — Fishing/Lake Scene (STRONGEST)
The most detailed nature landscape in the entire book. Echoes multiple Joy's Serenade clues:
- **Pine trees**: Dense pine forest lines entire lake shoreline → "sparkle even as you pine"
- **Lake/water**: Calm lake dominates → "magic in the water"
- **Fishing**: Father and son fishing from wooden dock → "pike are rather all around" (pike = fish)
- **Elevated forested terrain**: Trees on higher ground above lake → "don't go down / how high"
- **Mixed tree types**: Both deciduous (round canopy, possibly oaks) AND conifers/pines
- **Sunset/sunrise colors**: Pink, purple, yellow sky over lake

### 2. Back Cover — Purple Butterfly ("Dancers")
- Purple butterfly prominently featured flying near stone bird bath
- **Hopi call butterflies "dancers"** (Butterfly Dance ceremony)
- Chapter 8 of main book is about Hopi culture AND is where JCB states children's book contains clues
- "Dancers are on the land" = butterflies at/near treasure site
- Green caterpillar on page 6 establishes transformation motif → draws attention to butterfly
