## Refined pinpoint(s) — feature-by-feature, and why each was refuted

The refinement drilled the #1 region (Spruce Pine / Bakersville) for an actual coordinate. Three pins + an honest verdict were produced; **all were refuted** in adversarial review.

### Pin A — Rose Creek Trail / Heffner Gap Overlook, BRP MP 325.9–326 (~3,057–3,067 ft) — proposed LEADING PINPOINT
*Approx ~35.83 N, -82.00 W (verify on the ground).*

- **Claimed fit:** oak / mixed oak-pine canopy with mountain-laurel understory; in the Spruce Pine pegmatite district (mica/quartz sparkle); Rose Creek paralleling the lower trail (water beside you, no crossing); peak-ring of Linville/Honeycutt/Table Rock from the gap; trail-meets-Parkway "X" via the Overmountain Victory NHT; lightly trafficked (~58 AllTrails reviews); good dark sky; public NPS/OVT land, ≤1.5 mi one-way, easy grade, carryable; NC open, not the AT.
- **REFUTED — reasons (multiple, any one sufficient):**
  1. **No pin-level textual convergence.** Repo-wide grep found ZERO references to Heffner Gap, Rose Creek, Overmountain Victory Trail, Table Rock, Linville Mtn, or Honeycutt in chapters/ or pages/. The only tie is sitting inside the broad ~40-mi-NE radius (which also contains Bakersville, Roan, Mt. Mitchell, hundreds of sq mi). Pin sits in **McDowell County**, off the NE bearing, ~13 mi from the named town Bakersville (Mitchell Co). Genuine *region*-convergence, **zero pin-convergence** = the Pikes-Peak trap.
  2. **Conjunction split / features don't co-occur.** Heffner Gap Overlook has **NO water in view** (Virtual Blue Ridge: "Water Features: None"); Rose Creek is only beside the LOWER trail. So "high vantage + magic in the water + sparkling sit-rock" never co-occur at one point.
  3. **Lead uniqueness claim is FALSE (source conflation).** The thrice-repeated "oak and mixed oak-pine forests… dense understory of mountain laurel" belongs to the separate **Saddle Mountain Trail**; the Rose Creek Trail source actually says it "follows Little Rose Creek… through oak and COVE forests" (cove = hardwood/hemlock, NOT pine). The oak-PINE element rests on a misattribution.
  4. **Peak-ring / high-vantage contradicted by its own sources.** NPPlan calls Heffner Gap "a bit boring," "straight-on views," with "trees… that block the view of the lower half of most of the mountains." It's a 3,057-ft roadside GAP pull-off with a flat, partly-screened, one-directional view — not a high open 360° peak-ring. "Pike all around" fails; the "how high" cue is weak (a low gap, not a summit).
  5. **"Sparkle" is non-distinctive.** District-wide pegmatite/mica trait across a ~10×20 mi three-county area; no named sittable glittering outcrop documented on this trail. Heffner Gap (McDowell Co) is at the southern margin OUTSIDE the pegmatite core (Avery/Mitchell/Yancey).
  6. **HARD-CONSTRAINT VIOLATION — active federal closure.** NPS BRP status (2026-05-28): MP 325.9 is inside MP 324.7–327.5, **"Recreation of any kind is PROHIBITED"** (Helene damage; corridor not projected fully open until end of 2026). AllTrails also reports the Rose Creek Trail itself sustained "significant damage… numerous downed trees." **Legally off-limits now.**

### Pin B — Three Knobs Trail / Deer Lick Gap Overlook, BRP MP 337.2–338.8 (~3,452–3,875 ft) — proposed STRONG ALTERNATE
*Approx ~35.76 N, -82.18 W (verify on the ground).*

- **Claimed fit:** explicit mixed forest list "oak… and pine"; in the pegmatite district; Deer Lick Gap named for saltpeter rocks deer lick (freestanding gap rocks); Three Knobs Overlook (3,875 ft) over Seven Mile Ridge with the Black Mtns (Mt Mitchell massif) behind; trail leaves from the overlook ("X"); same dark-sky corridor; low-profile.
- **REFUTED — reasons:**
  1. **Water FAILS (conceded).** Neither overlook has a creek/river/lake in view; Crabtree Falls (MP 339.5) is not visible and needs a separate descent. "Magic in the water" (treated as hard here) is unmet.
  2. **Sparkle geologically wrong here.** Deer Lick Gap is in **McDowell County, OUTSIDE** the Spruce Pine pegmatite belt (Avery/Mitchell/Yancey along the North Toe), on Ashe Metamorphic Suite gneiss/amphibolite — the same "gneiss, no on-site sparkle" error that killed the Roan pins. A *saltpeter* deer-lick is a salt efflorescence, supporting neither grit nor sparkle.
  3. **Peak-ring overstated / "X" fails at the stated point.** NPS/Virtual Blue Ridge: Deer Lick Gap (MP 337.2) has **NO trails** and a 2/5 view "completely obscured by tall trees and brush," showing only Wood Mountain. The actual Black Mtns panorama is at Three Knobs (MP 338.8) — a *different* overlook 1.6 mi away. The fit is manufactured by conflating two separate spots. The "Three Knobs Trail" is essentially undocumented.
  4. **Convergence missing & geometry wrong.** Computed: this overlook is ~29 mi from Asheville at bearing ~57° (ENE), 13–16 mi SOUTH of Bakersville in a different county — NOT on the NE bearing. The claim of being "closest to the Bakersville/NC-226 core" is FALSE; it's farther from Bakersville than the named gem towns. No chapter/index/author signal lands here.
  5. **HARD-CONSTRAINT VIOLATION — active closure.** NPS BRP status (2026-05-28): MP 333.9–342.1 (containing both overlooks) is a Helene CLOSURE with **"recreation prohibited"**; reopening not expected until late 2026. Self-rated confidence "low."

### Pin C — Museum of NC Minerals pegmatite boulder + NC-226 "X", BRP MP 331 (~2,805 ft, Gillespie Gap) — LITERAL ANCHOR, self-disqualified as a hide
