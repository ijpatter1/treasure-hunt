## 1. Real-world constraints (firmest facts first)

These are the load-bearing facts. Everything downstream must respect them.

**No box has been found anywhere — including the Lion's Share.** The official site states verbatim: _"As of today, I am aware of no boxes that have been found. When a box is found I will announce it here."_ ([treasureinside.com](https://treasureinside.com/)). This held through the one-year anniversary (Nov 2025) and into 2026 ([Mysterious Writings](https://mysteriouswritings.com/theres-treasure-inside-by-jon-collins-black/)). **Consequence:** no recovered box geographically constrains the Lion's Share. Every region is still open, and nothing rules Rocky Face in or out by a "find."

**One box maximum per state.** ([treasureinside.com/the-treasure](https://treasureinside.com/the-treasure/)). This is the single most useful elimination tool we have.

**The Forrest Fenn box is in the Rocky Mountains (NM / CO / WY / MT)**, hidden at a real place the author visited while searching for Fenn's treasure ([MW Substack, 9/19/25](https://mysteriouswritings.substack.com/p/weekly-treasure-recap-91925-featuring)). **Consequence:** if that box is in Colorado, the one-box-per-state rule excludes a Colorado Lion's Share. This is the hard structural problem with the Pikes Peak read — see §3.

**The author actively warns against the North Carolina assumption.** Regarding the Appalachian Trail box: _"I think a lot of people assume it must be hidden in North Carolina, but I remind people the Appalachian Trail is a big place."_ ([MW Substack, 9/19/25](https://mysteriouswritings.substack.com/p/weekly-treasure-recap-91925-featuring)). This is about the AT box, not the Lion's Share, but it directly undercuts the "JCB is from NC, therefore NC" reasoning that drove the failed Rocky Face search.

**No "grand cipher" is required**, and the author **declines to eliminate any state** ("I do not feel the need to eliminate states") and says **do not rule out National Forests or National Parks** ([MW FAQ](https://mysteriouswritings.com/theres-treasure-inside-faq-treasure-hunt-by-jon-collins-black/)). The "no grand cipher" line is direct evidence _against_ the letter-extraction and number-to-state methods underpinning most community theories — including the failed "count haiku syllables to marker 318" method.

**Hard accessibility filters (author-confirmed):** not buried, not on private property, not in man-made structures, outdoors, within 3 miles of a road, no rock climbing / cliff scaling / river crossing, don't search in snow ([MW FAQ](https://mysteriouswritings.com/theres-treasure-inside-faq-treasure-hunt-by-jon-collins-black/)). The Lion's Share box is >25 lb and took **two trips** to place ([MW](https://mysteriouswritings.com/theres-treasure-inside-clues-found-what-is-known-to-date/)) — argues for a carryable distance from parking (a couple hours' walk max), not deep backcountry.

**The children's book clue is REAL.** The author confirmed he placed _"an extra clue or two in Our Unbreakable Thread to assist in finding the largest treasure box"_ ([MW Substack](https://mysteriouswritings.substack.com/p/theres-treasure-inside-lions-share)). The illustrator (Nicole Gsell) confirmed three deliberate post-submission edits: a **butterfly added**, the **baby's hairline modified**, a **green blob deleted** ([MW Substack, 7/4/25](https://mysteriouswritings.substack.com/p/weekly-treasure-recap-7425-featured)). These are NOT required, but they are author-endorsed and remain **undecoded into any location by anyone.** _(Caveat: a later direct-inspection vision pass — see FINAL-REPORT.md — judged the butterfly decorative and found the hairline/green-blob edits still uncracked.)_

**The one UNCERTAIN fact that matters most:** the author reportedly said _"I don't think there is any information in the poem or related to part 2 of the book"_ for the Lion's Share. Joy's Serenade is in Part 2. If true, this **invalidates every poem-driven solve — both Rocky Face AND Pikes Peak.** Adversarial verification rated this **UNCERTAIN** (secondhand attribution via Mysterious Writings, not confirmed against primary audio). It conflicts with a separate, vaguer claim that the poem is "pretty important." **Resolve this before any poem-based trip.**

## 2. Ranked location leads

Ranking logic: weight (a) author-confirmed constraints, (b) clues that survive the "no grand cipher / be literal" rule, and (c) physical accessibility — and heavily discount any lead that depends on the poem alone (per the UNCERTAIN "no info in the poem" statement) or on letter/number extraction.

No lead is strong. There is no community consensus and no biographical or author signal that pins the Lion's Share to any state. Treat the entire ranking as "least-bad first," not "likely."

### #1 — Washington State: Olympic Peninsula / US-101 corridor
- **Sub-area:** Olympic National Park / Olympic National Forest, accessible from the US-101 loop. Highest-probability micro-areas are road-adjacent old-growth/lake sites: Lake Crescent (NW Olympic, US-101 runs along its south shore, ~48.06 N, -123.80 W), Sol Duc, or the Quinault rainforest loop. All are within 3 mi of US-101 and non-technical.
- **Clues it satisfies (non-poem, which is its strength):** George Washington's "Jelly Glass" is a treasure item (Ch.17) → Washington State; two Olympic gold-medal items (Ch.15, 16) + "Olympic" Peninsula; p.27 mentions "101" → US-101, which **begins in LA (author's home) and ends in Washington** ([MW Top Theories](https://mysteriouswritings.com/top-location-theories-lions-share-box-theres-treasure-inside-by-jon-collins-black/)). It is one of two states the hub analyst Jenny Kile "likes a lot."
- **Biggest weakness:** the chain is pun/wordplay-based (Washington item → Washington State; Olympic medal → Olympic Peninsula), not a direct location clue, and has no biographical anchor.
- **One BOTG next step:** too large to search blind. Next step is **not** travel yet — test the children's-book art against the Olympic Peninsula. Only travel after a sub-mile target is identified.

### #2 — South Dakota: Black Hills (Black Hills NF / Custer State Park environs)
- **Sub-area:** Black Hills National Forest / Custer State Park, near US-16 / the Needles Highway / Iron Mountain Road, all road-accessible and non-technical (~43.76 N, -103.42 W region). Fits "central/largest box, central location."
- **Clues it satisfies:** deliberately **missing page 90** = Interstate 90 (runs through SD); SD as the geographic center of the 50 states; p.101 "X in waves" read as the Black Hills ([MW Top Theories](https://mysteriouswritings.com/top-location-theories-lions-share-box-theres-treasure-inside-by-jon-collins-black/)). The other state Kile "likes a lot."
- **Biggest weakness:** entirely dependent on number/anomaly decoding (missing page → highway number) — exactly the cipher-style method the author downplays. No author or biographical anchor.
- **One BOTG next step:** same as WA — too broad to search blind. Resolve the children's-book art and the missing-page-90 reasoning to a sub-area first.

### #3 — North Carolina: Great Smoky Mountains (far western NC) — NOT Rocky Face/Alexander County
- **Sub-area:** Great Smoky Mountains NP / Pisgah-Nantahala NF, far western NC. Note this is **~150 mi from Alexander County** — the community NC read points here, not to the author's home county.
- **Clues it satisfies (literal):** p.119 (Ch.13) _"Fifty boys showed up that day. Only twelve of us would make a team"_ = USA (50) + NC as the 12th state to ratify; p.128 image of burning woods "in the mountains" → Smoky Mountains; Cardinal (NC state bird) and milk (NC state beverage) in prose ([MW Top Theories](https://mysteriouswritings.com/top-location-theories-lions-share-box-theres-treasure-inside-by-jon-collins-black/)). Author's documented NC upbringing.
- **Biggest weakness:** the author publicly cautioned NC is over-assumed; support relies on number-to-state mapping ("no grand cipher" argues against it); points to the Smokies, **not** the failed Rocky Face site.
- **One BOTG next step:** if pursuing NC, abandon Alexander County entirely and identify a specific Smokies/Pisgah road-accessible granite-and-trout site first.

### #4 — Stone Mountain State Park, NC (best literal-poem NC site)
- **Sub-area:** Stone Mountain Loop Trail to the 600-ft granite dome, ~36.39 N, -81.04 W. Free, single-day, within 3 mi of a road, ~35-40 mi from the author's Hiddenite roots.
- **Clues it satisfies (poem/haiku, literal):** exposed 600-ft granite dome = haiku _"Be solid, have grit"_ and _"sat upon a rock, allowing nature's wide embrace"_; oak slopes = _"shady oaks"_; 17+ mi of designated trout waters = _"magic in the water, but the pike are rather all around"_; documented butterflies = _"Dancers are on the land"_.
- **Biggest weakness:** pure poem-imagery match — moot if "no info in the poem" holds; imagery is non-unique; no in-book/author clue names this park.
- **One BOTG next step:** only worth a day-trip if you've decided to bet on the poem AND want the strongest NC poem-fit. Lower priority than resolving the poem question itself.

### #5 — Pikes Peak / Manitou Springs / Colorado Springs, CO
- **Sub-area:** North Cheyenne Cañon Park & the Gold Camp Road corridor (old Short Line railroad grade), secondarily Garden of the Gods.
- **Clues it satisfies (best multi-line LITERAL poem fit of any candidate):** _"the pike are rather all around"_ = Pikes Peak inside **Pike** National Forest; _"magic in the water"_ = Manitou Springs' effervescent mineral springs; _"shimmering circles of gold"_ = golden aspen (near-absent in NC); _"Our conductor... gives direction" / "Will's straight edge"_ = Gen. **Will**iam Jackson Palmer's Short Line railroad → Gold Camp Road; _"sat upon a rock"_ = Garden of the Gods.
- **Biggest weakness (three strikes):** (1) the Rocky-Mountain Fenn box + one-box-per-state may already occupy Colorado; (2) the "no info in the poem" statement, if true, is fatal because this read is **entirely** poem-driven; (3) zero documented author-Colorado connection.
- **One BOTG next step:** **do not travel.** First establish whether the Fenn box is in CO and resolve the "no info in poem" question.

### #6 — Lake Tahoe "Near LT" (CA/NV) — tentative
- Derived from Ch.8 / p.85 Hopi-calendar over/underlined letters reading "NEAR LT" (also spells "ANTLER"). **Weakness:** depends on letter-extraction the author disavows; community ties Tahoe more strongly to the smaller box; already searched. **Next step:** none recommended.

### #7 — Michigan — lowest priority
- "Mitten" (p.11) = MI shape; chapters 9/13 → M/I; "magic in the water" → "Lake MIChIGAn". **Weakness:** almost entirely cipher-style wordplay; weakest evidence base. **Next step:** none recommended.

### Off-ranking — "113° W" children's-book numeric lead
A community "113" (ebook-vs-hardcover page difference) read as longitude 113° W → Wisdom, MT / Lukeville, AZ. Relies on page-number counting (a "no codes" violation); a single longitude line is not a location. Curiosity, not a destination.

## 3. Colorado Springs vs. Rocky Face NC — head-to-head

| Dimension | Pikes Peak / Colorado Springs | Rocky Face Mountain, NC |
|---|---|---|
| **"the pike are rather all around"** | **Strong, literal** — Pikes Peak in Pike NF | Weak — no "pike" place-name; only trout-stream stretch |
| **"magic in the water"** | **Strong** — Manitou's effervescent mineral springs | Weak — ordinary creeks |
| **"shimmering circles of gold"** | **Strong** — golden aspen (aspen near-absent in NC) | **Fails** — NC golden fall color is tulip poplar/hickory, not aspen |
| **"Will's straight edge" / "conductor gives direction"** | **Strong** — Will Palmer's Short Line railroad / Gold Camp Road | None |
| **"sat upon a rock" / "be solid, have grit"** | Good — Garden of the Gods | **Good** — literal granite "rocky face" dome |
| **"Dancers are on the land"** | Butterflies present | **Good** — two rare butterflies |
| **Biographical anchor** | **None** | **Strong** — author's childhood gem-digging county |
| **One-box-per-state risk** | **High** — possible CO Fenn box excludes it | Low |
| **Already searched / failed** | No | **Yes** — BOTG search failed |

**On pure literal poem fit, Colorado Springs decisively wins.** Rocky Face won the original hypothesis on **biography**, not on the poem — and "shimmering circles of gold" (aspen) actively fails in NC.

**Verdict: do NOT pivot to Colorado Springs, and do NOT return to Rocky Face.**

1. Both reads share their fatal flaw: both are poem-driven, and the author may have said the poem holds no Lion's Share info. You can't fix a failed poem-solve by substituting a prettier poem-solve when the method itself is in doubt.
2. Colorado has a structural disqualifier Rocky Face doesn't: the confirmed Rocky-Mountain Fenn box + one-box-per-state. Combined with zero author-CO connection and CO's absence from community theories, it's not worth a cross-country trip on poem coincidence alone.
3. Rocky Face is doubly spent: already searched, and the "marker 318" target came from a "no grand cipher" violation. No materially new reason to return.
