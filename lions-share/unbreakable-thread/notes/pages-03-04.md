## Thread Properties Revealed

This spread reveals crucial information about the thread:

1. **Invisible** - Cannot be seen ("Although you could not see it")
2. **Bilateral** - Connected to BOTH parent and child ("tied around my finger too")
3. **Elastic** - Can stretch as needed ("the thread needed to stretch")
4. **Adaptive** - Grows with the child's developing independence

## Symbolism

The parallel structure of the pages (father on one side, mother on other, child between) represents:
- Both parents connected to child
- Child moving between parents
- Thread stretching but not breaking
- Physical distance ≠ loss of connection
