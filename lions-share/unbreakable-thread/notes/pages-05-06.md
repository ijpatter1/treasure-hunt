## Symbolic Interpretation

### "Tangles"
The tangled thread metaphor elegantly captures:
- Toddler tantrums/emotional dysregulation
- Life's difficulties and challenges
- The messiness of growing up
- Things that temporarily complicate the connection

### "Undo the tangles"
- Parental patience and problem-solving
- Unconditional love through difficult moments
- The thread never breaks, just needs adjustment

### "Cover your eyes"
- Security blanket feeling
- Parental protection during vulnerability (sleep)
- Thread as active comfort, not just passive connection
