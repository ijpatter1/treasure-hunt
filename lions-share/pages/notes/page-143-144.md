## Left Page (143) - Nigerian Soccer History

### Nigerian Soccer Background
- England founded first national team in 1863
- Nigeria didn't have team until **1949** (86 years later)
- Before 1996: Qualified for Olympics 3 times, never won a game
- Record: Outscored 22-7 in Olympic play

### Key Figures
- **Sunday Oliseh** - Nigerian midfielder, joined team 1995
- **Jo Bonfrere** - Dutch head coach, in conflict with NFA (Nigerian Football Association)

### Team Challenges
- **Tribal politics** - Nigeria has hundreds of tribes with animosities
- NFA corruption - tribal politics over skill in team selections
- Financial crisis - NFA stopped paying Bonfrere's salary
- Coach was planning to resign during Olympic qualifying

### Olympic Qualifying
- Needed tie against Egypt in Cairo to continue
- Got the draw, qualified for Olympics
- Pool opponents: Hungary, Japan, **Brazil (world #1)**
