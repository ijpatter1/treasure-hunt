## Page 82 (left, dark) - Sonwai Ring Photo + Sidebar; Page 83 (right) - Sonwai & Flying for Inspiration

### Content
- Charles would mentor only two people in jewelry making
- One is his niece: **Sonwai** (real name **Verma Nequatewa**)
- She still makes jewelry today
- **"One of her rings is now in our treasure"**
- **Sonwai = feminine form of "beauty"** (Loloma = masculine form)
- Started studying at age 17, worked with uncle for 20+ years
- Her jewelry is as rare as her uncle's
- Creates only limited number of pieces per year

### **FLYING FOR INSPIRATION:**
- **"Like her uncle, Sonwai owns a plane and is a pilot"**
- Charles and Sonwai flew together often
- From the sky they'd see rock formations below
- These views became inspiration for inlay designs
- Sonwai quote: **"We used our planes to fly around Hopi and places like Canyon de Chelly to look at rock formations. The stones on the mesas are how our inlay designs came to be. It's a bird's eye view."**

### **SIDEBAR - SONWAI RING IN TREASURE:**
- **Hand made by Verma Nequatewa (Sonwai)**
- **Materials (stones): Sugilite, turquoise, coral, lapis lazuli, gold**
- **Estimated value: Over $20,000**
- **"This purple ring, along with the two Loloma rings in our treasure, was exhibited at the 'Loloma' exhibit at the Wheelwright Museum of Native American Art in Santa Fe"** (note: exhibited, not purchased)

### Potential Clues
- **Canyon de Chelly** - where they flew for inspiration
- **"Bird's eye view"** - aerial perspective theme
- **Mesas** - rock formations
- **Wheelwright Museum of Native American Art in Santa Fe** - where the rings were exhibited (the "Loloma" exhibit)

### Geographic References

- Hopi mesas

### Content
## MORE ABOUT THE HOPI
- Hopi migrated from Mexico over 2,500 years ago
- 1600s: Spanish arrived with smallpox, devastated Hopi population
- 1800s: U.S. government seized much of Hopi land
- Government tried to eradicate Hopi culture:
  - Forced children to be educated away from tribes
  - Forced Hopi to cut their hair
  - Pressured conversion to Christianity
- When Hopi resisted, **chief Loma-hongvoma was sent to prison in Alcatraz**

### **ALCATRAZ - TREASURE ISLAND CONNECTION:**
- **"Alcatraz stands less than three miles from Treasure Island where Charles Loloma's murals later blazed"**

### Potential Clues
- **Alcatraz** - <3 miles from Treasure Island
- San Francisco Bay geography
- Loma-hongvoma (Hopi chief)

### Geographic References
