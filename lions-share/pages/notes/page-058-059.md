## Left Page (58) - Art Smith's Origins

### Content
- April 19, 1879: **Mary Elizabeth Williams** born in **Morgan's Pass, Jamaica**
- Mary learned to read from local priest, read Bible scriptures
- Mary took job as hotel maid in **Kingston, Jamaica**
- Met **James Smith** - bonded over love of literature
- Married, moved to **Cuba** for business opportunities
- Mary gave birth to **Arthur George Smith** (Art Smith)
- **July 21, 1920**: Family boarded ship from Cuba to **New York City**
- Art was bullied for Jamaican accent but remained committed

## Right Page (59) - Cooper Union & World's Fair

### Content
- 1934: No commercially successful black artists in US
- Art granted full scholarship to **Cooper Union** (art, engineering, architecture)
- Art Smith became one of only **6 black students** enrolled
- Cities competing to host **World's Fair**
- **New York World's Fair Corporation** hosted design contest
- **Art Smith won first prize** - design for multi-purpose building
- Advisors wanted Art to pursue civil engineering
- **Art politely turned down** - had no passion for engineering
- Art dropped painting class for **sculpture and three-dimensional design**
- Less than 1% of black men held college degrees when Art graduated

### Potential Clues
- **Cooper Union** - prestigious school in NYC
- **New York World's Fair** connection
- Art's determination to follow his own path

### Geographic References
- Cooper Union, New York City
- New York World's Fair

### Names/Dates
- 1934 (Art proclaimed he'd become artist)
- World's Fair timing (late 1930s)

- Medium - NYC focus but no direct treasure location clues
