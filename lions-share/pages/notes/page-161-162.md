## Right Page (162) - Carnegie Biography

### Carnegie's Life Story
- **Born: Dunfermline, Scotland**
- Dunfermline known for its linen factories
- Family was poor - entire family slept in the small upstairs of their stone house (bottom floor held father's loom); mother mended shoes to pay for food
- **Immigrated to United States: 1848**
- Mother decided to move to **Pittsburgh**
- **Age at immigration: 13**
- **First job: Bobbin boy in textile mill**
  - 12 hours/day, 6 days/week
  - Pennies a day; made $1.20 per week
  - "Illegal child laborer" by today's standards
- Got job as messenger boy at Western Union
- Impressed Thomas A. Scott, who hired him as his own personal secretary
- Scott later became VP of Pennsylvania Railroad
- Promoted to **superintendent of western division** at Pennsylvania Railroad
- Started investing, building wealth
- 1889: Released a book titled **"Gospel of Wealth"**
  - "The man who dies thus rich dies disgraced"
  - First person of great wealth to argue publicly that the wealthy have a moral obligation to give back

### Potential Clues
- Carnegie's journey from poverty to philanthropy
- Gospel of Wealth philosophy
