## Right Page (162) - Carnegie Biography

### Carnegie's Life Story
- **Born: Dunfermline, Scotland**
- Family was poor - entire family slept in corner of house
- **Immigrated to United States: 1848**
- Mother decided to move to **Pittsburgh**
- **Age at immigration: 13**
- **First job: Bobbin boy in cotton factory**
  - 16 hours/day, 6 days/week
  - Pennies for pay
  - "Illegal child laborer" by today's standards
- Got job as messenger boy at Western Union
- Impressed Thomas A. Scott (later VP of Pennsylvania Railroad)
- Scott became Carnegie's first mentor
- Promoted to **superintendent of western division** at Pennsylvania Railroad
- Started investing, building wealth
- 1889: Wrote **"Gospel of Wealth"**
  - "The man who dies thus rich dies disgraced"
  - First American to argue wealthy have moral obligation to give back

### Potential Clues
- Carnegie's journey from poverty to philanthropy
- Gospel of Wealth philosophy

### Geographic References
- **Dunfermline, Scotland** (birthplace)
- **Pittsburgh** (first American home)
- **Pennsylvania** (railroad career)
