## Page 10 (Left) - Treasure Structure

### Critical Information - The Five Boxes

**Four Smaller Boxes:**
- Similar size to each other
- Value: "comfortably in the six figures" each
- Each has ONE dedicated chapter in Part Two
- Clues contained within those specific chapters

**Fifth Box (Lion's Share):**
- "Substantially larger" than the other four
- Contains "the lion's share of our valuable trove"
- NO single chapter dedicated to it
- Clues scattered throughout ENTIRE book
- "Almost every chapter in Part One offers at least one important detail"
- Helpful information may even be in the Introduction
