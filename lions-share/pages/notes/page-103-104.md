## Left Page (103) - La Luz Cargo Details

### Content
- Captain Fonseca smiling thinking about his ship
- Ship had been sailing 4 years from Portugal

### Cargo Details (IMPORTANT)
- **Official cargo:** 12,500 freshly minted gold coins for King Ferdinand VII of Spain
- **Contract:** 50/50 split with the King
- **Hidden cargo (secret):**
  - 200,000 pesos worth of gold coins
  - 40 large gold bars and disks
  - Hidden in barrels in the powder hold
  - King didn't know about this

### Pedro De Lea
- Fonseca's friend and "good man"
- Company's official representative in Buenos Aires
- Coordinated arrival of gold coins from Lima and Santiago
- Concerned about ship being overloaded

### Ship Details
- 185 passengers
- 12 cannons (disguised as "warship")
- Cargo, animals, months of rations
- Ship was over capacity

### Geographic References
- **Portugal** (origin)
- **Spain** (destination)
- **Buenos Aires** (3 years ported)
- **Montevideo** (current port)
- **Lima** (gold source)
- **Santiago** (gold source - machine minted escudos)

## Right Page (104) - The Pampero Storm

### Content
- Pedro De Lea bursts in warning: "Pampero! Pampero!"
- **Pampero:** Violent wind storm on Rio de la Plata
- Summer pamperos can have winds up to **180 km/h**
- 130+ people on board La Luz anchored 3 miles offshore

### The Disaster
- Fonseca couldn't reach his ship in time
- Crew decided to sail into Rio de la Plata rather than stay anchored
- Waves were 12 feet high
- Fonseca had to turn back to shore
- Watched through telescope as La Luz drifted away into storm

### Key Phrase
- **"His Lady of the Light was drifting away."**
