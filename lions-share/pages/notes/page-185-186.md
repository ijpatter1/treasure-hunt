### Hercules Story
- Infant Hercules **strangled two snakes** sent by Hera
- Hera inflicted madness - Hercules killed own wife and children
- Had to complete **twelve mission impossibles** to atone
- First task: Kill Hera's **Nemean lion**
- **Carved club from olive tree**, pummeled lion to death
- **Club of Hercules** = central brand of Herculean mythology
- Wearing club = powers of protection, especially for children

### Amethyst Origin Myth
- Dionysus got drunk, set lions to kill next stranger
- Stranger was lovely maiden
- Dionysus turned her into quartz crystal to save her
- His tears of remorse turned crystal into **purple amethyst**
- Greeks believed amethyst could keep you sober
- Also: peace of mind in business, courage, emotional stability, sleep
