## Left Page (203) - Costa Rica Jade Necklace

### TREASURE ITEM: Jade and Gold Amulet Necklace
- **200 to 600 AD**
- Origin: **Costa Rica**
- Interpretive recreation of original appearance
- Jade carvings and gold beads = **all original**
- Small emerald beads and gold clasp = added later

### Nicoya Civilization
- Spanish explorers arrived **16th century**
- Found culture with diverse cities, governments, agriculture, arts
- Jade held important symbolism:
  - **Believed to protect wearer**
  - **Mediate between wearer and spiritual world**

### Geographic References
- **Costa Rica** (Nicoya civilization)
