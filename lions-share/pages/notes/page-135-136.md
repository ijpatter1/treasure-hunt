## Left Page (135) - Wilma Rudolph's Early Life

### Wilma Rudolph Biography
- **Born 1940 in Clarksville, Tennessee**
- Mother: Blanche Rudolph
- **20th of 22 children**
- Born 2 months premature (4.5 lbs)
- Weakened immune system - pneumonia, measles, mumps, whooping cough, scarlet fever

### Polio & Paralysis
- Age 5: Developed paralysis in left leg from polio
- Fitted with steel leg brace
- Doctors said she might never walk again
- Rehabilitation at **Meharry Medical School in Nashville** (50 miles away)
  - Twice weekly bus rides
  - Traction, massage, pool therapy (4 hours each session)

### Family Doctor
- **Dr. Coleman** - lone Black doctor in Clarksville
- Made house calls to poor families

### Potential Clues
- **Clarksville, Tennessee** - birthplace
- **Nashville** - rehabilitation location
- 22 children in family - large family

### Geographic References
- **Clarksville, Tennessee**
- **Nashville, Tennessee**
- **Meharry Medical School**

## Right Page (136) - Recovery & Basketball

### Recovery Milestone
- **Age 9:** Removed brace and walked into church without it
- **Age 12:** Brace removed permanently - full recovery from polio

### Basketball
- Started playing in 7th grade
- **Coach Clinton Grey**
- Sat on bench for 3 years
- Became starting guard by 10th grade (almost 6 feet tall)

### Track Discovery
- Coach Grey formed informal track team
- Wilma won every race against classmates and local schools

### First Competition Loss
- **Tuskegee, Alabama** - large track event
- First time against trained competition
- Lost every race - was devastated
- Response: "heightened sense of determination"

### Track Coach
- **Ed Temple** - coached at Tennessee State College
- Also refereed basketball games
- Scouted Wilma for his AAU developmental program

### Potential Clues
- Tennessee State College connection
- Tuskegee, Alabama competition

### Geographic References
- **Clarksville, Tennessee**
- **Tuskegee, Alabama**
- **Tennessee State College**
