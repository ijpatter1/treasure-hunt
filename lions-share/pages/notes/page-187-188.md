## Chapter 21 Summary

### Key Takeaways
1. **Four Treasure Items from Alexander Era:**
   - Greek gold wreath (olive/oak leaves, acorns) - 3rd-4th c. BC
   - Pair of gold ibex-headed bracelets - 3rd-4th c. BC
   - Egyptian gold snake bracelet - 1st BC-1st AD (Cleopatra era)
   - Amethyst/gold necklace with Club of Hercules - 2nd-3rd c. BC
2. **TREASURE HUNTING HINT:**
   - "Make It Make Sense" chapter title is reference to finding treasure
   - "Look beyond just ancient myths and legends"
3. **Alexander the Great:**
   - Ruled 336 BC - age 32 (13 years)
   - Conquered 3000 miles from Macedonia to India
   - 20+ cities named Alexandria
4. **Symbolism:**
   - Oak/olive = Zeus, wisdom
   - Goat = Dionysus, fertility
   - Snake = healing (Asklepios, AMA symbol)
   - Club of Hercules = protection
   - Amethyst = sobriety, peace
