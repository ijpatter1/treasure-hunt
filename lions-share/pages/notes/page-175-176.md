### Tsavorite Garnet Discovery
- Discoverer: **Campbell Bridges**
- Location: **Tanzania** (countryside)
- Story: Buffalo charged at him, jumped into ditch
- Noticed "glimmer of green amongst an outcrop of rock"
- Went home, later transferred to different mining region
- **Searched 7 more years** before official discovery
- **"This area of Tanzania is still the only area where tsavorite has ever been found"**
