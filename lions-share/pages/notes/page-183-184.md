### Ibex/Goat Symbolism
- Gold = symbol of wealth and prestige
- Ibex (goat) = "foreteller of seduction and potency"
- Associated with **Dionysus** (god of fertility, wine, pleasure)

### Dionysus Mythology
- "Dionysus liked to party"
- Had superpower: could eviscerate taboos
- No rules, customs, or laws with Dionysus
- Followers: **Maenads women** (possessed super strength)
- Often turned himself into a goat
- Greeks had Dionysus-styled parties, goat sacrifices
- Cult of fertility befriended cult of Aphrodite
