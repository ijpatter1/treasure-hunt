### The Overview Effect
- Phenomenon experienced by astronauts viewing Earth from space
- **Effects reported:**
  - Heightened sense of interconnectedness with all humanity
  - Increased appreciation for sheer beauty of planet
  - Unexpected wellspring of deep emotions, including love
  - Some reported spiritual awakenings
  - Some felt deep sadness at visible destruction on Earth
- "Universal" effect across all countries, backgrounds, cultures
