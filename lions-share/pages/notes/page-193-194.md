## Left Page (193) - Picasso & Jacqueline Together

### Persistence Pays Off
- **"Over the course of half a year"** = 6 months of daily roses
- Each time Jacqueline said no
- Then one day she accepted dinner invitation
- **"They would spend the rest of his life together"**

### Their Relationship
- Last two decades of Picasso's life = inseparable
- She was: wife, lover, muse, manager, accountant, caretaker, agent, best friend
- "Truly a perfect match"
- She refused to pose like former mistresses
- Became his "most influential and powerful inspiration"
- **Picasso painted Jacqueline in over 400 of his works**

### After Picasso's Death
- April 1973: Jacqueline by his side when he passed
- Oversaw Picasso's estate for over a decade
- Lost her sparkle after his death
- **Joined him in death 13 years later** ("mostly in solitude")

### Pendant Provenance
- Designed by **Picasso in 1956**
- Produced by **François Hugo of Hugo Ateliers in France**
- **François = great-grandson of Victor Hugo** (Hunchback of Notre Dame, Les Misérables)
- **François's grandson Nicolas** still runs family workshop today
- **Author acquired pendant directly from Nicolas**

### Pendant Details
- **24 different Picasso pendant designs** produced
- Each design very personal to Picasso
- "Jacqueline at an easel" = design most dear to Picasso's heart
- Not offered for sale until after Picasso's passing

### Treasure Hunting Advice
- **"Picasso was persistent and brave"**
- **"Taking the first step out your door might be the bravest action required of you here"**

### Potential Clues
- Hugo Ateliers in France = source of pendant
- Victor Hugo connection
- Bravery and persistence as traits for treasure hunting

### Geographic References
- **France** (Hugo Ateliers)
