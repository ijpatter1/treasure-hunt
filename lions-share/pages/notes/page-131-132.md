## Left Page (131) - Thoreau's Activism & Author's Fear of Failure

### Thoreau's Beliefs/Activism
- Believed every human was divine and fundamentally equal
- Protected children from forced labor
- Argued Native Americans deserved equal place in society
- Believed women were equals to men
- Active in anti-slave movement and **underground railroad**

### Harper's Ferry Event
- **October 16, 1859** - John Brown's raid at Harper's Ferry, Virginia
- Attack to establish safe place for freed slaves in Virginia and Maryland
- Thoreau gave speech: **"A Plea for Captain John Brown"**
- Thoreau was lone nationally-recognized voice to defend Brown
- John Brown hanged the following day
- Event "ushered in the American Civil War"

### Author's Fear of Failure (Personal Details)
- "I feared failing for many years of my life"
- As child, desperately wanted parents' approval
- Studied hard for good grades
- Conflated grades with parents' love
- Feared rejection = failure in relationships
- Purposely avoided situations where rejection could occur

### Potential Clues
- Harper's Ferry, Virginia geographic reference
- Underground railroad connection

### Geographic References
- **Harper's Ferry, Virginia**
- **Maryland**
