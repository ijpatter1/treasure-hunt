## Right Page (81) - Stone Inlay Technique & Hotevilla

### Content
- Olgivanna Wright suggested putting stone in mistake hole
- This became Charles' **second signature creation** - stone inlay technique

### **HOTEVILLA:**
- **"Despite all of his commercial success, Charles Loloma continued to live in Hotevilla"**
- **"His home was one of many tightly clustered homes on the Hopi reservation: homes with no running water, phones, or electricity"**
- Charles tended fields, played music, entertained children as clown

### Charles Loloma Quote:
- "I'd like to be known for beauty"
- **"Appropriately, the name Loloma means beauty"**

### Debbie Drye (Hopi Tribe) Quote:
- "His house sits right on the edge of Hopi, so when you look out there you see the vastness...Charles was that way, he was just so vast"

### **SIDEBAR - TWO LOLOMA RINGS:**
- **Two Charles Loloma STONE rings in treasure** (not "silver" rings)
- Blue ring (shown page 76): free-form turquoise stone; rim inlayed with fossilized ivory, ironwood, and coral
- Purple ring (shown above, page 81): sugilite stone with lapis lazuli, coral, and turquoise

### Potential Clues
- **Hotevilla** - village on Hopi reservation
- "Edge of Hopi" - vantage point description
- Loloma = "beauty" in Hopi
