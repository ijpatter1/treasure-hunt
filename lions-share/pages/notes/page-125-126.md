## Right Page (126) - Louis Comfort Tiffany Biography

### Louis Comfort Tiffany
- Son of **Charles Tiffany** (founder of Tiffany & Co.)
- Masterful painter - 27 exhibitions before age 21
- Interior/exterior designer
- **1882: President Chester Arthur commissioned Louis to decorate White House**
- Created The Tiffany Glass and Design Co.
- 8 years of experimentation with team of scientists
- **1893: Unveiled "Favrile" glass** - new colored glass technique
- Tiffany stained glass in church chapels, government buildings, White House

### Enamel Pieces
- First exhibited 1900 in Paris
- **Only 750 enamel pieces ever made**
- Louis kept 90 in private collection
- Never offered to public (except special exhibits)
- Our iris vase: hot glass dripped over copper base
- Technique is inexact - shapes are "impressionistic"
- Most of Louis' enamel pieces marked **SG** (Stourbridge Glass, his enameling division) or **EL** - the meaning of EL sentence is cut off at the page break (continues on next spread)

### Potential Clues
- White House connection
- Favrile glass technique
- Rarity of enamel pieces

### Geographic References
- Paris (1900 exhibition)
- White House
