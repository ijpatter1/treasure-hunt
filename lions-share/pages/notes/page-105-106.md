## Right Page (106) - Shipwreck History & Treasure Items

### Content
- **July 2, 1752:** La Luz sank near Montevideo
- Note: Text says "Brazil" but this appears to be an error - Montevideo is in Uruguay
- Wreckage strewn over **50 miles**
- **No survivors**
- Bodies found with gold coins in pockets
- Captain Fonseca recovered **over 90%** of cargo in less than a year
- Hidden cargo was never recovered (wasn't supposed to exist)

### Rio de la Plata Facts
- **"One of the deadliest shipwreck graveyards in the world"**
- **More than 1,000 ships** have disappeared in its depths

### 1993 Rediscovery
- Discoverer: **Señor Ruben Corella**
- Was searching for another shipwreck (El Preciado)
- Found La Luz wreckage **50 miles from bay of Montevideo**
- Recovered: dozens of gold ingots, thousands of gold coins

### THREE Treasure Items from La Luz
1. **Gold bar** (p.105) - Most beautiful of recovered bars
2. **Gold disk** (p.107) - Purity .975 (23.4 carats), 22+ troy oz, purest from La Luz
3. **Eight escudo coin** (p.108) - 1750, Lima mint, MS 63 rating

### Commemorative Sets
- 245 limited edition boxed sets created
- Included: description, 8-escudo coin, gilded silver replica of gold bar
- Currently offered online for ~$10,000
