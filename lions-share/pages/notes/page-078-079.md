## Left Page (78) - Hopi History & Charles Loloma Birth

### Content
- Traditions discussion - Thanksgiving, Christmas, Halloween, Ramadan, Yom Kippur, La Tomatina
- **American Indian Removal Act of 1830** - ignored Native American cultures
- **Oldest North American tribe is the Hopi** - migrated from Mexico ~500 BC
- Hopi customs extend back thousands of years
- **Hopi village of Oraibi = oldest occupied Native American settlement**
- Today: Hopi have only 9% of original land
- Hopi means "peaceful" and "polite"
- Rich ceremonies, some sacred and kept secret from outside world

### **Charles Loloma:**
- **Born 1921 into Badger clan**
- Lived on Hopi reservation his entire life
- Followed Hopi calendar, farmed the land
- Became elder and leader in his clan
- Roles: musician, mime, clown, **Snake Priest**, **Bean Dance ceremony leader**

### Potential Clues
- **Oraibi** - oldest Native American settlement
- **Badger clan** - Loloma's clan
- **Bean Dance** - significant Hopi ceremony

### Geographic References
- Hopi reservation (Arizona)
- Mexico (Hopi origin)
- Oraibi village

## Right Page (79) - Loloma's Art Career & Treasure Island

### Content
- Charles was creative rebel, visionary, bucked norms
- **"Most prominent and famous Native American jewelry designer in the world"**
- **"Today, Charles Loloma's jewelry pieces are the rarest and most sought after Native American jewelry anywhere"**
- Started as painter - decorated high school with murals
- **Fred Kabotie** - Native American artist who discovered Loloma's talent

### **TREASURE ISLAND:**
- Fred invited Charles to San Francisco
- **"On Treasure Island in the San Francisco Bay, Charles was one of three Native Americans to paint installations in the Indian Court of the island's Federal Building"**
- **"Over half a million people came to Treasure Island to see them"**
- Later painted murals at **Museum of Modern Art in New York City**

### Gallup Inter-Tribal Ceremonial:
- Largest Native American art fair at the time
- **Rejected his jewelry** - "not Indian enough"
- White business owners controlled the fair

### Potential Clues
- **Treasure Island, San Francisco Bay** - location where Loloma painted
- "Treasure Island" name is notable given context
- MoMA, NYC connection

### Geographic References
- Treasure Island, San Francisco Bay
- Museum of Modern Art, New York City
- Gallup (Inter-Tribal Ceremonial location)

### Confidence Level
- Medium-High - "Treasure Island" reference is interesting but may be coincidental
