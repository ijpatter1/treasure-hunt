### Halftime
- Nigeria down 3-1 to Brazil (best team in world)

### Second Half

- Ronaldo's substitute turned ball over

### Final Minutes

- 4 minutes in: ball bounced off Nigerian player to Kanu
- **Kanu scored winning goal**
- Final: **Nigeria 4, Brazil 3**
