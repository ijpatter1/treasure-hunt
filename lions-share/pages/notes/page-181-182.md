## Right Page (182) - Wreath Symbolism

### Wreath History
- First worn at **inaugural Olympic Games 776 BC**
- Originally real olive branches for champions
- Later: oak, myrtle, laurel leaves

### Leaf Symbolism
- **Laurel**: Great achievement, purification (Apollo/Daphne myth = "Poet Laureate")
- **Olive**: Zeus, wisdom, peace ("extend olive branch")
- **Oak**: Zeus, wisdom, strength, endurance; priests received guidance from Zeus through rustling branches

### Gold Wreaths
- Fabricated from pure gold after Alexander's conquests
- Hammered into paper-thin sheets, cut into leaves
- Worn only on special occasions
- Awarded for achievements, mark of honor
- Thought to help with hangovers
- Gold = indestructible, immortal qualities
- Often worn to graves

### Potential Clues
- Olympic Games connection
- Wisdom symbolism (oak, olive)

### Geographic References
- **Greece** (Olympic Games, mythology)

### Confidence Level
- HIGH for treasure item and symbolism
