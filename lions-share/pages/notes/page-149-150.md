## Left Page (149) - Gold Medal Match vs Argentina

### Cultural Impact
- Song created: **"When Nigeria Beat Brazil"**
- Still sung by Nigerian fans 30 years later
- Nigeria won hearts of soccer fans at Olympics

### Gold Medal Match vs Argentina
- Stadium filled with Nigerian green and white colors
- Argentina scored in **3 minutes** (1-0)
- Nigeria equalized in **24th minute** (1-1)
- Argentina scored again (2-1)
- **74th minute:** Daniel Amokachi heel flick goal (2-2)

### Winning Goal
- Less than 1 minute remaining
- Argentina committed foul, Nigeria awarded free kick
- Argentina tried offsides trap but moved too late
- **Emmanuel Amunike** alone in front of goal
- Calmly redirected ball past goalkeeper
- **Final: Nigeria 3, Argentina 2**
- "Greatest underdog story in history of Olympic soccer"
