## Left Page (88) - Spanish Explorers & Chavin de Huantar

### Content
- Spanish explorers first encountered Chavin de Huantar in 16th century
- 45-foot walls with massive carved heads protruding
- **"Chavin de Huantar, believed to be built around 900 BC"**
- Explorers couldn't find entrance through massive walls

### Confirmation Bias Example
- One explorer wrote back to Spain that it was built by "a race of giants"
- Another insisted it was "an ancient fortress"
- **"Confirmation bias was in full effect, and their ideas were all wrong"**

### Truth About Chavin de Huantar
- Main temple of a religious cult
- Religious dominion (not soldiers/weapons) kept Chavin united 1000+ years
- **First highland civilization in Peru**
- Chavin excelled in: monument design, metallurgy, sculpture, architecture, hydraulics

### Key Phrase
- **"As if it is a gigantic puzzle box, Chavin de Huantar has no discernible entrance"**
- PUZZLE BOX reference - connects to Chapter 3 puzzle box treasure item?

### Potential Clues
- **"Puzzle box"** metaphor - repeated treasure motif
- Hidden entrances theme
- 10,000+ feet elevation reference

### Geographic References
- Andes Mountains, Peru
- Chavin de Huantar
