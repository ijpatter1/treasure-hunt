## Right Page (112) - Gold Rush Spread

### Content
- Word spread from San Francisco, became ghost town as people headed east
- December 1848: President Polk's State of Union declared "abundance of gold"
- 1849: ~90,000 young men came to California
- 1850: Another 90,000
- 1852: Over 1% of entire US population had relocated to California
- Word reached Australia, China, Central/South America
- Ships abandoned in San Francisco harbor
- US Navy stopped deploying to San Francisco

### Mining Evolution
- First: Gold pans for small nuggets
- Then: River dams to expose sandy bed
- Then: Hydraulic mines
- Then: Large quartz mining operations
- By 1852: Most gold found by large private groups, not individuals

### Potential Clues
- "First mover advantage" theme
- Individual effort vs. group effort

### Geographic References
- **San Francisco** (harbor, port)
- **California** (desert regions)
