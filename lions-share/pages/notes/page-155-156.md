## Left Page (155) - Delaware Crossing

### Washington's Military Background
- Never formally trained as soldier
- "Playing the part" of stoic alpha soldier
- Army was "bunch of ragtag mercenaries, sons of farmers"
- Lost first battles badly against British (1776 New York)
- Troops hungry, retreating, freezing cold

### Crossing the Delaware
- Fled southward across Delaware River
- German mercenaries camped on opposite side
- Christmas night: Wild plan to cross back
- Divided troops into 3 groups
- **Two groups couldn't cross - water froze to ice**
- Only Washington's boat made it
- Germans dismissed spy warnings as "sheer madness"
- Victory against unprepared Germans
- "First of many improbable triumphs"

### Potential Clues
- Improbable victories theme
- Bold action despite odds

### Geographic References
- **New York** (first major attack 1776)
- **Delaware River**

## Right Page (156) - TREASURE ITEM DETAILS

### Washington's War Legend
- Never hit by bullets despite being in line of fire for 8 years
- "Invincibility took on mythological proportions"
- Battle of Monmouth (June 1778) - rallied retreating troops

### First President
- Moved to Philadelphia for presidency
- Only consensus candidate for first President

### **TREASURE ITEM - JELLY GLASS:**
- **George Washington's crystal dessert/jelly glass**
- **Used during Washington's presidency** for dinners, functions, entertaining
- After presidency: Brought to **Mount Vernon, Virginia**
- **Washington family GREEN WAX SEAL on bottom**
- **One of only TWO known to exist**
- Displayed in **more than 10 museums** including:
  - Gerald Ford Presidential Library
  - Herbert Hoover Presidential Library
- **Sister glass at Mount Vernon Museum**

### Recommended Reading
