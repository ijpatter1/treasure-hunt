### Historical Context

- Life was "simpler" - rotary phones, milkmen, daily newspapers

- Movie "Jaws" by Steven Spielberg was popular

## Page 21 (Right) - Apple's Founding

### Key Figures
| Person | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Steve Jobs | Younger Steve, tall, slender, confident, artist |
| Steve Wozniak ("Woz") | Older Steve, shorter, quiet, practical, engineer |
| Bill Fernandez | Introduced the two Steves |
| Mike Markkula | First investor, worked at Intel and Fairchild |

### Apple Origin Story
- Two Steves met through Bill Fernandez
- First business: "Blue box" phone device - sold 200 units
- **1975**: Homebrew Computer Club formed in **Menlo Park, California**
- Wozniak designed Apple I - Hewlett Packard rejected it 5 times
- Jobs named company "Apple" after returning from **fruit orchard in Oregon**
- First retail order: 50 computers

### Key Quote
> "Just as this book can guide you towards the treasures I have hidden, Wozniak and Jobs needed direction."
