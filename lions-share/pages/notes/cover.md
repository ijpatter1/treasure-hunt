# Cover Notes

## Observations

### Back Cover Poem
The back cover contains a short poem in italic:

> *Be solid, have grit;*
> *sparkle even as you pine.*
> *Here lies a joy divined*

**Analysis:**
- "Be solid, have grit" - could refer to rocks/minerals, or perseverance
- "sparkle even as you pine" - "pine" could be a tree reference OR "pine" as in longing
- "Here lies a joy divined" - "divined" suggests discovery/revelation; "here lies" often used for graves/burial
- The poem has a riddle-like quality

### Front Cover
- Title: "THERE'S TREASURE INSIDE"
- Author: JON COLLINS-BLACK (in gold)
- Logo: Red circle with crossed pickaxe and shovel

### Logo Significance
- Mining/excavation tools (pickaxe + shovel)
- Red color stands out against black background
- Tools suggest digging, but postscript says NO DIGGING required
- Circle has hand-drawn/rough edge style (not clean geometric) - suggests adventure/authenticity

### Design Elements
- Two parallel gold/tan horizontal decorative lines between title and author name
- Could represent: equals sign, path, boundary, or simply decorative
- Gold color matches author name - creates visual connection
