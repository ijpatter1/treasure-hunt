## Left Page (129) - Thoreau Fire Aftermath & Journal Pages

### Thoreau Fire Aftermath
- **Over 300 acres burned**, thousands of trees perished
- Required "entire citizenry of Concord" to put out fire
- Thoreau became "accidental villain and local public enemy number one"

### Thoreau's Journal Response
- Struggled with accountability
- Wrote: "I have set fire to the forest, but I have done no wrong therein"
- Still writing about fire 6 years later
- Fire may have led to Walden Pond retreat (began 1 year after fire)

### Thoreau's Advocacy After Fire
- Advocated for volunteer fire department in Concord
- Drum alarm system and firefighting tools
