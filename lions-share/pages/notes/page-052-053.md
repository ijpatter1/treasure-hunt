## Right Page (53) - Chivor Mine Discovery

### Content
- **Pedro de Alvarado** - conquistador claimed tribe had emerald carved into parrot shape
- **1537**: Emerald mine discovered in **Andes Mountains** - named **Chivor**
- Spanish mined Chivor for over a century
- **1675**: Pope pressured Spain to end holy war, mining halted
- Chivor abandoned, **Andes Forest grew over it**
- Comparison: "not unlike the jungle surrounding the Angkor temples of the Khmer"
- **Chivor lay hidden for 200 years**
- **1880s**: **Don Francisco Restrepo** set out to find lost mine
- No maps, no GPS - only one clue
- **KEY CLUE**: Mine was near vista where **"Plains of the Llanos could be seen below"** the Andes
- Restrepo had to locate this specific vantage point
- **"He had to blaze his own path"**
- Team searched for weeks, men became exhausted, some wanted to give up
- Each morning they decided to follow their clue
