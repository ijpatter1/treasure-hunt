# Chapter 1: Pages 22-23 (The 120 Carat Sapphire)

## Page 22 (Left) - Mike Scott & Apple's Growth

### Mike Scott ("Scotty")
- Came from **National Semiconductor** (largest semiconductor maker)
- Became Apple's first CEO
- Employee badge #007
- Known for pragmatic leadership
- Captained Apple **1977-1981**

### Apple's Four Founders
| Name | Nickname |
|------|----------|
| Mike Scott | Scotty |
| Mike Markkula | Mike |
| Steve Wozniak | Woz |
| Steve Jobs | Jobs |

### Apple Growth Under Scott
- Company doubled in size every 3 months
- Apple II and Apple III released
- First 5 1/2" floppy disk
- Apple II sold over 6 million units
- Third largest home computer manufacturer

## Page 23 (Right) - Apple IPO & Mike Scott's Gems

### Apple IPO (1980)
- First day trading: **$1.7 billion**
- **40 millionaires** created that day
- 260 more millionaires created shortly after
- Today: Apple valued at **$2.5 trillion**

### Mike Scott Post-Apple
- Designed and built **rocket ships**
- Developed **Star Trek-like tricorder**
- Built one of world's most valuable **rare gem collections**

### Mike Scott's Gem Collection
- **2,600 different mineral specimens**
- Called "most amazing gem collection" by experts
- **Scottybite** - cobalt blue mineral named after him
- Collection displayed at **Bowers Museum near Los Angeles**

### The Treasure Sapphire
> "The sapphire pictured on the next page is the same as the one featured at the beginning of this chapter. Once owned by Mike Scott, it now is in our treasure."

- 120-carat raw sapphire crystal
- **Fewer than a dozen exist** in the world
- Found in streams, edges normally weathered by water

### Sidebar
> "Not to be confused with Scotty from Star Trek"
