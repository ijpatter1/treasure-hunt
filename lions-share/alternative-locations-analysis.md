# Alternative Location Analysis: Steelmanning Against Rocky Face Mountain

## Executive Summary

This document presents a critical analysis of evidence that does NOT support Rocky Face Mountain as the Lion's Share treasure location. The goal is to steelman alternative hypotheses, particularly **South Mountains State Park**.

**Key Finding:** While the main treasure book overwhelmingly points to Rocky Face Mountain, the children's book "Our Unbreakable Thread" contains water/lake imagery that Rocky Face cannot accommodate. This creates a paradox worth examining.

## Evidence AGAINST Rocky Face Mountain

### 1. Stone Arched Bridge (Pages 25-26)

The children's book lake scene (pp. 25-26) shows a **stone arched bridge** in the background.

- This is a specific, identifiable architectural feature
- Rocky Face Mountain area has no documented stone arch bridges near water
- Such bridges exist at various NC state parks with developed lake facilities

**Implication:** The illustrator depicted a real location that doesn't match Rocky Face.

### 2. Yellow Rental Rowboats (Pages 2, 25-26)

Multiple pages show **yellow rental rowboats** on a lake:
- Page 2: Painting of yellow boats in nursery (meaningful to family)
- Pages 25-26: Actual yellow boats being rowed
- Page 29: Final family scene at same type of lake

**Implication:** This depicts a park with organized boat rental facilities. Rocky Face has no such facility.

### 3. Deciduous Trees in Lake Scenes (Not Pines)

Critical observation from fresh analysis:

| Page | Trees Shown | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| 18 (fishing) | **PINE TREES** explicitly | Matches "sparkle even as you pine" |
| 25-26 (lake) | **DECIDUOUS trees** | "Dense tree line" - NOT pines |
| 29 (final) | **GREEN TREES** | Unspecified |

**Implication:** The fishing scene (p.18) matches Rocky Face's pine landscape. But the main recreation lake scenes (pp.25-26) show deciduous trees - a different type of setting.

### 4. Park Infrastructure

Pages 25-26 show:
- Walking paths
- Picnic areas with families
- People with cameras/backpacks (tourists)
- Developed recreation facilities

**Implication:** This describes a **developed state park** with recreation infrastructure, not a climbing/hiking destination like Rocky Face.

### 5. No Geological Features Shown

Rocky Face Mountain is defined by its dramatic **granite gneiss dome** - the "rocky face" that gives it its name.

Yet in the children's book water scenes:
- No rock outcrops visible
- No dramatic geological features
- Just: water, trees, boats, families

**Implication:** If this depicted Rocky Face, wouldn't the distinctive rock formations appear?

### 6. The "Magic in the Water" Reinterpretation

**Joy's Serenade (Stanza 4):**
> "There may be magic in the water, but the pike are rather all around."

**Current interpretation:** Misdirection - don't focus on water

**Alternative interpretation:**
- "Magic in the water" = treasure IS near water
- "Pike" = fish (not mountains)
- Trout fishing at South Mountains = "pike all around"
- The children's book's 5 water scenes SUPPORT this reading

## Resolution Options

### Option 1: Rocky Face is Correct (Water = Misdirection)

**Argument:**
- "Magic in the water" explicitly warns against focusing on water
- Children's book water is narrative backdrop (father-son bonding), not treasure clue
- The pine tree fishing scene (p.18) is the actual clue
- Main book text evidence is overwhelmingly explicit

**Strength:** Consistent with main book clues
**Weakness:** Dismisses 17% of children's book imagery

### Option 2: Two Locations Theory

**Argument:**
- Main treasure at Rocky Face (all text clues point here)
- Children's book "extra clue" points to South Mountains
- JCB said "an extra clue or two" - could reference different location

**Strength:** Accounts for both text and visual evidence
**Weakness:** No precedent for multi-location treasure in this hunt

### Option 3: South Mountains is Correct

**Argument:**
- Water imagery is the hidden "extra clue"
- Main book Hiddenite/Alexander references are misdirection
- Lake with boats is the actual search area

**Strength:** Explains water imagery
**Weakness:** Requires ignoring explicit text evidence (Hiddenite, Alexander, 318)
