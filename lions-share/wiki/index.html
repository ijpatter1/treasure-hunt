The Author's Confirmed Rules

  • Public land — on accessible public land, not private property.
  • Not buried — no digging required.
  • Within ~3 miles of a road — reasonably accessible.
  • Not dangerous — no water crossings, cliff scaling, or hazardous terrain.
  • Boots on the ground required — a physical search is needed after solving.
  • One box per US state — the five boxes are spread across different states.
  • Clues are literal — the author says there is no grand cipher or code; read plainly.
