## Recommendations for Physical Search

### Primary Target
**Hollow Rock Trail, near marker 318**

### Search Protocol
1. **Park** at main facility (3451 Rocky Face Church Road)
2. **Navigate** to Hollow Rock Trail (red blazes)
3. **Follow** markers sequentially toward 318
4. **Note** junction where Grindstone Trail crosses (~markers 305-307)
5. **At marker 318:**
   - Identify trail curve ("the haiku curls")
   - Locate granite outcrops
   - Look for shady oaks nearby
6. **Critical:** Leave trail and descend ("go down")
7. **Search** rock features below trail level

### What to Look For
- Natural crevices or hiding spots in rock
- Underhangs or sheltered areas
- Base of granite outcrops
- Areas with mica "sparkle" in sunlight
- Butterfly activity (Giant Swallowtails)

### Optimal Timing
| Factor | Recommendation |
