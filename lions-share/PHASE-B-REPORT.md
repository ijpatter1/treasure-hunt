## Honest treatment of the Hiddenite / Alexander anchor

The Ch4 emerald anchor is the most evocative (childhood emerald mine; doubly name-stamped by the Ch21
"Antiquities of Alexander" echo and the unverified 1875 "Alexander County" meteorite). **It did NOT earn
a pinpoint.** Adversarial validation (verdict: **thin**) found:

- **Alexander County has essentially ONE genuinely accessible public parcel: Rocky Face Mountain Rec
  Area.** The hard filters (public land, within 3 mi of road) ARE satisfiable there. But it (a) merely
  re-points at a **prior FAILED search** (multiple BOTG trips with recorded GPX tracks — "Rocky Face NC
  FAILED" in project memory), adding nothing new; (b) is defined by granite **climbing CLIFFS**
  (Big Rock/Little Rock) that rub against the no-cliff/not-dangerous filter; and (c) is fit only by
  GENERIC discriminators shared by every western-NC foothill park.
- **Emerald Hollow Mine** (the literal Ch4 emerald site) is **PRIVATE, paid, fee-dig** — fails the
  public-land filter outright. It is the textbook lone-provenance trap.
- **The Brushies generally** are mostly PRIVATE (orchards, farms, gem mines); the emerging "Brushy
  Mountains State Natural Area" is PENDING transfer/management and not yet confirmed accessible.

**Conclusion on Hiddenite/Alexander:** treat it as a region-level SEED only, NOT a BOTG target.
Re-searching Rocky Face would repeat a known failure. No specific public-land spot in this anchor's zone
earned its way up on discriminators.
