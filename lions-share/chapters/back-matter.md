## Postscript: Safety Guidelines

The postscript is on **pages 207-208**.

**Ch 11 states:** "You can find these details in the postscript of this book. It's a must read."

**Literal-reading directive (p.207, opening of the postscript, verbatim):** "There is no subterfuge in what I am going to say to you now. No double meanings. No misdirection. No innuendos. There is no subtext. No clues. No code. You should take what I say here as literally as you possibly can."

Key confirmed guidelines:
- **Five treasure boxes** total (stated on p.207 and restated on p.208)
- Boxes placed in **safe locations** - "I have hidden none of our five treasure boxes in a dangerous place" (p.207)
- **No box is hidden under any body of water** - "You do not need to get into a raft, or canoe, or a water vessel of any kind to come to or find any of our treasure boxes" (p.207)
- **No box is precariously close to a swift current or a high or dangerous ledge** (p.207)
- **No cliff or rock face scaling** - "you will not need to scale a cliff or rock face to find a box" (p.207)
- **Not buried** - no digging required
- **Not on private property** - accessible public land
- **Within 3 miles of a road** - "no treasure hidden more than three miles from any road"
- **"Boots on the ground" required** - physical search needed
