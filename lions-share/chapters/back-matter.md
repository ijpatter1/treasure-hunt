### Back Cover Haiku Decoded

```
Be solid, have grit;           ← 5 syllables (Line 1)
sparkle even as you pine.      ← 7 syllables (Line 2)
Here lies a joy divined        ← 6 syllables (Line 3)
```

**Numerical derivation:**
- **3 lines** in haiku
- **18 total syllables** (5+7+6)
- Combined: **318** = Hollow Rock Trail Marker

**Geological derivation:**
- "solid, grit" = granite rock
- "sparkle" = mica in granite
- "pine" = shortleaf pine at Rocky Face
- "joy divined" = treasure discovered
