## Chapter Summary

The author's personal story about an unprepared sales presentation illustrates the theme. The chapter then introduces **Tuyet Nguyet**, a Vietnamese investigative journalist who founded *Arts of Asia* magazine in 1970 with husband **Stephen Markbreiter**. She curated 100 gold rings over 40 years.

The narrative covers Hindu/Buddhist gold symbolism, the Khmer Empire, and Angkor (once "the largest city in the world"—**the size of Los Angeles**). Key detail: Khmer rings "were discovered mostly in **hidden caches, secreted away within temple walls**."

Chapter concludes with explicit advice for treasure hunters and introduces **Seth Gould**, who forged all five treasure boxes over 18 months.
