## Key Information for Lion's Share Hunt

### The Five Boxes Structure
- **Four smaller boxes**: Each worth "comfortably in the six figures"; each has ONE dedicated CHAPTER (not a separate book) within this same book. Per p.10: "To help you locate these boxes, I wrote four chapters. Each of these four chapters is dedicated to one box... You will find these chapters in Part Two of this book."
- **Lion's Share (fifth box)**: "Substantially larger", contains majority of treasure value, NO single chapter - clues scattered throughout ENTIRE book

### Part One vs. Part Two (p.10, p.16)
- **Part Two = the four smaller-box chapters** - "the four chapters in Part Two contain primarily all you need to discover the location of the other four treasure boxes" (p.16)
- **Lion's Share clues live in Part One** - "almost every chapter in Part One of this book offers at least one important detail to help guide you to the location of the larger box" (p.10)
- Note: the printed Table of Contents (front-03-04) carries NO explicit "Part One"/"Part Two" section headers - chapters are listed 1-23 sequentially. The book's prose calls Part Two "the four chapters" dedicated to the smaller boxes.

### Critical Guidance from Author
1. **No cipher or code** - take text literally
2. **Clues across the chapters** - "almost every chapter of the first twenty-three includes at least some morsel of information helpful for finding the location of the largest treasure box" (p.16)
3. **Item histories contain clues** - research the treasure objects' origins
4. **Research captivating topics** - if something interests you, dig deeper
5. **Trust your intuition** - "jot down anything that makes your Spidey sense tingle"
6. **Boots on ground required** - physical search needed after solving

### Treasure Location Confirmed
- Somewhere in the United States (stated in the front matter, p.9-10)

Note: The following rules do NOT appear anywhere in the front matter (pages 1-17); they originate in the Postscript / author's later confirmations and are listed here only for reference:
- On public land (not private property) - Postscript / later rules, not front matter
- Within 3 miles of a road - Postscript / later rules, not front matter
- Not buried (no digging) - Postscript / later rules, not front matter
- Not dangerous to access - Postscript / later rules, not front matter
