## Chapter Summary

The author recalls his father comforting him at age 11 after losing a football playoff game. This speech about resilience inspired the author's only tattoo: a Sanskrit phrase on his left forearm meaning "I welcome the good with the bad, as they both come from the one true source."

Jackie Kennedy Onassis exemplified resilience through immense tragedy: JFK's assassination (November 22, 1963 in Dallas), RFK's assassination, a miscarriage, a stillborn birth, and the death of infant Patrick just three months before JFK died. Despite this, she reinvented herself multiple times - marrying Aristotle Onassis for privacy, then becoming the first former first lady to return to a traditional workplace at Viking Press and later Doubleday (16 years as editor). She created the White House Historical Association and fought to save Grand Central Station from demolition.

The author explicitly states this treasure hunt is designed for joy, not suffering, with no great hardship required.
