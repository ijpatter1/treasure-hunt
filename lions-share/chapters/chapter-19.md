## Chapter Summary

The author confesses to being "a bit of a nerd" - Star Trek: The Next Generation was his favorite show from ages 16-22, with Captain Jean-Luc Picard as his hero.

Growing up in the countryside, the author could see the Milky Way every night (something he misses living near a large city now). He received a small telescope for Christmas and would climb onto the roof of his parents' minivan to stargaze, dreaming of touching the moon. "Forty years later, I figured out a way to do just that."

The chapter discusses the Overview Effect - the emotional and perspective shift astronauts experience viewing Earth from space, including heightened interconnectedness, appreciation, and spiritual awakenings. The author applies this to treasure hunting: **new perspectives are key to finding treasure**.
