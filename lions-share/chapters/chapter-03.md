## Chapter Summary

Author's 2018 trip to Japan leads to discovery of Hakone puzzle boxes. Returning to Los Angeles, he later discovers **Seth Gould**'s work "Coffer" at the **Smithsonian American Art Museum**. Seth, a blacksmith in **Bakersville, North Carolina** (40 miles NE of Asheville), was commissioned to create all five treasure boxes.

The Perkhin magnifying glass represents the chapter's theme—a "Sherlock Holmesian symbol, reminding us of the importance of paying attention to the smallest things."

The Third Imperial Faberge Egg story (found in Midwest US antiques show after 87 years, valued at $33M) reinforces the theme: treasure can hide in plain sight for years until someone examines it closely.
