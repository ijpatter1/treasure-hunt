## Chapter Summary

**Art Smith** (Arthur George Smith) was born in Cuba to Jamaican parents. His mother **Mary Elizabeth Williams** was born April 19, 1879 in **Morgan's Pass, Jamaica**. On **July 21, 1920**, the family sailed from Cuba to **New York City**.

Art earned a full scholarship to **Cooper Union**, won first prize in the **New York World's Fair Corporation** design contest, but rejected the civil engineering path to pursue art. He taught at an art center in **Harlem**, met mentor **Winifred Mason** (who had a shop in **Greenwich Village**), and eventually opened his own shop.

His big break came through **Talley Beatty**, a choreographer who worked with **Duke Ellington**. Talley commissioned Art to create bold jewelry for dancers that was "striking from both close and far away."

Art Smith's signature style: **asymmetrical forms of copper, silver, and brass** influenced by **ancient Egypt, tribal Africa, and surrealism**.
