## Chapter Summary

Art Smith (Arthur George Smith) was born in Cuba to Jamaican parents, arriving in **New York City** in 1920. He earned a scholarship to **Cooper Union**, won a design contest for the **New York World's Fair**, but followed his passion for sculpture over engineering.

After teaching in **Harlem**, Art opened a jewelry shop in **Greenwich Village** (despite racism and vandalism). His connection with choreographer **Talley Beatty** (who worked with **Duke Ellington**) led to iconic jewelry for dance performances. His work influenced by ancient Egypt, tribal Africa, and surrealism.

**Key author advice:** Trust your own instincts, not online speculation. "Faith is akin to having an ethereal treasure map guiding each step of our way."
