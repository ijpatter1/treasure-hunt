# JCB's Intended Method, Collected — and the Next Pinpointed Approach

*Lion's Share treasure hunt. "There's Treasure Inside" by Jon Collins-Black (JCB).*
*This document does NOT pick a location. It reconstructs the author's own solving method from the book, then specs the next approach that obeys that method. Built from two independent syntheses + a full pass over the chapter lessons, framing matter, in-text asides, and the poem. All quotes are verbatim from the faithful page transcriptions; over-reading is flagged throughout.*

---

## TL;DR

**JCB's intended method (2-3 sentences).** Read the whole book (all 23 chapters *plus* the Introduction, How-To-Read, Postscript, and Joy's Serenade), capture every "Spidey-sense" signal into a flat ledger, then deepen the two externally-verifiable channels he explicitly sanctions — researched topic-tangents and the documented histories of the treasure objects. Read everything **literally** (he forbids cipher/code), screen every candidate through the **Postscript hard filters** (≤3 mi of a road, public land, not buried, no water/cliff, seasonally-snowy temperate, single-day-walkable), and keep only the place where **multiple independent signals converge** — the poem's "any arc may align at a proper point" is literal triangulation, the structural opposite of lone-association. The chapter lessons are not an ordered 1→23 decode; they are mostly mindset/guardrails wrapped around that procedure, and the loudest guardrail — **Confirmation Bias** — is the direct indictment of this project's failure history.

**Headline of the planned approach.** A **multi-candidate convergence engine**: build a per-chapter + per-item + framing-matter + poem ledger of *literal* geographic/observational signals, score *several* candidate regions (NC, CA, and others) in parallel against the Postscript exclusion filters, and elevate **only** the region where the largest number of *independent* signals geometrically converge and "make sense" as a whole — never the prettiest single lead.

**Postscript hard filters (literal-truth zone, p.207-208)** — apply as exclusion screens to *every* candidate:
- **≤ 3 miles from a road** (the single most powerful quantitative filter — draw a 3-mi road-buffer).
- **Public land only**; no private property; lone exception = a public trail briefly crossing eased private land.
- **Not buried / no digging** — surface concealment (niche, crevice, overhang, deadfall).
- **No water hazard** — not under/near water, no boat, no swift current.
- **No cliff / ledge / rock-face scaling.**
- **Seasonally-snowy temperate terrain** ("a blanket of snow" would hide it → upland that gets snow; search spring-fall; also avoid hot/humid no-shade timing).
- **Single-day on-foot searchable** by a small party.
- *Wildlife line (bison/moose/elk):* a **weak** westward hint stated as generic safety ("such as"). **Faint tiebreaker only, never primary evidence.**
- *Insider rule:* "I did not give anyone clues… do not ask them." → **biography/provenance is not a location directive.**

### 1C. Key in-text asides (mid-chapter directives)

- **Ch4 vista (pp.53-55):** Restrepo's "one solitary clue" — the Chivor mine sat near "a very particular outlook… a view gaped open between two jagged grey peaks." JCB explicitly parallels this to his hunt. **Use the PATTERN (a clued framing-outlook with the box nearby), not the Colombian props.**
- **Ch7 reveal (p.75):** "past the next tree line, over the next rise, or around the next corner" — search-phase terrain expectation; keep **generic** (don't over-specify a species/landform).

### 1D. The poem's self-guidance (Joy's Serenade, pp.205-206)

The poem is in-bounds back-matter. Its **only safe method-level reads**:
- "we may find music **in a poem**" — the clues live in the poem; mine its concrete nouns as constraints.
- **"Use will's straight edge, as the turning square or any arc may align at a proper point"** — the single most operational line: a **sight-line / triangulation** instruction. A straight edge (line), a turning square (right-angle / second bearing), an arc (radius/range) → **align at a proper point** = the answer is the *intersection of multiple independent geometric constraints*. **This is literally convergence, and the structural opposite of lone-association.** ("will's" stays **lowercase/ambiguous** — do NOT capitalize to "William.")
- "This is no imaginary wonderland. **Now look.**" — the place is **real, mappable, visually verified** on the ground.
- "if you don't go down you may never know how high" — **elevated** location, with a possibly **non-monotonic** access route (descend, then ascend).
- "sat upon a rock… maybe **ask them to assist you**" — a sittable rock/feature on site; enlist people/rangers present for the final gap.
- "**X** leads the way" / "shimmering circles of gold" / "magic in the **water**, but the **pike** are rather all around" / "**Dancers** are on the land" — hold each as a **separate, unresolved constraint**, tested independently, *not* fused into a forced story.

**Poem over-reading flags (contamination removed; do NOT re-adopt):** "pike" stays **triple-ambiguous** (fish / turnpike / peaked-hill); the line's own grammar ("magic in the **water**, but the pike") actually favors the **fish/water** sense, undercutting the old "peaked-hill" lock. "**Dancers**" stays an unresolved literal token — **not** Hopi butterflies. The poem is a set of *cross-checkable constraints*, **never a decoder.**

## Part 2 — Synthesis

### Ordered procedure, or guardrails?

**Both — at two different layers.** There **is** a genuinely ordered procedure, but it lives in the **framing matter**, not the subtitles:

> (1) read whole + re-read → (2) capture every signal into a ledger → (3) deepen the two verifiable channels (topic-tangents + item histories) → (4) read literally → (5) screen by the Postscript hard filters → (6) keep only the candidate where independent signals **converge** (tolerating noise) → (7) reduce to a day-walkable polygon → (8) boots-on-the-ground.

That sequence is real and executable. The **chapter lessons (1→23) are NOT an executable step list** — they *repeat out of order* (anti-CB at Ch8 *and* Ch9; perspective at Ch8/19/20; accessibility at Ch10/11/12; read-slow at Ch2/3), **Part Two (Ch15-18) is about the four smaller boxes**, and JCB calls them "attributes" to "apply." So the correct frame is: **a small ordered method-spine (framing matter) wrapped in a larger ring of behavioral guardrails (the subtitles), plus a handful of cross-cutting analytical priors** (convergence, perspective-shift, the unexpected answer, the vista pattern, literal field cues).

### How the guidance says the answer is structured and found

Not as a cipher and not concentrated in one clue. The location is encoded as a **convergence of many small, literal, plain-language signals** distributed across all 23 chapters + framing matter + item histories + the poem. By design ("almost every chapter… at least one morsel," "no one chapter solely devoted," "you won't need to understand every clue"), **the correct place is the one the largest number of independent signals point at — and a candidate backed by essentially one chapter/association is, by JCB's own construction, UNDER-supported.** The poem's "any arc may align at a proper point" restates this as triangulation. The answer resolves to a small, day-walkable polygon passing the Postscript filters; the final ~200 yards is closed by visual confirmation on site ("Now look").

### Traps JCB warned against — and where OUR prior process violated them

1. **CONFIRMATION BIAS** (named more than any other — Ch8 "most dreaded foe," Ch9 "no need to **force the clues to fit a location you want**"). **WE VIOLATED IT, by the project's own admission:** the NC-fixation forced clues onto the already-searched-**EMPTY** Rocky Face Mountain, then read the book to confirm it. This is the textbook behavior Ch9 forbids.
2. **CIPHER/CODE FABRICATION** (banned p.17, p.207). **WE VIOLATED IT:** capitalizing lowercase "will's"→"William," locking "pike"="peaked hill," decoding "Dancers"="Hopi butterflies" — precisely the "double meanings/code" JCB disclaims.
3. **LONE-ASSOCIATION / SINGLE-LEAD OVER-INDEXING** (counter-mandated by the distributed-clue + convergence design; Ch21 "consider more"; the poem's "align at a proper point"). **WE VIOLATED IT repeatedly:** Rocky Face, Roan, Bakersville, Red Ledge/Washington-CA, and the children's book were each built up on essentially one association and **collapsed for lack of independent convergence.** The children's book is a thin *theme-confirmation* layer, not a location-density source.
4. **BIOGRAPHY-AS-CLUE** (Postscript closes "he lives near X" reasoning). **WE VIOLATED IT:** treating Bakersville (box-maker), Hiddenite/Alexander Co. (childhood), and the CA residence as location evidence rather than provenance.
5. **RUSHING / SYSTEM-1** (Ch13 "running off with no sense of direction"; Ch12 Kahneman). **WE VIOLATED IT:** pins were drilled before convergence was established.
6. **FIXED VANTAGE / not shifting perspective** (Ch19/20). **PARTIAL violation:** the project stayed at one frame (NC ground-level) too long.
7. **FAILING TO FAIL FORWARD** (Ch14). **PARTIAL violation → now corrected:** prior passes replaced one collapsed pin with the next single pin; the current clean-up (removing the contamination, keeping NC as *one* candidate among CA/others) is the corrective application.

## Part 3 — THE PLAN (the next pinpointed approach)

### The reconciled derived method, in a phrase

**A multi-candidate, literal-signal convergence engine: ledger → filter → triangulate → discriminate by coherence → narrow to a polygon → boots-on-the-ground — never a single lead.**

### Concrete steps (each tagged to the guidance it is faithful to)

**STEP 0 — Build the method before any location.**
- *faithful to: Ch1 "A Plan That Changed the World"; Ch9 "let them guide you."*
- Freeze the procedure below as the rule every candidate must pass. No region is the default; none is excluded.

**STEP 1 — Build the full-corpus SIGNAL LEDGER (capture broadly, rank later).**
- *faithful to: How-To p.16 "Spidey sense… jot down anything"; "Do not overlook any part"; Intro p.10 framing matter in-bounds; Ch2 "Don't Wing It"; Ch3 "Joy Is in the Details."*
- One flat table. Columns: `signal_id | source (ch/page/item/poem/framing) | verbatim quote | literal plain-language reading | signal type {placename | descriptive-geography | flora/fauna | item-provenance | terrain/vista | elevation | access} | independence-group | candidate-region(s) it implies | strength`.
- Cover **all 23 chapters + Introduction + How-To + Postscript + Joy's Serenade + captions + acknowledgements.** Down-weight Ch15-18 asides (Part Two → smaller boxes) unless they address "the treasures"/all boxes (*faithful to: How-To p.16 Part-Two rule*).
- **Literal only.** Reject any signal that requires capitalization tricks, letter-extraction, anagrams, or forced etymology (*faithful to: p.17 / p.207 "no code… take it literally"*). Hold ambiguous poem tokens ("will," "X," "pike," "Dancers," "shimmering circles of gold") as **multi-sense** rows, never decoded.

**STEP 2 — Deepen the two verifiable channels.**
- *faithful to: How-To p.16 "research it further"; Intro p.13 "histories of treasure items… panning for gold."*
- (a) Research each *captivating topic/figure* to a concrete place/region. (b) Research each *treasure object's documented provenance* (origin region, owners, discovery site, manufacture). Tag each resulting place into the ledger as an **independent** signal (these are falsifiable anchors). Remember "**some of**" item histories carry place clues — do not force one out of every item.

**STEP 3 — Apply the Postscript HARD FILTERS as exclusion screens.**
- *faithful to: Ch11 "must read the postscript"; Postscript p.207-208; Ch10/Ch12 accessibility.*
- For every candidate region, require: within a **3-mi road buffer**; **public land**; not buried; no water hazard; no cliff/scaling; **seasonally-snowy temperate** upland; **day-walkable**. Drop anything that fails. (This is a *screen*, not a clue source — don't mine the Postscript for location hints, except the wildlife line as a **faint tiebreaker only**.)

**STEP 4 — TRIANGULATE: keep only where independent signals converge.**
- *faithful to: poem "any arc may align at a proper point"; How-To p.17 "won't need every clue"; Ch21 "consider more"; Ch4 breadcrumbs.*
- Treat each surviving signal as a constraint (a placename anchor, a descriptive-geography band, an item-provenance region, a flora/elevation/vista expectation). Score each candidate region by **count of *independent* signals that converge on it** (independence-group dedupes signals that are really the same association — e.g. all of "Bakersville + Hiddenite + CA-residence" collapse to **one** *biography* group, which then gets **down-weighted to non-evidence** per the insider rule). Elevate only regions with **multi-group convergence.**

**STEP 5 — DISCRIMINATE among finalists.**
- *faithful to: Ch21 "Make It Make Sense"; Ch16 "Defy Expectations"; Ch19/20 perspective.*
- Apply, in order: (a) **Coherence** — does the candidate "make sense" across *many independent* threads without special-pleading any clue? (b) **Triangulation tightness** — do the line/bearing/arc-style constraints actually intersect at a *proper point*, or is it a loose cloud? (c) **Defy Expectations** — between two equally-converged finalists, the *unexpected/improbable* one is favored over the obvious "prettiest." (d) **Perspective re-test** — re-read each top finalist's signals from a *changed vantage* (different map scale, aerial/topo, an inverted assumption) and confirm convergence survives the reframe.

**STEP 6 — Narrow to a day-walkable POLYGON, then boots-on-the-ground.**
- *faithful to: How-To p.17 "only bring you so close… boots on the ground"; ack. p.211 single-day; poem "Now look," "ask them to assist you."*
- For the top candidate, produce a polygon sized for a one-day on-foot search inside the 3-mi road buffer on public land. On site, expect: an **elevated framing-vista/outlook** (Ch4 pattern), the reveal **just past a tree line / rise / corner** (Ch7), surface concealment **behind/under a rock** (Ch6, "not buried"), **blue/pink wildflowers** nearby (Ch14), a **sittable rock** (poem); enlist people/rangers present.

### How it stays multi-candidate / anti-single-lead

- **Several candidate regions are scored in parallel** through Steps 3-5; no region is elevated until Step 4 convergence and Step 5 coherence rank it above the others. (*faithful to: Ch9, Ch21, the distributed-clue design.*)
- **Independence-grouping** is the mechanical guard: it prevents three facets of one association from masquerading as three signals — the exact error behind Rocky Face/Bakersville. A lead supported by one group is, by JCB's design, *under-supported*.
- **A pre-registered disconfirmation gate** (*faithful to: Ch9 "look for reasons it's wrong"; Ch14 "Fail Forward"*): before any candidate is promoted to boots-on-the-ground, write down the evidence that *would* refute it and check that it does not already exist (e.g. "Rocky Face was searched empty" is itself disconfirming data that must retire that pin).

### How NC stays a LIVE candidate without re-importing contamination

- NC's **genuine geology/flora/fauna fit** (temperate, seasonally-snowy Appalachian upland; blue/pink wildflowers; oak; accessible public land) makes it a **legitimate candidate region** entering Step 3 alongside CA and others.
- **What is barred from NC's score:** the removed contaminations — capitalizing "will's"→William, "pike"=peaked-hill, "Dancers"=Hopi-butterflies — and the **biography group** (Bakersville/Hiddenite/family), which collapses to non-evidence under the insider rule. NC must earn its rank on **independent literal signals + verifiable item-provenance**, the same as every other region. **No region is the default; none is excluded.** (*faithful to: Ch9; Postscript insider rule; p.207 literalism.*)

### Discrimination criteria (the decision rule)

A region is promoted only if it satisfies **all three**:
1. **Convergence** — the most *independent* signal-groups (Step 4), not the most signals.
2. **"Make It Make Sense" coherence** — internally coherent across many threads, no special-pleading (Ch21).
3. **Poem triangulation** — the line/bearing/arc constraints "align at a proper point" (the answer is an *intersection*, not a single match), and the place is **real/elevated/observable** ("Now look").
Ties broken by **Defy Expectations** (favor the improbable) and only then the faint **wildlife** tiebreaker.

### Spec for the workflow that EXECUTES this approach

A phased pipeline (each phase a discrete, resumable workflow stage):

- **Phase A — Ledger Build (capture).** Parse all 23 chapters + framing matter + poem + captions/acks into the signal ledger schema (Step 1). Output: `signal-ledger.json`. *Gate:* literalism check — reject any row needing a cipher move.
- **Phase B — Channel Research (deepen).** For each captivating topic and each treasure item, run sourced research to a concrete region; append as independent signals with provenance citations (Step 2). Output: ledger v2 with `item-provenance` and `topic-tangent` rows.
- **Phase C — Filter & Geocode (screen).** Geocode every candidate region; apply the Postscript hard filters (3-mi road buffer, public-land layer, no-water, no-cliff, seasonal-snow, day-walkable) (Step 3). Output: surviving candidate regions + a disqualification log.
- **Phase D — Convergence Engine (triangulate).** Independence-group the signals; score each surviving region by independent-group convergence; produce a ranked candidate set with the constraint geometry drawn out (Step 4). Output: `convergence-scores.json` + map overlays.
- **Phase E — Discrimination (decide).** Apply coherence + triangulation-tightness + Defy-Expectations + perspective-reframe to the finalists; run the disconfirmation gate (retire any already-refuted pin) (Step 5). Output: a single top candidate *with its refutation register*.
- **Phase F — Polygon & Field Plan (narrow).** Produce the day-walkable search polygon and the on-site feature checklist (vista, tree-line/rise/corner, behind-a-rock, blue/pink flora, sittable rock) (Step 6). Output: a boots-on-the-ground plan.
- **Cross-cutting:** every phase writes to its **own** report filename (never overwrite a shared report — per the project's logged feedback), and the convergence scores + refutation register are version-controlled so each "Fail Forward" iteration updates the model rather than restarting.

## Part 4 — Self-critique (applying JCB's own lessons to OUR process)

- **Confirmation Bias (Ch9).** The headline correction. This plan **never starts from a wanted location**; it derives candidates from the ledger, scores several in parallel, and gates every promotion behind a pre-registered disconfirmation test. The independence-grouping rule directly attacks the mechanism that built up Rocky Face (one association dressed as several). Honest risk: scoring is still partly judgment — "independence" can be gamed; the gate must be enforced literally, in writing, or CB sneaks back in.
- **Defy Expectations (Ch16).** We previously built up the *most narratively attractive* lead (the prettiest NC story). The plan makes "unexpected over obvious" an explicit tiebreaker — but I flag that this is the **softest** lesson (most plausibly motivational); it is used only to break ties between already-converged finalists, never to manufacture a contrarian pin (which would be CB inverted).
