**My single best place-bet — REFUTED as a specific pin by the 3-vote adversarial pass (the verifiers judged the NC-lake / dome / garden bets to be overfit storybook art; treat this as a region+typology to field-test, NOT a coordinate to trust), stated with low confidence:** a public fishing lake in the **NC Piedmont** that meets the "no ridge on the horizon" test — most defensibly **Lake Norman State Park, Troutman, Iredell County, NC** (fishing pier + shallow wading beach, ~35.6504, -80.9491), with **Lake Hickory access points** (Glenn C. A flat-horizon lake with a dock matches thousands of Eastern/Midwest lakes. And the author explicitly warns against the "JCB is from NC, therefore NC" reflex (see §4). **The three artifacts the hunter (correctly) singled out as highest-intent — the JCB-made Dancers art, the JCB-assembled necklace, and the Hopi calendar — were inspected directly and DO NOT yield a location or a cipher (see §3). ## 2. The visual case (lead with the strongest convergences + the 3 confirmed edits)

### 2a. The three CONFIRMED children's-book edits ("an extra clue or two… to assist in finding the largest box")
Author-confirmed he hid clues to the **largest** box in *Our Unbreakable Thread*; illustrator Nicole Gsell confirmed three deliberate post-submission edits ([MW Substack 7/4/25](https://mysteriouswritings.substack.com/p/weekly-treasure-recap-7425-featured); [marblepress.com/our-unbreakable-thread](https://marblepress.com/our-unbreakable-thread/)):

1. **Butterfly ADDED** (back cover / garden). Direct-inspection verdict: a **violet/purple, dark-edged, swallowtail-like** butterfly. Every iridescent purple "swallowtail-looking" butterfly in North America is an **EASTERN** species (Pipevine *Battus philenor*, Spicebush *Papilio troilus*, or Red-spotted Purple *Limenitis arthemis astyanax*) — all common in the **NC Piedmont**; **there is NO native purple swallowtail in the Los Angeles area** (LA swallowtails are yellow/black). **This is a directional signal: EAST, not West.** ([Battus philenor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battus_philenor); [Battus philenor hirsuta — N/Central CA only](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battus_philenor_hirsuta); [NC spicebush](https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/spicebush-swallowtail))
2. **Baby's HAIRLINE modified** (p01 nursery vs. later grandson pages). On direct inspection the p01 baby has a defined reddish-brown hairline with **no surrounding landscape** — reading it as a "ridge silhouette" is an unsupported leap. **No location derivable.** It remains a real edit whose *meaning* is undecoded.
3. **Green BLOB deleted.** Densest green-object cluster is the night-bedroom p06 (turtle + kite + green bedding); the lake far-shore canopy (children's-book p18) is the other plausible erase site. **No residual scar resolves into a shape or a place.** Undecoded.

> **Net from the edits:** the only one that yields a *vector* is the butterfly, and it points **EAST (Carolinas/Appalachian Piedmont)** and **against** the Los Angeles/jacaranda reading. The other two are real but undecoded and give no geography.

## 3. The three "high-intent" artifacts — direct inspection (the key negative result)

The hunter correctly prioritized the artifacts JCB *personally* made/assembled. I rendered and zoomed each from the in-repo crops. **All three fail to yield a location or a cipher:**

- **Dancers art, main-book p60-61** (`screenshots/page-060-061 cropped.png`, JCB AI-generated). Direct view: **8 figures with vertical staffs**; the **gold dots are jewelry/accents worn by the dancers** (necklace pendants, anklets, a ring) — **not** a free-floating constellation or X-pattern. The staffs are near-vertical and do **not** resolve into deliberate alignment lines crossing at a point. The "gold-dot map / X" read is **not supported** by the pixels. Its honest value is thematic only: it literalizes "**Dancers are on the land**."
- **Egyptian faience necklace, main-book p195-196** (`screenshots/page-195-196 cropped.png`, JCB-assembled). Direct view confirms the lazy-S with a high-center carved (Bes-style) **lion/leopard head** pendant and a southern bulb — **but this is the textbook catenary drape of any heavy-pendant broad-collar strand laid flat** ([Usekh collar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usekh_collar); [Met broad collar](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/543873)). It did **not** match any tested public-land loop/lollipop trail or lake-cove/oxbow at correct scale and orientation. **No route match.** Only carry-forward: *if* it ever anchors, the **lion-head pendant marks the DESTINATION** (the LION'S Share), not the trailhead.
- **Hopi calendar, main-book p84-85** (`screenshots/page-084-085 cropped.png`). Direct high-res read of the month band: the "underscore/overscore that encodes a message" is **the calendar's own two concentric ring lines** — *every* month sits between the same outer-rim arc and inner arc. **There is no selective under- vs over-scoring of specific months; there is no hidden readout.** What *is* real and verified: the wheel labels "**21ST SUMMER SOLSTICE**" on the east/right and "**21ST WINTER SOLSTICE**" on the west/left, with a Katsina/Non-Katsina split along a **chevron (notched-ridge) line**. That supports a thematic "**watch a solstice sunrise over a notched ridge**" overlook idea and argues **against** an Arizona pin — but it resolves to **no specific dome**. ([Hopi calendar / Soyal solstice context]).

> **Why this matters:** these were the leads most likely to "crack it," and on inspection they behave exactly as the author's **"no grand cipher"** rule predicts — they're thematic mood-setters (dance, route, solstice/season), not encoded coordinates. Spend zero further effort trying to decode them into digits.

## 6. Boots-on-the-ground plan (typology test, not a dig)

Run this as a **falsification loop** at 1–3 candidate lakes in a single day; do **not** commit to one rock sight-unseen.

**Primary: Lake Norman State Park, Troutman, NC (~35.6504, -80.9491).**
- **Park:** the State Park Road day-use lot serving the **fishing pier + swim/wading beach** (a short, flat walk — satisfies ≤3 mi and carryable two-trip placement).
- **The four-criteria dock test (must ALL pass to keep the lake):** (1) a **small wooden fishing dock/pier**; (2) a **flat, rounded oak/maple/hickory far shore**; (3) **NO ridge/mountain behind that treeline**; (4) the dock **faces open water roughly W** (evening sun glints). If a mountain shows on the horizon, **eliminate the lake and move on** — that single criterion already killed W. Kerr Scott, Rhodhiss, and partially Lake Hickory.
- **The walk + what to look for ("peering behind a rock"):** from the dock/beach, follow the shoreline trail to the nearest **low rock outcrop / flatrock you reach by going slightly UP** (the "if you don't go down…" + "sat upon a rock, wide embrace" pairing). Inspect, do **not dig** (author: not buried): look **behind/under exfoliation slabs, in overhangs, at the base of a lone pine among hardwoods** ("sparkle even as you pine"), and at any **west-facing flat rock** with an open lake view. The box is **>25 lb** — look for a stash a person could carry a few hundred yards from parking, not deep backcountry.
- **Cross-check on site:** does the **lion-head = destination** logic point you to the *far/higher* feature with parking at the near "clasp"? Is there an Eastern **purple swallowtail** present (in-season corroboration of the added-butterfly vector)?

**Secondary (same trip if time): Lake Hickory accesses — Glenn C. Hilton Jr. Memorial Park & Geitner-Rotary Park, Hickory NC.** Closest to the author's Hiddenite/Alexander-County biography; run the identical four-criteria dock test (note: Lake Hickory shows foothill ridges to the north, so it likely *fails* criterion 3 — confirm on site).

## 7. What to confirm BEFORE traveling, and the biggest risk

**Confirm first (cheap, do remotely):**
1. **The poem is valid — do NOT disqualify it (corrected 2026-05-29).** A prior version here claimed _"Joy's Serenade is in Part 2"_ and used a secondhand quote (_"…no information in the poem or related to part 2"_) to invalidate the poem-driven elements. **That is false.** Per the book, **Part Two is the four-chapter section for the four _smaller_ boxes** (p.16-17), while **Joy's Serenade is the back-matter/Postscript Final Poem (pp.205-206), outside Part Two.** The poem is a primary clue vehicle for the Lion's Share (the largest box's clues "may be found anywhere inside this book"; a separate attribution calls the poem "pretty important"). The poem-driven elements of this report stand. See `research/book-structure-verified.md`. ([MW FAQ](https://mysteriouswritings.com/theres-treasure-inside-faq-treasure-hunt-by-jon-collins-black/))
2. **Get a clean back-cover scan of *Our Unbreakable Thread*** (ISBN 9781958325087) and read the **butterfly's open-wing dorsal pattern** — Pipevine vs Spicebush vs Red-spotted Purple is decidable from one good photo and would harden the "EAST" vector; also check whether the butterfly's flight line **points at a map element**.
3. **Settle the flowering-tree species** (jacaranda=LA vs redbud/cherry=NC) from a high-res cover crop — it's the one feature that could swing the whole region to SoCal. Current read: contested; the Eastern butterfly outvotes it.
4. **Satellite-confirm the four dock criteria** for each candidate lake (especially "no ridge on the horizon" and "dock faces W") before driving — eliminate failures from your couch.

**Biggest risk this is wrong (be honest):**
- **The art is symbolic painting, so there is no true pin** — a flat-horizon lake-with-dock fits a huge number of Eastern/Midwest lakes; this is a *typology* match, and the specific lake (Lake Norman) is a reasoned guess, not a solve.
- **The NC anchor is biography, not a location clue, and the author explicitly warns against it** — the same reasoning already produced one failed BOTG search (Rocky Face). If a non-biographical clue ever points elsewhere, follow it.
- **If the flowering tree is jacaranda**, the region flips to **Los Angeles/SoCal** and this entire NC read collapses.
- **The three highest-intent artifacts gave nothing** (§3) — so the location case rests on the *general* water/overlook convergence, which is real but low-resolution. Do not over-trust any single coordinate, including mine.
