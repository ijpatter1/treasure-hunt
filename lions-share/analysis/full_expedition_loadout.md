## NOTES

### On Thermal Imaging
Honestly, skip the FLIR unless money is no object. A metal box that's been sitting for years is in thermal equilibrium with its surroundings. Your $350 is better spent on a backup battery or extra drone flights.

### On the inReach
This is the one "optional" item I'd call essential if you're searching remote National Forest terrain. Sub-60-second SOS response has saved lives. The $15/month is cheap insurance.

### On Grid Searching
JCB said searchers have been within 200 feet of multiple boxes. That's close enough that systematic coverage matters more than fancy technology. Your detector, methodical grid approach, and patience will do more work than any gadget.

### On Documentation
Recording everything means you can review footage later with fresh eyes. The chest-mounted GoPro captures what you actually saw, not what you remember seeing.
