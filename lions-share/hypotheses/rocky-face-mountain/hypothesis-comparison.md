## Critical Discrepancy: Huntersville vs Statesville

### The Issue

**Fresh Analysis (from book text):**
> "My parents met each other in seminary. Soon after graduation, they helped start and lead a new church in **Huntersville, NC**" (PS-085)

**Existing Hypothesis (from research):**
> "David Comer, Founding Pastor, Grace Baptist Church, **Statesville, NC** (1970)"

### Geographic Significance

| Location | Distance from Rocky Face | Region |
|----------|-------------------------|--------|
| **Huntersville** | ~45 miles SE | Charlotte metro area |
| **Statesville** | ~15 miles SE | Iredell County, Piedmont |

These are different cities. Huntersville is in Mecklenburg County near Charlotte. Statesville is in Iredell County, further north.

### Possible Explanations

1. **Multiple churches** - David Comer may have served at churches in both locations at different times
2. **Author used pen location** - Book may use nearby/generic location for privacy
3. **Research error** - External research may have identified wrong church
4. **Fresh analysis misread** - Need to verify book text

### Recommendation

**Verify the source text** in the original book. If it truly says Huntersville, this may shift the geographic center of the search area OR suggest the existing research correctly identified a different/later church.
