## SEARCH PROTOCOL: "Behind a Rock"

### The Fundamental Rule (Chapter 6, p. 69)

> "Just like **peering behind a rock to find a treasure box**, inspiration can appear almost anywhere."

**BEHIND means BEHIND.** Not in crevices. Not under overhangs. Not wedged into cracks. The box is on the **far side** of a prominent rock from the trail approach direction.

### How to Search Each Rock Feature

1. **Identify** prominent rocks (waist-height or larger)
2. **Walk around** to the side facing AWAY from the trail
3. **Peer behind** - bend/lean to see the far side
4. **Check the base** on the far side
5. **Look for** a weather-resistant container sitting or placed naturally
6. **Remember:** "It might surprise you how simple it was to find" (JCB interview)

### What You're Looking For

- Custom-built, weather-resistant treasure box
- **NOT buried** - sitting in a natural spot
- Designed to withstand outdoor exposure (18 months construction)
- Likely visible once you look at the right side of the right rock
- Protected from direct weather but not deeply hidden

## Priority 1: Zone C — 319-320 Granite Clearing (MARKER 320 FOCUS)

**Status:** Downhill side searched Feb 14. Uphill side, far perimeter, and behind-rock areas are UNSEARCHED.

**Why this is Priority 1 — THREE-LINE CONVERGENCE:**
1. **Stanza ordering:** Poem reverses trail order (Stanza 5 treasure before Stanza 6 campsite) = return journey past Zone C
2. **Necklace theory:** Orange bead in the 13-bead / 13-marker mapping = markers 319-320
3. **Hopi calendar:** Five poem-connected ceremony months (1,2,4,5,8) — product = 320, sum+300 = 320
4. "The haiku curls a little further on" = past the 317-318 curve
5. Large prominent rock formation visible on satellite imagery

**Hollow Rock clarification:** The granite gneiss dome sounds hollow when tapped THROUGHOUT the summit. "Hollow Rock" is the geology of the entire summit, NOT a single feature to find. Stop looking for a specific hollow rock formation — search systematically instead.

- [ ] Check any overhangs or sheltered spots on the **uphill edge**
- [ ] Look for natural granite cavities — treasure is "secreted away within walls" (Ch 2)

- [ ] **Get LOW** — "lie on the ground to read" (Ch 9) — look at ground level behind rocks

## ~~Priority 4: The "Hollow Rock" Namesake Feature~~ — RESOLVED

**Status:** RESOLVED (Feb 16, 2026). The granite gneiss dome sounds hollow when tapped throughout the summit. "Hollow Rock" is a geological property of the entire summit, not a single identifiable formation.

**What this means:**
- The trail name describes the geology of where you are, not a specific feature to find
- The "find the Hollow Rock" search strategy is **eliminated**
- The Emerald **Hollow** Mine to **Hollow Rock** Trail naming parallel still holds as thematic evidence
- Search effort previously allocated here is redirected to Zone C systematic searching

**The AllTrails reviewer's "circling hollow rock"** likely refers to the summit loop trail circling the hollow-sounding granite dome itself — the entire summit IS "hollow rock."
