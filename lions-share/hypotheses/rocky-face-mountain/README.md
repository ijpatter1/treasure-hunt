- **Alexander County, NC** - Author's childhood home (Hiddenite area)

- Five explicit NC references cluster around western NC region

### Back Cover Haiku Decoding
```
Be solid, have grit;           ← 5 syllables
sparkle even as you pine.      ← 7 syllables
```

## Theory Summary

The Rocky Face Mountain hypothesis proposes that:
1. The back cover haiku encodes "318" (3 lines + 18 syllables)
2. This corresponds to Marker 318 on Hollow Rock Trail
3. Rocky Face Mountain is in Alexander County, NC - the author's childhood region
4. The treasure is hidden off-trail near a granite outcrop at marker 318
5. Descending from the trail ("go down") leads to the hiding spot
