## Theory Summary

The Rocky Face Mountain hypothesis proposes that:

1. The back cover haiku encodes "**318**" (3 lines + 18 syllables)
2. The park is exactly **318 acres** (independent confirmation)
3. Marker 318 exists on Hollow Rock Trail
4. Rocky Face is in **Hiddenite, NC**—author's childhood gem-hunting area
5. The trail crossing at markers 305-307 forms the "**X**" that "leads the way"
6. Giant Swallowtail butterflies (via Hop-tree) are the "**Dancers**" (Hopi tradition)
7. The treasure is hidden **off-trail**, below marker 318 ("go down")

## Search Protocol Summary

1. Park at Rocky Face Mountain (3451 Rocky Face Church Road, Hiddenite, NC 28636)
2. Take Hollow Rock Trail (red blazes)
3. Pass the X junction where Grindstone Trail crosses (~markers 305-307)
4. Continue to marker 318 where trail curves
5. **Leave trail and descend** to search rock formations below
6. Look for natural crevices, underhangs, sheltered areas in granite

**Optimal timing:** Late spring through fall (butterfly activity), dry conditions
