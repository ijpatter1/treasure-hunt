## Pike Etymology Research

From web search on geographic meaning of "pike":

**Definition**: In dialectal England, "pike" means "a mountain or hill having a peaked summit" - used especially in place names.

**Etymology**: From Middle English *pyke* meaning "sharp point, mountain, peak." Related to Old English *pīc* and Scandinavian origin (West Norwegian *pik* = "pointed mountain").

**Relationship**: "Peak" is a variant of "pike" meaning "sharp point." The mountain meaning recorded ~1400 for pike, ~1630s for peak.

**Usage**: Chiefly Northern England (e.g., Scafell Pike - highest mountain in England).

**Implication for Treasure Hunt**: "Pike" in poem likely refers to peaked/pointed hills or mountains, not the fish.
