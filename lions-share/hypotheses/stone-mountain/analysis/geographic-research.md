## Geological Features

### Stone Mountain (Main Dome)

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Height above valley** | 600 feet |
| **Summit elevation** | 2,305 feet |
| **Rock type** | Biotite granodiorite (quartz diorite) |
| **Age** | ~400 million years (Devonian) |
| **Formation** | Plutonic igneous intrusion into Alligator Back Formation |
| **Designation** | Largest plutonic monadnock in North Carolina |

**Composition:**
- Quartz (light gray, sparkles)
- Alkali feldspar (white/pink)
- Plagioclase feldspar
- Biotite mica (black flakes)
- Muscovite mica (silver flakes)

**Notable Features:**
- Exfoliation dome shape
- Brown-gray color
- Visible from Blue Ridge Parkway
- Weather pits (depressions from differential weathering)
- Xenoliths (dark inclusions of biotite gneiss)

## Trail System

### Hiking Trails

| Trail | Distance | Difficulty | Blaze | Key Features |
|-------|----------|------------|-------|--------------|
| **Stone Mountain Loop** | 4.5 mi loop | Strenuous | Orange circles | Summit, waterfall, homestead |
| **Wolf Rock Trail** | 1.5 mi one-way | Moderate | Red squares | Wolf Rock summit, MST connection |
| **Cedar Rock Trail** | 1.0 mi one-way | Moderate | Red circles | Cedar Rock views |
| **Middle/Lower Falls Trail** | 1.0 mi one-way | Moderate | Blue circles | Two waterfalls |
| **Widow's Creek Trail** | 2.5 mi one-way | Strenuous | Unknown | Creek access, swimming |
| **Black Jack Ridge Trail** | 1.5 mi one-way | Moderate | White rectangles | MST connection |
| **Connector Trail** | 0.75 mi one-way | Moderate | Unknown | Trail connector |
| **Stone Mountain K.I.P. Track** | 0.4 mi one-way | Easy | Unknown | First section of Loop |
| **Mountains-to-Sea Trail** | 11.32 mi one-way | Advanced | White blazes | Multi-park connector |

### Equestrian Trails

| Trail | Distance | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Bridle Loop Trail** | 2 mi loop | Horseback and hiking |
| **Bridle Out-and-Back** | 4 mi one-way | Horseback and hiking |

### Trail Characteristics

- **Most Popular:** Stone Mountain Loop Trail (4,500+ AllTrails reviews)
- **Less Crowded:** Wolf Rock Trail, Cedar Rock Trail ("underrated and often underpopulated")
- **Trail Surface:** Mix of gravel, rock, dirt; some sections cross open granite
- **Blazes on Rock:** Painted directly on granite in exposed areas

## Historic Sites

### Hutchinson Homestead

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Established** | 1855 |
| **Founders** | John and Cidney Jane Hutchinson |
| **Family size** | 8 children |
| **Restored** | 1998 |
| **State acquisition** | 1969 (Jim Hutchinson became first park ranger) |

**Structures:**
- Log cabin (original 1855)
- Barn
- Corn crib
- Smokehouse
- Blacksmith shop
- Orchard

**Location:** At base of Stone Mountain, accessible via Stone Mountain Loop Trail
