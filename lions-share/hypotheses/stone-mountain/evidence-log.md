## 2026-01-10 — Trail Analysis: 11 Trails Identified

**Source:** NC Parks website trails page
**Finding:** Park has 11 trails:
1. Black Jack Ridge Trail (1.5 mi, moderate)
2. Cedar Rock Trail (1 mi, moderate)
3. Connector Trail (0.75 mi, moderate)
4. Middle Falls/Lower Falls Trail (1 mi, moderate)
5. Stone Mountain Loop Trail (4.5 mi loop, strenuous)
6. Stone Mountain K.I.P. Track Trail (0.4 mi, easy)
7. Widow's Creek Trail (2.5 mi, strenuous)
8. Wolf Rock Trail (1.5 mi, moderate)
9. Mountains-to-Sea State Trail (11.32 mi, advanced)
10. Bridle Loop Trail (2 mi loop, moderate)
11. Bridle Out-and-Back Trail (4 mi, moderate)
**Classification:** Direct
**Impact:** Neutral
**Notes:** Trail blazes use geometric shapes (squares, circles, hexagons, triangles), NOT numbered markers. This differs from Rocky Face which has numbered markers (318).
