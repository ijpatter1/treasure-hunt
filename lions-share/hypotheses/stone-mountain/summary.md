# Stone Mountain State Park - Hypothesis Summary

*Last updated: 2026-01-10*

---

## Executive Summary

Stone Mountain State Park in northwestern North Carolina is a **Tier 1 candidate** based on strong geographic matches to Joy's Serenade poem elements (granite dome, waterfall, elevated terrain). However, it **lacks the critical evidence** that makes Rocky Face Mountain compelling:

- **No 318 encoding** discovered
- **Not in author's childhood area** (Wilkes/Alleghany counties, not Alexander County/Hiddenite)
- **No direct chapter references** to Stone Mountain, Wilkes County, or Sparta
- **No numbered trail markers** (uses geometric shapes instead)

**Current Assessment:** Worth investigating, but significantly weaker than Rocky Face hypothesis.

## Conclusion

Stone Mountain State Park offers **strong geographic matches** to Joy's Serenade poem elements but **critically lacks**:
- Any 318 numerical encoding
- Direct connection to author's childhood (Hiddenite/Alexander County)
- Chapter references pointing to this specific location
- The "relatively unknown" character that matches "where few have seen it"

**Recommendation:** Maintain as secondary candidate. Prioritize Rocky Face Mountain hypothesis which has triple 318 confirmation, is IN the author's childhood area, and matches more poem elements with direct evidence.
