### Ch 6: "Rubies to Wear" / "Inspiration Is Welcome" — THE SEARCH METHOD
- p.69: "Just like **peering BEHIND a rock** to find a treasure box"
- BEHIND = directional. The box is on the far side of a rock, hidden from your approach direction
- "A rock" = singular, a specific freestanding rock you walk around

### Ch 14: "Tiffany's Furnace & Thoreau's Fire" / "Fail Forward" — FLOWERS + NATURE
- p.127: "blue and pink irises...perhaps you'll pass by similar flowers along your way"
- Dwarf crested iris (Iris cristata) blooms March-May at Rocky Face elevation
