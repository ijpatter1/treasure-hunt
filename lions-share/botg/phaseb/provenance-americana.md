# Phase B — Item-Provenance-to-Ground: Americana Cluster (NC-Unconstrained)

**Agent:** item-provenance-to-ground (americana), run NC-UNCONSTRAINED
**Task:** Trace each item's real-world US geography to a SPECIFIC, ACCESSIBLE US PUBLIC-LAND site that could be a "clue by association" to a hide.
**Hard filter:** A tie only QUALIFIES if it lands on accessible public land within ~3 mi of a road, not buried, not dangerous. A private collection, private/hazardous mine, or an urban museum building does NOT qualify (note as context only).

**Bottom line up front:** The four americana items are overwhelmingly tied to *private collections, urban museums, presidential libraries, auction houses, and private estates* — none of which qualify as a hide site. Exactly ONE item touches a genuinely accessible US public-land natural site (Thoreau → Walden Pond State Reservation, MA), and it points to Massachusetts, away from NC. The Golden Chalice is a foreign-origin artifact (gold Chavin chalice, Peru) and is pure decoy. The single NC-orbit thread (Tiffany collection at Biltmore, Asheville) is DISQUALIFIED — Biltmore is a private ticketed estate, not public land. Net: this cluster does NOT reinforce a North Carolina hide. Detail below.

## 1. George Washington Jelly Glass (Ch. 17 — "Share Your Story")

**Book's stated provenance (Ch. 17 note):** Crystal jelly glass, English rose pattern, green wax Washington-family seal on the bottom; one of only 2 known. Used during Washington's **presidency in Philadelphia** for dinners/entertaining, then brought to **Mount Vernon, Virginia** after his two terms. Displayed in **more than 10 museums including the Gerald Ford and Herbert Hoover presidential libraries**; the sister glass is at the **Mount Vernon Museum**.

**Geography of origin/association:**
- An 18th-century blown-glass jelly/sweetmeat glass associated with George Washington. The glass's *pattern* is English (rose-pattern crystal) — origin abroad = decoy/context per the rules. Its US associations are **Philadelphia** (presidential use) and **Mount Vernon, Fairfax County, Virginia** (Potomac River), plus the **10+ museums** that have displayed it (Gerald Ford Presidential Library/Museum, Grand Rapids/Ann Arbor MI; Herbert Hoover Presidential Library, West Branch IA; Mount Vernon Museum, VA).
- Washington's broader Federal-era footprint also touches **Federal Hall / Wall Street, NYC** (1789 inauguration) and Revolutionary sites (Delaware River, NY, Monmouth NJ).

**Lands on accessible public land?** NO (as a hide site).
- The 10+ museums and presidential libraries are **urban/institutional buildings**, not accessible wildland public land. Context only.
- Mount Vernon is a **private historic estate** (Mount Vernon Ladies' Association), ticketed, not public land. Context only.
- Federal Hall is an **urban NPS building** (Federal Hall National Memorial) — a building, not hide-relevant wild public land. Context only.

**Distinctiveness:** Low for our purposes. The display footprint is nationally dispersed (MI, IA, VA, NYC) and entirely institutional. Nothing lands on a wild, accessible public-land parcel.

**Reinforces NC?** NO. The documented geography points to **Philadelphia / Virginia / MI / IA** — not North Carolina. (Even Washington's 1791 Southern Tour, which did pass through NC, attaches to towns/roads, not an accessible wild public-land hide site, and the glass itself has no NC tie.)

**Sources:** Repo Ch. 17 note (book provenance); Mount Vernon Ladies' Association "Jelly glass" object record (emuseum.mountvernon.org); Gerald Ford / Herbert Hoover presidential libraries. Confirmed via web search this run.

## 2. The Golden Chalice (Ch. 9 — "Confirmation Bias")

**CORRECTION from book provenance (repo Ch. 9 note):** The item is NOT an ecclesiastical/European chalice. It is a **gold Chavin chalice — Chavin culture, Peru, ~600–1000 BC**, a ceremonial drinking vessel possibly tied to the Chavín de Huántar temple in the Peruvian Andes (10,000+ ft, at a confluence of two rivers).

**Geography of origin/association:**
- The item's entire factual provenance is **Peru (Andes)** — a foreign origin, which is **decoy/context per the rules** (the hide is in the US; foreign item origins do not count). There is NO US natural-site provenance for this object at all.
- The chapter's *US* content is purely metaphorical (Chavín de Huántar used as an allegory for confirmation bias) plus the author's childhood church memory (unlocated Baptist congregation). Neither attaches to a specific US public-land parcel.

**Lands on accessible public land?** NO. Foreign-origin artifact; no US site of any kind. Context only.

**Distinctiveness:** N/A for US geography — there is no US tie to assess.

**Reinforces NC?** NO — neutral/none. (The chapter's "confluence of two rivers" and "look both ways before crossing" are general thematic motifs, not item-provenance ground-ties, and would belong to a clue-analysis pass, not this provenance pass.)

**Sources:** Repo Ch. 9 note (book provenance: gold Chavin chalice, Peru). Web search returned no US provenance for the object (only unrelated Holy-Grail/legend and game results), consistent with a foreign-origin artifact.

**Reinforces NC?** NO. Points firmly to the **Northeast (NYC / Cape Cod / Martha's Vineyard)**. No NC tie of any kind.

## 4. Tiffany Favrile Glass (Furnace) & Thoreau Journal Pages (Ch. 14 — "Fail Forward")

**Geography of origin/association:**

*Tiffany favrile glass / furnace (note: book item is the one-of-a-kind Tiffany 'Iris' vase, ~1900, LCT initials; sister piece at the Met):*
- Louis Comfort Tiffany's favrile glass was produced at the **Tiffany Furnaces / Stourbridge Glass Co. in Corona, Queens, New York** (Arthur J. Nash, ex-Stourbridge UK, ran the works; patent 1894). Tiffany's estate **Laurelton Hall** was at **Cold Spring Harbor / Oyster Bay, Long Island, NY** (burned 1957). Tiffany glass is concentrated in **museums** (Charles Hosmer Morse Museum, Winter Park, FL; The Met, NYC; Neustadt Collection, Queens).
- **NC tie surfaced (but DISQUALIFIED):** The **Biltmore Estate, Asheville, NC** holds a significant Louis Comfort Tiffany collection — a 1890 hand-blown Tiffany vase in the Louis XV "Claude Room" suite, plus ~45 Tiffany stained-glass lamps. This is the ONLY North-Carolina association anywhere in the americana cluster. HOWEVER, **Biltmore is a private, ticketed estate (the Vanderbilt/Cecil family) — NOT accessible public land.** It is a building/private-grounds tie, so it does NOT qualify as a hide site. Context only. (It is mildly interesting that the Tiffany trail touches Asheville/Buncombe Co., which is in the western NC orbit, but it cannot seed a hide.)
- All other Tiffany geography is **NYC / Long Island (urban + private estate)** plus museums. None is accessible wild public land.

*Thoreau journal / "Thoreau's Fire":*
- Henry David Thoreau is anchored to **Concord, Massachusetts** — specifically **Walden Pond (Walden Pond State Reservation), Middlesex County, MA**. THIS is the one genuinely accessible US public-land natural site in the entire americana cluster: a **state reservation, day-use, within yards of a road (Route 126), not buried, not dangerous, gets winter snow.**
- "Thoreau's Fire" references the **1844 Concord woods fire** Thoreau accidentally set (Fair Haven / Concord woods area, MA) — also Concord public/conservation land.

**Lands on accessible US public land?** PARTIAL — ONE qualifying site, but it is in **Massachusetts, not NC**:
- **Walden Pond State Reservation, Concord, MA** = a real, accessible, day-use public-land natural site that fits the discriminator stack (water at the hide, woods, road-accessible, winter snow). It QUALIFIES as an accessible public-land tie *in the abstract* — but it points to **Massachusetts**, an eastern-outlier direction inconsistent with the NC zone.
- Tiffany side: NO qualifying public land (all urban/private/museum). Context only.

**Distinctiveness:** HIGH for Thoreau (Walden Pond is singular and uniquely public/natural). LOW for Tiffany.

**Reinforces NC?** NO — the only clean public-land tie (Walden Pond) points to **Massachusetts**, away from NC. Tiffany points to NY. This item, if anything, is a counter-weight to NC, not support.

**Sources:** Tiffany Furnaces (Corona, Queens) and Laurelton Hall history; Walden Pond State Reservation (MA DCR); Thoreau 1844 Concord fire. Live web verification unavailable this run.
