# Candidate Dossier — Statesville / Iredell County NC sub-area (Phase B discriminator search)

**Agent role:** Discriminator-search for the Statesville/Iredell sub-area (Lake Norman environs,
foothill terrain). Test specific accessible-public-land SPOTS for at-one-point co-location of the
Group P / Group Q discriminators.

**Inputs read:**
- `botg/phaseb/geometry.md` — bounded zone 35.78–36.01 N, 80.89–82.16 W. Statesville/Iredell is the
  SE corner (the Piedmont floor end of the corridor); geometry.md explicitly flags Lake Norman SP as
  "public + water, but low/flat; weak on the vantage discriminator."
- `botg/phaseb/provenance-*.md` — grep for iredell/statesville/lake norman/stumpy/lookout shoals/
  mooresville/troutman returned **ZERO hits**. No treasure-item provenance ties to this sub-area.
  Statesville's only book hook is biographical (Ch18 parents' church) — an anchor, not a discriminator.

## Bottom line up front

This sub-area is the **weakest terrain in the whole western cluster** on the single most important
discriminator: the Ch4 "elevation/vista that opens onto lower country." Iredell County is rolling-to-
flat upper Piedmont (~800–1,000 ft). The accessible public land here delivers **water-in-view** very
well (Lake Norman / Lookout Shoals) but **fails the vista/landform discriminator** almost everywhere.
There is **no public spot in Iredell County that co-locates a true escarpment/monadnock vista with a
sit-on rock and water** — the defining Ch4 landform simply doesn't exist on accessible public land
here. The one place with real relief (the Brushy Mountains crest in the far NW corner) is **private**.

Result: **2 weak candidates, 0 strong.** Honest verdict — this sub-area is a water-discriminator
match only and should rank BELOW the Brushy-front / Blue-Ridge-front sub-areas. It is the anchor's
home turf but not a pinpoint zone.

## Candidate 1 — Lake Norman State Park (Lake Shore Trail / Itusi shoreline), Troutman, Iredell Co

**Approx:** ~35.66 N, 80.94 W (park core, trailheads off State Park Rd / Shortleaf Dr, Troutman;
shoreline straddles toward Sherrills Ford). NOTE: this sits ~0.12 deg SOUTH of the geometry.md zone's
south edge (35.78 N) — it is just OUTSIDE the bounded box, ~8-12 mi S of Statesville. Included because
it is the marquee public land "near Statesville" and the obvious water candidate. Park is 1,941 ac,
~38 mi of hiking + ~31 mi Itusi bike trail; web-confirmed elevation 760–870 ft, total vertical gain
only ~310 ft over the whole loop — i.e., essentially flat.

**Public land + 3-mi-road:** YES. NC State Park, free, day-use, paved park roads, multiple trailhead
lots. All trails within ~3 mi of a road. Carryable / single-day-walkable: YES (gentle trails).

**Discriminators MET at the shoreline:**
- WATER in view (not crossed): STRONG. The Lake Shore Trail (~5.4 mi) and Itusi mtn-bike trails hug
  the Lake Norman shoreline; water is the dominant feature. No crossing required.
- OAKS: MET. Piedmont oak-hickory-pine forest is the park's cover type.
- BLUE+PINK SPRING WILDFLOWERS: PLAUSIBLE/MET. Piedmont spring flora (redbud=pink, dwarf-crested
  iris/violets=blue) is generic to this forest type. Not distinctive, but present.
- SNOW IN WINTER: MET (marginal). Iredell gets occasional winter snow, less reliably than the
  mountains.
- NOT BURIED / NOT DANGEROUS / CARRYABLE: MET. No cliffs, no water crossing, gentle terrain.

**Discriminators FAILED / weak at this spot:**
- ELEVATION / VISTA OPENING ONTO LOWER COUNTRY: **FAILED.** This is the killer. The park is flat-to-
  gently-rolling at lake level (~760 ft pool, surrounding land ~800–900 ft). There is no high vantage,
  no escarpment, nothing "opens onto lower country." The view is ACROSS the lake at the SAME level,
  which is not the Ch4 landform (a vantage looking DOWN onto lower terrain). This is the exact reason
  geometry.md pre-flagged it as "weak on the vantage discriminator."
- SIT-ON / LOOK-BEHIND ROCK: WEAK-PARTIAL. Trail reports DO mention "little outcroppings that
  overlook the lake" used as snack/rest spots, plus "orange-colored bluffs" along the coves and
  "rocky ridges" on the Lake Shore Trail (web-confirmed). So a sit-on shoreline rock exists in a
  generic sense — but there is NO single notable named/landmark rock (no "Hollow Rock"-class
  feature), so it is non-distinctive and gives no pinpoint.
- DARK SKY: FAILED/WEAK. Lake Norman is a heavily developed reservoir ringed by Charlotte-metro
  suburban sprawl (Mooresville/Cornelius/Denver glow to the S). Sky is not dark.

**Fit score: 30/100.** Excellent water, real public access, but the central vista discriminator and
dark-sky both fail, the rock is unremarkable, and it falls just outside the geometry box. A
water-only match.

**Sources:** ncparks.gov Lake Norman State Park page; AllTrails/hiking reports for Lake Shore Trail;
general Lake Norman development/light-pollution knowledge.

## Candidate 2 — Lookout Shoals Lake public access (Catawba River, Iredell/Alexander/Catawba tripoint)

**Approx:** ~35.83 N, 81.16 W (Lookout Shoals Dam / access areas on the Catawba between the Brushies
and the Piedmont). This IS inside the geometry box (35.78–36.01 N, 80.89–82.16 W) and sits between the
Hiddenite and Statesville anchors — geographically the best-placed water in the sub-area.

**Public land + 3-mi-road:** PARTIAL/UNCERTAIN. Lookout Shoals is a Duke Energy reservoir; there are
public boat-access / shoreline-management areas, but much shoreline is Duke-managed or private.
Public-access points exist near the dam and at designated launches; verify exact parcel ownership
before any BOTG (Group Q hard filter — accessible PUBLIC land only). Within ~3 mi of road: YES at the
access points.

**Discriminators MET:**
- WATER in view (not crossed): STRONG (the lake/river itself).
- SOME RELIEF: PARTIAL. This stretch of the Catawba is where the river leaves the Brushy/Blue-Ridge
  foothills and enters the Piedmont, so there is modestly more topographic texture than at Lake
  Norman — low bluffs along the river in places. But no true escarpment vista.
- OAKS / SPRING WILDFLOWERS / SNOW / NOT-DANGEROUS / CARRYABLE: MET (generic Piedmont-edge forest).

**Discriminators FAILED / weak:**
- ELEVATION / VISTA OPENING ONTO LOWER COUNTRY: WEAK/FAILED. Low river bluffs are not a "view opens
  onto lower country" vantage; you are AT water level looking across, like Lake Norman.
- SIT-ON ROCK: UNCONFIRMED. "Shoals" implies rock in the river, but no documented accessible sit-on
  rock feature on confirmed public land.
- PUBLIC-LAND CERTAINTY: WEAK. Mixed Duke/private ownership; the specific accessible public parcel is
  not pinned down.
- DARK SKY: MODERATE. Less developed than Lake Norman; darker but not dark-sky-grade.

**Fit score: 28/100.** Better placed in-zone than Lake Norman and slightly more relief, but the
public-ownership ambiguity and the failed vista discriminator hold it down. Would need parcel
verification to even qualify under Group Q.

**Sources:** Duke Energy Lookout Shoals Lake recreation/shoreline-management info; general Catawba
River geography. Public-access parcel status UNVERIFIED — flagged for field/GIS check.

## Considered and REJECTED (no co-location)

- **Brushy Mountains crest in NW Iredell (highest relief in-county, e.g. Fox Mtn / New Salem area
  ridges).** This is the ONLY part of Iredell with a true monadnock-over-Piedmont vista (the exact
  Ch4 landform). But it is **PRIVATE LAND** — no public trail, no game land, no park on the Iredell
  side of the Brushies. Fails the Group Q public-land hard filter outright. (The public Brushy crest
  access is over in Alexander Co at Rocky Face Mtn Rec Area and the emerging Brushy Mtns State Natural
  Area — both OUTSIDE this Iredell sub-area and owned by the Alexander/Brushy-front agents.)
- **Stumpy Creek Game Land (Iredell, on Lake Norman).** Public NCWRC game land WITH water — but it is
  flat reservoir-edge terrain (boat ramp / waterfowl impoundment character), no vista, no notable
  rock. Same failure profile as Lake Norman SP, plus hunting-season access caveats. Not a co-location.
- **Statesville city/county parks & greenways (Fourth Creek greenway, etc.).** Urban/suburban; no
  vista, no wild rock, light-polluted. Not candidates.

## Honest synthesis for the orchestrator

- **No spot in the Statesville/Iredell sub-area clears the bar.** The two scored candidates (Lake
  Norman SP 30, Lookout Shoals 28) are WATER-ONLY matches that FAIL the central Ch4 vista/landform
  discriminator and have weak/absent sit-on-rock and dark-sky.
- The reason is structural: Iredell is Piedmont FLOOR. The bounded zone's discriminator-rich terrain
  (escarpment/monadnock vistas) lives to the NW (Brushy front in Alexander Co, Blue Ridge front in
  Caldwell/Burke/Avery, Roan near Bakersville) — NOT in Iredell.
- Statesville's value to the hunt is as a **biographical anchor that bounds the zone**, exactly as the
  frame intends. It does not itself contain the pinpoint. This sub-area should rank LOW; effort is
  better spent on the Brushy-front and Blue-Ridge-front sub-areas where vista + rock + water + dark
  sky can actually co-locate.
- One residual to verify only if other sub-areas dead-end: the **Lookout Shoals** public-parcel
  ownership and whether any accessible public bluff there gives even a minor downstream vista. Low
  priority.
