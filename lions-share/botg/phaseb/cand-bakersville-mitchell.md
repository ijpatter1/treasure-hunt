# Candidate Dossier — Bakersville / Mitchell County NC sub-area

**Agent:** Discriminator-search, NC sub-area `bakersville-mitchell`.
**Anchor seeded by:** Ch3 box-maker Seth Gould; "40 mi NE of Asheville" (geometry.md anchor at
~36.01 N, 82.16 W). Provenance tie: the Spruce Pine Mining District (Mitchell/Avery/Yancey) gem +

**Frame:** This anchor bounds a region; the region-agnostic discriminators must do the pinpointing.
I tested each candidate against the FULL stack at ONE point.

## Discriminator stack (the bar each spot must clear)

Group P (terrain/flora, want all at one point): (1) sit-on / look-behind ROCK; (2) water in view,
not crossed; (3) ELEVATION with a vista that "opens onto **lower country**" (Ch4 landform —
specifically lower/Piedmont-like country, not a panorama of *other mountains*); (4) shady **oaks**;
(5) blue + pink **spring** wildflowers; (6) dark-ish sky.
Group Q (hard filters): accessible PUBLIC land; within ~3 mi of a road; not buried; not dangerous
(no water crossing / swift current / cliff / ledge); not underwater; gets winter snow; bear +
venomous-snake habitat plausible; box carryable & single-day-walkable.

## The structural problem with this sub-area (read first)

Mitchell County's public land is overwhelmingly the **Roan Highlands high country (5,500–6,300 ft)**.
That terrain is excellent on three discriminators (blue+pink spring/early-summer flora, dark sky,
guaranteed winter snow) but **systematically fails or strains the others**:

- **Oaks (FAIL at the high spots):** Roan's summits are **grassy bald + spruce-fir + rhododendron**,
  not oak forest. The "shady oak" discriminator dies above ~4,500 ft here.
- **"Opens onto lower country" (PARTIAL/STRAINED):** The Ch4 landform wants high ground looking out
  over markedly **lower country (Piedmont-like flats)**. Roan's balds look out over *more
  mountains* and deep valleys, not onto open lower country. The truer "lower country" escarpment
  overlook in this western corridor lives on the **Blue Ridge front** (Grandfather/South Mountains/
  Parkway) — i.e., **outside** this sub-area, in the Caldwell/Burke/Avery escarpment block.
- **"Not dangerous / winter-searchable / within 3 mi of road" (STRAINED):** the best balds (Grassy
  Ridge, Hump, Little Hump) sit miles up the AT from the nearest road and/or behind seasonally
  **gated** forest roads (e.g., FR 130 to Roan High Bluff). High exposure + winter ice work against
  "not dangerous" and "single-day-walkable."

Net: **no spot in this sub-area co-locates the full stack cleanly.** Several score moderate; none is
a confident X. This is a valid "region-level, no pinpoint" result for this anchor. Honest verdict:
the Bakersville anchor is best read as a *regional* signal (box-maker biography + gem belt), and the
Ch4 "lower country" vista discriminator actually points the search **east of here, to the Blue Ridge
escarpment front**, not to the Roan high country.

## Candidates (each scored against the full stack AT ONE POINT)

### 1. Roan High Bluff overlook (Pisgah NF, Toecane/Appalachian RD) — fitScore 47
Approx 36.10 N, 82.13 W (high Roan crest, ~6,267 ft). Wooden observation platform built at the lip
of a rock bluff, reached via **FR 130** off the Rhododendron Gardens road.
- MET: a real **rock** bluff (look-behind/edge rock) ✔; **vista** ✔ (and on clear days it does see
  toward lower country S/SW, the strongest "lower country" read among the high spots); **dark sky** ✔;
  **winter snow** ✔; **blue+pink flora** ✔ (Catawba rhododendron = pink; spring ephemerals lower
  down); **public land** ✔.
- FAILED/strained: **oaks** ✗ (spruce-fir, not oak); **water in view** ✗ (no water at the lip);
  **3-mi-from-road / accessible** strained (FR 130 is **seasonally gated**, so winter access poor —
  conflicts with the snow/searchability intent); **not dangerous** strained (cliff edge — the brief's
  "no cliff/ledge" filter pushes here; the platform mitigates but the landform is a bluff).
- Why not higher: two hard P-discriminators absent (oaks, water) and a Q strain (cliff + gated).

### 2. Grassy Ridge Bald (AT from Carvers Gap, Pisgah NF) — fitScore 43
Approx 36.10 N, 82.10 W (~6,160 ft). The only true 360° bald here; rock outcrops on the crest.
- MET: **rock** outcrops ✔; sweeping **vista** ✔; **dark sky** ✔; **snow** ✔; **blue+pink flora** ✔
  (rhododendron + bald wildflowers); **public** ✔; **bear/snake habitat** plausible ✔.
- FAILED/strained: **oaks** ✗ (grassy bald); **water in view** ✗; **"lower country"** ✗ (views are
  onto *other mountains*, not lower country); **within 3 mi of road** ✗ (round-trip ~4–5 mi on the AT
  from Carvers Gap — the summit is **>2 mi** from the trailhead). Multiple core failures.

### 3. Round Bald / Jane Bald (AT just N of Carvers Gap NC-261) — fitScore 41
Approx 36.11 N, 82.11 W (~5,800 ft). Closest balds to a paved road (Carvers Gap on NC-261, ~1 mi to
Round Bald) → this one **does** clear the 3-mi filter.
- MET: **3-mi/road access** ✔ (paved trailhead, ~1 mi walk); **vista** ✔; **blue+pink flora** ✔;
  **dark sky** ✔; **snow** ✔; **public** ✔.
- FAILED/strained: **oaks** ✗ (grassy/spruce edge); **sit-on/look-behind rock** weak (grassy bald,
  few distinct outcrops vs. a named seat-rock); **water in view** ✗; **"lower country"** ✗ (mountain
  panorama, not lower country). Good access but thin on the rock + water + lower-country + oak core.

### 4. Toe River / Spruce Pine Riverwalk (town park) — fitScore 14
Approx 35.92 N, 82.07 W. Developed paved greenway along the North Toe River.
- MET: **water** ✔ (river right there); **public** ✔; **3-mi/road** ✔; **oaks** plausible along
  banks ✔.
- FAILED: **vista / "opens onto lower country"** ✗ (valley floor, no elevation); **sit-on rock /
  vantage** ✗; this is flat town parkland — fails the entire Ch4 vantage-landform premise. Listed
  only to show the valley-floor option was checked and rejected.

## Excluded (checked, do not qualify)

  fails the public-land hard filter. Excluded.
- **Hump Mountain / Little Hump Mountain:** grassy balds ~5,500 ft; the view *does* drop toward the
  NC/TN lower valley (a better "lower country" read), BUT the summits are a **strenuous, multi-mile
  AT walk** from any road (fails ~3-mi filter) and grassy, not oak. Excluded on access + oaks.
- **Roan Mountain Rhododendron Gardens / Cloudland Trail:** paved interpretive area; flora ✔ and snow
  ✔ but it's a managed garden loop without a sit-rock-over-lower-country point; spruce-fir, no oaks,
  no water-in-view vista. Not a credible single-point X.
- **Spruce Pine Mining District / gem mines:** private + hazardous source land — explicitly does NOT
  count per the rules (mirrors the CA "Red Ledge" dead end). Provenance/region color only.

## Bottom line for this sub-area

**Few/zero strong candidates.** The honest result: the Bakersville/Mitchell anchor seeds a region
whose dominant public land (Roan high country) is **type-mismatched** to the FULL discriminator
stack — it lacks the **oak + water-in-view + true-lower-country** combination at any single
accessible, road-near, non-dangerous, winter-searchable point. The highest local score (Roan High
Bluff, 47) is dragged down by missing oaks, no water, a cliff/ledge strain, and a winter-gated road.

Most useful cross-finding: the Ch4 **"view opens onto lower country"** discriminator is satisfied
much more naturally on the **Blue Ridge escarpment front EAST of here** (Grandfather / South
Mountains / Blue Ridge Parkway overlooks over the Piedmont) and on the **Brushy Mountains monadnock
near the Hiddenite/Statesville anchors** — both *outside* this sub-area. I recommend the orchestrator
weight those sub-areas above Bakersville for the pinpoint, and treat Bakersville as **regional
corroboration** (box-maker biography + gem belt), not as the hide location.
