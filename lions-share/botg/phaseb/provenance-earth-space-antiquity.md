## METHOD & GATE
- Trace each item's real-world geography. Item ORIGINS abroad = decoy/context per the rules; the hide is in the US.
- A tie COUNTS only if it lands on **accessible US PUBLIC land, within ~3 mi of a road, not private, not hazardous, not an urban museum building, not a private/hazardous mine.**
- An object's *find/fall site* can in principle qualify (it is ground), but only if that exact site is public land — most meteorite find spots are private farm fields or un-memorialized GPS points with no public access.
- Honest default: most items have NO qualifying US public-land tie. Said plainly where true. No X manufactured.
