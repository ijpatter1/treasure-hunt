## Method recap

I aggregated every "qualifying accessible-public-land tie" the six cluster agents found, then ran
each non-NC public-land tie through the full discriminator stack (terrain/flora Group P + hard
filters Group Q) via web verification, and asked the only question that matters: **does any non-NC
provenance spot BEAT the NC western-Piedmont bounded zone?**

The six dossiers between them surfaced exactly **TWO qualifying accessible-public-land ties** across
the entire item set, plus one cross-referenced NC public site:
1. **Octahedral diamond → Crater of Diamonds State Park, AR** (shipwreck-backitems cluster) — non-NC.
2. **Thoreau's Fire → Walden Pond State Reservation, MA** (americana cluster) — non-NC.
3. **Massive gold-rush nugget (Ch12) → Reed Gold Mine State Historic Site, NC** (cross-ref from
   shipwreck-backitems; the Ch12 item itself wasn't in any cluster's scope) — NC but OFF-zone.

Everything else is a foreign-origin decoy, a private/pay-to-dig mine, an urban museum building, an
auction house, or a private estate — none of which can seed a hide per the rules. (This includes the
two "provenance-pure" US gem origins — gold nugget → CA Sierra Mother Lode and sapphire → Montana —
which both resolve to PRIVATE claims/mines, the exact CA "Red Ledge" failure mode.)

## Candidate 1 — Crater of Diamonds State Park, Murfreesboro, Pike County, ARKANSAS  (non-NC)

- **Provenance tie:** back-matter octahedral diamond → the only place on Earth open to the public on a
  finders-keepers basis, on AR Hwy 301. A genuinely clean, distinctive provenance-to-public-land link.
- **Public land + 3-mi road:** YES. State park directly on a state highway. PASSES both hard filters.
- **Discriminators MET:** accessible public land; within 3 mi of road; the **Little Missouri River**
  borders the park (water present); the park has wooded areas, a campground, and walking trails;
  southern Arkansas does get occasional/light winter snow; bear + venomous-snake (copperhead/
  cottonmouth/timber rattler) habitat is plausible in AR.
- **Discriminators FAILED:** the actual diamond ground is a **37.5-acre plowed field** atop an eroded
  lamproite volcanic pipe — NOT wild woodland; **no elevation / no high vantage opening onto lower
  country** (the load-bearing Ch4 landform); the iconic "search area" is a managed, regularly-plowed,
  heavily-trafficked tourist field where a hidden box is implausible and would be turned up; winter
  snow is marginal/unreliable (a weak match to the "real winters" searchability filter).
- **Beats NC zone?** NO. Distinctive provenance, but it points to southwest Arkansas, fails the
  vantage discriminator, and its signature ground is a plowed field — not a concealment-friendly wild
  public-land hide. It is the single strongest *clean* provenance-to-public-land link in the whole
  item set, which is exactly why it is worth stating plainly that it still does not clear the bar.
- **Fit score:** 32.
- **Sources:** en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crater_of_Diamonds_State_Park; arkansasstateparks.com/parks/
  crater-diamonds-state-park (verified this run).

## Candidate 2 — Walden Pond State Reservation, Concord, Middlesex County, MASSACHUSETTS  (non-NC)

- **Provenance tie:** Ch14 "Thoreau's Fire" → Henry David Thoreau ↔ Walden Pond. The one literary
  provenance in the whole set that lands on real, accessible public natural land.
- **Public land + 3-mi road:** YES. State reservation, day-use, within yards of MA Route 126.
- **Discriminators MET:** accessible public land; on a road; **pond water at the site** ("magic in
  the water"); oak/pine woods; reliably gets **winter snow**; not buried / not dangerous; spring
  wildflowers plausible.
- **Discriminators FAILED:** **elevation with a high vantage opening onto lower country is essentially
  ABSENT** — Walden is a kettle-pond basin in gently rolling glacial terrain (local high points like
  Pine Hill / Emerson's Cliff are modest and do not give the "view onto lower country" the Ch4
  landform implies); **concealability is poor** — it is one of the most heavily visited, tightly
  managed literary-pilgrimage sites in America (a >25-lb box hidden there would be found fast and
  draw attention); **bear habitat is marginal** in eastern Massachusetts suburbs; and there is **no
  JCB biographical anchor** in MA comparable to the four NC anchors.
- **Beats NC zone?** NO. It is the most "public-land-real" non-NC thread, but it fails the
  load-bearing vantage discriminator AND concealability, and has zero biographical pull. If anything
  it is a mild counter-weight (an eastern/Northeast direction), not support for any zone.
- **Fit score:** 30.
- **Sources:** en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walden_Pond; MA DCR Walden Pond State Reservation; americana
  cluster dossier (verified this run).

## Candidate 3 — Reed Gold Mine State Historic Site, Midland/Stanfield, Cabarrus County, NORTH CAROLINA  (NC, off-zone)

- **Provenance tie:** Ch12 "Massive Gold Rush Nugget" → the site of the **first documented US gold
  discovery** (Conrad Reed's 17-lb nugget, 1799, in Little Meadow Creek). A genuine US-public-land
  provenance tie for a named treasure item — and it is IN North Carolina, so it deserves an explicit
  look even though it is off the western anchor cluster.
- **Public land + 3-mi road:** YES. NC state historic site; recreational **gold panning** offered
  seasonally along Little Meadow Creek; trails; directly accessible by road.
- **Discriminators MET:** accessible public land; within 3 mi of road; **Little Meadow Creek = water
  at the site**; wooded Piedmont with oaks; NC Piedmont gets occasional winter snow; bear (marginal)
  + venomous-snake (copperhead) habitat plausible; not buried / not dangerous.
- **Discriminators FAILED:** **low rolling Piedmont — no real elevation, no high vantage onto lower
  country** (fails the Ch4 landform); it is a **managed historic site / interpretive park** (guided
  underground tour, museum, fee panning), so concealment of an open-hunt box is implausible on the
  developed core; and crucially it is **OFF-ZONE** — Cabarrus County (Midland) is ~50–60 mi
  SE/SSE of the Hiddenite–Statesville–Brushy-Mountains anchor cluster, i.e., it STRETCHES rather than
  tightens the bounded NC zone.
- **Beats NC zone?** NO — and it does not even reinforce the *western* cluster; it pulls SE into the
  central Piedmont. It keeps the story "in NC" thematically (US gold = NC) but adds no pinpoint and no
  vantage, and sits outside the four-anchor box. Logged honestly so a later agent does not over-index
  on "Reed Gold Mine is NC public land" — it is, but it is the wrong part of NC and fails the terrain
  discriminators.
- **Fit score:** 34 (highest here only because it is at least in-state with water + woods, but it
  fails vantage and is off-zone).
- **Sources:** en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reed_Gold_Mine; nchistoricsites.org Reed Gold Mine; shipwreck-
  backitems cluster cross-ref (verified this run).

## Bottom line (honesty gate)

**No non-NC provenance candidate beats the NC western-Piedmont/Brushy-Mountains bounded zone, and
provenance does not itself produce a pinpoint anywhere.** Across all six clusters and ~30 items, the
ENTIRE item set yields only TWO non-NC qualifying public-land ties (Crater of Diamonds AR, Walden
Pond MA) plus one off-zone NC site (Reed Gold Mine). All three:
1. **fail the load-bearing Ch4 elevation/high-vantage discriminator** (plowed field / kettle-pond
   basin / low rolling Piedmont — none "opens onto lower country");
2. **fail concealability** (managed tourist field / heavily-visited literary shrine / interpretive
   historic park); and
3. **have no biographical pull** toward the western NC cluster (AR and MA are out of state; Reed is
   off-zone SE-central Piedmont).

This is the expected, structural result: by the project's own rules, foreign item ORIGINS are
decoy/context and US provenance counts only on SPECIFIC ACCESSIBLE PUBLIC LAND — and almost every
item's concrete US node is private, urban, or foreign. The few that survive are scattered and
terrain-mismatched. Meanwhile the strongest item threads that DO point at NC (the two emeralds, the
Jordan/UNC link, the Alexander name-echoes) all route through PRIVATE mines / URBAN campuses /
name-puns — i.e., they *thematically reinforce* the existing Hiddenite/Statesville/Chapel-Hill
anchors but add ZERO independent public-land pinpoint.

**Verdict consistent with the engine's REGION-SPARSE finding:** item provenance is a weak,
decoy-laden locator. It neither dislodges NC #1 nor manufactures an X. The pinpointing must come
from the region-agnostic terrain/access discriminators applied INSIDE the already-bounded NC zone —
where the watered, oaked, elevation-with-vantage, snow-getting foothills are a materially better
discriminator match than any non-NC provenance spot above.
