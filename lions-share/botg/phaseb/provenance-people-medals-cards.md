# Phase B — Item-Provenance-to-Ground: People / Medals / Cards (NC-UNCONSTRAINED)

Agent cluster: people-medals-cards. Task: trace each item's real-world US geography
to a SPECIFIC, ACCESSIBLE US PUBLIC-LAND site that could function as a "clue by
association" to a hide. Hard bar: must be accessible public land within ~3 mi of a
road; a private collection, private/hazardous source, or an urban museum BUILDING
does NOT qualify (context only). Honest default = NO qualifying tie.

Method note: the book (per CLAUDE.md chapter table) does not, in the public record,
name the specific athletes behind the two Olympic medals, and the Earhart "autograph"
and Jordan rookie card are mass-produced/portable objects with no intrinsic single
geography. External searches (WebSearch, May 2026) did not surface athlete names for
the medals. So provenance below is traced to the most defensible real-world geography
for each object class and tested against the public-land bar.

## 1. Amelia Earhart autograph (Ch 7, "Explore More")

**KEY PROVENANCE (confirmed).** Per JCB's own account (ScreenRant interview), the
autograph he bought at auction was on a PHOTO that had been mislabeled; on research it
shows Earhart's landing at **Londonderry / Derry, IRELAND** — her 1932 solo
transatlantic landing (Ballyarnett field, now the Amelia Earhart Centre & Wildlife
Sanctuary, Northern Ireland). That is the flight "that made her an icon." So the
artifact's defining geography is ABROAD (Ireland) = DECOY/context by the rules, not a
US hide site. This is the single most important fact for this item: it points overseas.

**Geography of the object class.** An autograph is a portable signed artifact with no
fixed location; its "geography" is biographical — Amelia Earhart's life sites:
- **Atchison, Kansas** — birthplace. Cluster of memorials: Amelia Earhart Birthplace
  Museum (a private/foundation house museum, on a bluff over the Missouri River), the
  Amelia Earhart Memorial Bridge (a highway bridge), the "Amelia Earhart Earthwork"
  (a 1-acre crop-art portrait, viewable from the air/an overlook), and the
  International Forest of Friendship (a living memorial in Warnock Lake park, Atchison).
- **Pacific Ocean (Howland Island vicinity)** — disappearance, July 1937. Not US
  accessible land; irrelevant as a hide site.
- **North Carolina** — only thin, unverified ties (period autogiro touring;
  possible airfield visits). No confirmed Earhart site on NC public land surfaced.

**Lands on accessible US public land usable as a hide?** NO for a hide. The defining
geography is Ireland (decoy). The fallback US biographical sites (Atchison KS) are
real and partly public (Forest of Friendship sits in Warnock Lake city park; the
bridge/earthwork are public), but (a) they are in KANSAS, not the NC zone, and (b)
they are developed civic memorials, not the wild-but-accessible woodland the
discriminator stack describes (water + oaks + sit-on rock + high vantage + winter
snow + bear/snake habitat). Thin, unverified NC ties exist (Earhart visited Southern
Pines NC and Raleigh Municipal Airport in 1931) but no confirmed Earhart site on NC
public land — and a 1931 visitor stop is not a hide tie. Context only.

**Distinctiveness:** Low for our purposes. The autograph's geography is generic
(Kansas birthplace / Pacific loss); neither is a wilderness public-land hide.

**Reinforces NC zone?** NO. The artifact's own geography points to IRELAND (decoy);
its US biographical anchor is KANSAS (Atchison) — both outside the four-anchor NC zone.
The chapter subtitle "Explore More" reads as a generic exhortation, not a place clue.
Treat Earhart geography as decoy/context.

Sources: ScreenRant JCB interview (autographed photo = Londonderry/Derry Ireland
landing, mislabeled at auction); VisitDerry / Amelia Earhart Centre & Wildlife
Sanctuary (Ballyarnett, N. Ireland landing site); Amelia Earhart Birthplace Museum /
Atchison memorial cluster (Warnock Lake / Intl Forest of Friendship); DigitalNC
(Earhart visited Southern Pines NC, Nov 1931) & WRAL (Raleigh airport 1931) — thin,
non-hide NC visitor ties; general bio (b. Atchison KS 1897; lost over Pacific 1937).

## 2. 1960 Rome Olympic gold medal (Ch 15, "Don't Give Up")

**ATHLETE CONFIRMED: Wilma Rudolph.** Multiple secondary sources (the "There's
Treasure Inside: The Unofficial Podcast" Ch15 episode framing, and search corroboration)
identify the Ch15 1960 Rome gold as **Wilma Rudolph's** (3x gold, track — 100m, 200m,
4x100m relay). The medal struck for Rome 1960 has its *manufacturing* origin abroad
(Italy) = DECOY/context. Its US geography is Rudolph's biography:
- **Clarksville / St. Bethlehem, TENNESSEE** — born St. Bethlehem (now part of
  Clarksville), raised Clarksville, Burt High School; discovered by TSU coach Ed Temple.
  Memorials: **Wilma Rudolph Statue on the Cumberland Riverwalk** (near Liberty Park),
  Wilma Rudolph Blvd, Wilma Rudolph Event Center, Tennessee State Univ (Nashville).
  ALL urban/civic — a riverwalk statue and a boulevard, NOT wild accessible public land.
- No North Carolina tie of any kind for Rudolph.

**Lands on accessible US public land usable as a hide?** NO. Rudolph's footprint is
entirely urban/civic Clarksville TN (riverwalk statue, boulevard, event center) — none
in NC, none wild accessible public land within 3 mi of a road matching the terrain
discriminators. The Italian mint origin is explicitly decoy.

**Distinctiveness:** Medium. The athlete is now pinned (Rudolph → Clarksville TN), but
that pins to a TENNESSEE civic riverwalk, not an NC wilderness hide.

**Reinforces NC zone?** NO. Points to Clarksville, TENNESSEE (NW TN, ~300+ mi W of the
western NC anchors). Subtitle "Don't Give Up" is motivational, not geographic.

**FALSE-POSITIVE logged (honesty).** A "Rudolph Trail (TR 716)" DOES exist on accessible
public land in Uwharrie National Forest, central NC (Montgomery/Randolph/Davidson Cos).
Tempting as a name-match to Wilma Rudolph — but verification shows it is a short (~0.3 mi)
OHV connector trail with NO connection to the Olympic sprinter; the name is a pure
coincidence. EXCLUDED — does not count as a provenance tie. (Noting it so the next agent
doesn't re-discover it and over-index. This is exactly the kind of name-coincidence the
honesty gate exists to catch.)

Sources: TII Unofficial Podcast Ch15 + search corroboration (Ch15 = Wilma Rudolph's
1960 Rome gold); Wilma Rudolph bio (b. St. Bethlehem TN; Clarksville; 3x Rome gold);
Clarksville Parks & Rec / VisitClarksville (Rudolph Statue on Cumberland Riverwalk,
Rudolph Blvd, Event Center).

## 3. 1996 Atlanta Olympic gold medal (Ch 16, "Defy Expectations")

**Geography of the object class.** Same logic: athlete not named in public record.
The one geography that is *fixed and US* is the **host city, Atlanta, Georgia**, and
specifically **Centennial Olympic Park** (a real, accessible public park in downtown
Atlanta built for the 1996 Games). That is the strongest concrete public-land tie in
this entire item set — BUT it is an urban downtown park (fountains, lawns, event
plaza), not the wild-but-accessible woodland the discriminator stack requires, and it
is in GEORGIA, not NC.

Plausible American 1996 Atlanta gold honorees (scenarios): Michael Johnson (track,
TX), Kerri Strug / Magnificent Seven gymnastics, Dot Richardson (softball), the US
"Dream Team III" basketball, etc. — none yield an NC wild-public-land memorial.

**Lands on accessible US public land usable as a hide?** Centennial Olympic Park is
genuine accessible public land within yards of roads — so technically it CLEARS the
"public + roadside" filters — but it FAILS the terrain/searchability discriminators
(no wild oaks/sit-rock/high vantage/snow-prone wilderness; it is a paved downtown
park where a buried/hidden box is implausible and it is outside the NC zone). Counts
as the only *real public-land* node in this cluster, but NOT as a credible hide and
NOT in NC. Logged as context, not a qualifying hide tie.

**Distinctiveness:** Medium (Atlanta/Centennial Park is a sharply specific place) —
but distinctiveness toward GEORGIA, away from NC.

**Reinforces NC zone?** NO — points to Atlanta, GA. (Atlanta is ~150–250 mi SW of
the western NC anchors; it stretches, not tightens, the zone.) Subtitle "Defy
Expectations" is motivational.

Sources: 1996 Atlanta Games host city; Centennial Olympic Park (downtown Atlanta,
public). US 1996 gold medalists (Michael Johnson, gymnastics, etc.).

## 4. Michael Jordan rookie card (Ch 13, "Be Like Mike")

**Geography of the object — two layers.**
- **The card itself (1986–87 Fleer #57):** manufactured by **Fleer, Philadelphia,
  Pennsylvania**. No NC geography; PA is outside the zone. Decoy-ish (manufacturing).
- **The subject, Michael Jordan — strong, genuine NORTH CAROLINA biography:**
  - **Wilmington, NC** (New Hanover County, coastal/SE NC): grew up there; Emsley A.
    Laney High School (famously cut from varsity). Coastal SE NC — far from the four
    western anchors.
  - **Chapel Hill, NC (UNC), Orange County:** played for UNC 1981–84; hit the 1982
    NCAA title-winning shot (as a freshman) under Dean Smith. THIS DIRECTLY MATCHES
    THE LEDGER'S CHAPEL HILL ANCHOR (Ch2/Ch13 = JCB's college). "Be Like Mike" +
    Jordan-at-UNC is the cleanest internal cross-reference in this cluster.

**Lands on accessible US public land usable as a hide?** NO. Jordan's NC footprint is
urban/campus: Laney HS (a working public school, not a search site), and UNC's campus
/ Dean Smith Center (a university building/campus — an "urban/institutional building,"
explicitly excluded). Neither is wild accessible public land within 3 mi of a road
matching the terrain discriminators. The Fleer card itself ties to Philadelphia PA.
So: NO qualifying public-land hide tie.

**Distinctiveness:** HIGH as an internal cross-reference (Jordan ↔ UNC Chapel Hill ↔
the Ch13 anchor), but it confirms a *region anchor already in the ledger*, not a new
ground site. It does not pinpoint.

**Reinforces NC zone?** PARTIAL/YES at the region level — it independently reinforces
the **Chapel Hill / Orange County** anchor (eastern outlier) via the "Be Like Mike" /
Jordan-UNC link, and adds Wilmington NC as an additional NC node. But it does NOT
favor the western Piedmont/Brushy Mountains cluster, and it produces NO accessible
wild public-land hide point. Net: reinforces NC *as a state/region* and specifically
the Chapel Hill anchor; does not pinpoint and does not strengthen the western cluster.

Sources: 1986–87 Fleer #57 (Fleer, Philadelphia PA); Jordan bio — raised Wilmington
NC, Laney HS; UNC Chapel Hill 1981–84, 1982 NCAA-winning shot under Dean Smith.
