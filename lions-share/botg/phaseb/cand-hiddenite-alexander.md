# Phase B — Discriminator Search: Hiddenite / Alexander County sub-area

**Agent:** discriminator-search (NC sub-area: hiddenite-alexander)
**Date:** 2026-05-29
**Frame:** The 4 ledger anchors bound a ZONE; they do not pinpoint. This pass searches the
Hiddenite/Alexander Co corner of that zone on its merits and asks, at SPECIFIC accessible-public-land
points, how many region-agnostic discriminators co-locate AT ONE SPOT. Honesty gate is in force —
"few/zero strong spots; still region-level" is a valid result.

---

## Land-inventory reality check (drives everything below)

Alexander County has almost no natural public land. Per repo research (`research/alexander_county.md`)
and live confirmation this pass:
- **NO** national forest, NO state park, NO state forest, NO NCWRC game land, NO TNC preserve in-county.
- County parks are otherwise ball fields / lakefront boat ramps (no terrain).
- The ONE significant natural public block is **Rocky Face Mountain Recreation Area** (318 ac, county
  park, off Rocky Face Church Rd, Hiddenite; ~35.95 N, 81.18 W).
- Brushy Mtn neighbors (Round Top 1,591 ft; the Brushy crest generally) are **private** — fail the
  hard filter. Round Top shares Rocky Face's rare soil complex but has no public access/trail.
- The emerging **Brushy Mountains State Natural Area** (~1,460 ac, Blue Ridge + Foothills
  Conservancy; Wilkes/Alexander/Iredell tripoint) is intended for transfer to NCWRC as game land but
  **public access is still PENDING** (2025 land was just acquired; transfer not finalized) — fails the
  Group Q "accessible public land" filter TODAY. Logged as a watch-item, not a current candidate.
- Just OUTSIDE Alexander Co but in the immediate Brushy family: **Rendezvous Mountain SP/State
  Forest** (Wilkes, ~30 mi NE) and **Hibriten Mountain** (Caldwell/Lenoir, W end of the Brushies).
  Hibriten's summit nature trail **crosses ~7 private properties** (USFS does not own the trail) —
  fails the public-land filter. Rendezvous is genuine public land but is ~30 mi off the Hiddenite
  anchor (drifts toward the Blue Ridge front; better handled by an escarpment agent).

**Net:** In the Hiddenite/Alexander sub-area proper, **Rocky Face Mountain is effectively the only
accessible-public-land block where the discriminators can even be tested.** Everything else is private
or pending. So this dossier evaluates specific SPOTS *within* Rocky Face, plus one out-of-county
backstop (Rendezvous).

## Discriminator scorecard — Rocky Face Mountain, by spot

### Spot A — Summit granite dome / Hollow Rock Trail upper loop (blaze ~315–320 picnic-table area)
The flat, bald granite face beside the true summit on the Hollow Rock (a.k.a. "Hollow Brook") Trail
upper loop, ~mile 1.6–2.0. Picnic tables sit ON the exposed dome (AllTrails blaze 315 and 320 are on
the dome; 319 just off it). Approx ~35.95 N, 81.18 W; ~1,800 ft.

| Discriminator | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Sit-on / look-behind ROCK | **MET (strong)** | Acres of bare exfoliating granite dome; "massive rock faces," flat slabs you sit on; named "Hollow Rock" formation + Hollow Rock camping site (no-fire) at/near the ridge — a literal hollow/overhang in the rock to "look behind." |
| Elevation / vista onto LOWER COUNTRY (Ch4 landform) | **MET (strong)** | 600 ft over the surrounding floor; on the climb, "views to the south of the rolling hills of the Yadkin Valley" (= lower country); summit "wide open views" NW to the Brushy peaks (Pores Knob 2,660), W to Grandfather on clear days. Textbook monadnock-edge-over-Piedmont vantage nearest the Hiddenite anchor. |
| Oaks / shady trees | **MET (moderate)** | Piedmont Monadnock Forest = oaks; Virginia pine, red cedar on outcrop margins (NHP). Dome itself is open; oak shade flanks it. |
| Blue + pink spring wildflowers | **MET (moderate)** | Low-Elevation Granitic Dome = flatrock community: Diamorpha/elf-orpine (red stems, pink-centered flowers) blooms early May; sandwort (white); bluets (blue) typical of these flatrocks; Keever's Onion (light pink-white, G2 endemic) blooms May here. Blue+pink both plausibly present in season. |
| Water at/in view ("magic in the water") | **WEAK / FAILED** | Multiple trail write-ups: NO lake/creek highlighted as visible from the summit. SW vista *may* include Rhodhiss Lake on the far horizon on a clear day — distant/conditional, not water AT the hide. Creeks (Rocky Face Branch, Beaverdam, Wallace) are at the BASE, not the summit rock. This is the spot's main weakness. |
| Open / dark sky | **PARTIAL** | Open sky: yes (bald dome). Dark: western Piedmont ~Bortle 4–5 (NC's darkest only reach ~3; Pilot Mtn ~4). Open, not notably dark. |
| Public land | **MET** | Alexander County recreation area, free, open 7 days. |
| Within ~3 mi of road | **MET** | Trailhead on Rocky Face Church Rd; summit ~1.5–2 mi walk. |
| Not buried / not dangerous / carryable / single-day | **MET** | No dig; non-technical (use Hollow Rock/Grindstone, not the Vertical-Mile slab); easy day hike. (Avoid the 100-ft quarry cliff edge & the 30° Vertical-Mile slab to stay inside "not dangerous.") |
| Snow in winter | **MET** | Piedmont foothills get winter snow. |
| Bear + venomous-snake habitat | **MET** | Documented bear + snake habitat (repo research). |

**Co-location count at Spot A:** ~9–10 of 12 MET (water WEAK/FAILED; dark-sky PARTIAL). This is the
single densest discriminator stack in the whole sub-area.

### Spot B — Grindstone Trail creek crossing (lower, wooded)
~0.5 mi up the Grindstone Trail there is a genuine **creek crossing** in oak/hardwood shade.

| Key discriminators | Status |
|---|---|
| Water at the spot | **MET** (actual creek crossing — the one place water IS at the spot) |
| Oaks / shade | **MET** |
| Sit-on rock | weak (no dome here; trailside rocks only) |
| Vista onto lower country (Ch4) | **FAILED** (in the woods, no view) |
| Spring wildflowers (blue+pink) | plausible (riparian) but not the flatrock display |
| Public + 3-mi + not buried/dangerous + snow + bear/snake | MET |

**Co-location at Spot B:** water+oak+access MET, but it **FAILS the defining Ch4 vantage** and the
sit-rock. It is the inverse of Spot A (has water, lacks the view). Neither spot has BOTH water AND the
vista together — the dome has the view but no water; the creek has water but no view.

### Spot C (out-of-county backstop) — Rendezvous Mountain SP/Forest, Wilkes (~30 mi NE)
Genuine public land, summit overlook with views to Grandfather/Table Rock/Hawksbill, fire tower, CCC
cabin, hardwood forest, picnic shelter; Amadahy waterfall trail (water present). Strong all-around
match (vista MET, oaks MET, water MET via waterfall trail, public+road MET, snow/bear/snake MET).
BUT it is ~30 mi NE of the Hiddenite anchor, drifting off the Alexander sub-area toward the Blue Ridge
front. Flagged for the escarpment/Wilkes agent; not scored as a Hiddenite-area pinpoint here.

## Honest assessment

- **Rocky Face Mountain Spot A (summit dome / Hollow Rock area)** is a genuinely strong
  discriminator co-location: sit-on/look-behind rock + Ch4 "view opens onto lower country" (S over
  the Yadkin Valley lowlands) + oaks + plausible blue/pink flatrock wildflowers + all hard filters,
  all at one accessible public spot ~2 mi from the road, directly adjacent to the Ch4 Hiddenite
  anchor. This is the best spot in the sub-area, full stop.
- **The recurring gap is WATER.** The "magic in the water" discriminator does NOT co-locate with the
  summit vantage. Water at Rocky Face lives at the base (creeks, quarry wetland/boardwalk) and on the
  Grindstone crossing — none of which has the vista. A distant, weather-dependent glimpse of Rhodhiss
  Lake from the SW dome is the only summit "water," and it's thin. So no single Rocky Face spot
  achieves the FULL Group-P stack.
- **Provenance adds nothing pinpointing here.** Per `provenance-gems-jewelry.md`, the emerald/Chivor
  and Carnegie-emerald threads re-converge on Hiddenite/Alexander THEMATICALLY, but every concrete
  node is a private pay-to-dig mine (Emerald Hollow / North American Emerald Mine) or an urban museum
  — ZERO qualifying public-land tie. The Carolina Emperor emerald (Hiddenite, 2009; Tiffany link)
  reinforces the region but yields no X. Provenance does not manufacture a point in this sub-area.
- **The land inventory is the ceiling.** With no NF/SP/game-land in-county and the Brushy Natural
  Area not yet open, the searchable surface is essentially Rocky Face's 318 ac. If the hide is in
  this sub-area, it is almost certainly on Rocky Face; the open question is *where on it*, and the
  best-supported reading is the summit dome / Hollow Rock formation area (Spot A).

**Bottom line:** ONE genuinely strong spot (Rocky Face summit dome / Hollow Rock, fit ~78), ONE
partial complement (Grindstone creek crossing, fit ~45 — has the water the dome lacks but fails the
vista), ONE out-of-area public backstop (Rendezvous, fit ~60 but ~30 mi off-anchor). No spot in the
sub-area co-locates the FULL discriminator stack because water and vista never meet at one point —
honest caveat, not a manufactured X. Region-level confidence in Hiddenite stays moderate; pinpoint
confidence is capped by the water gap and the thin public-land inventory.
