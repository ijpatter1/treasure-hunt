# Coherence Scrutiny — Lens: National Myth / Theme (Track: WIDE)

Scrutineer role: CHARITABLE coherence check, not adversarial. A leap is KEPT (even if
unproven) unless it is (a) internally incoherent/self-contradictory, (b) built on a CIPHER
(letter/number/syllable counting), or (c) breaks a HARD CONSTRAINT (private land,
dangerous, buried, not in US, contradicted by a known fact). For each KEPT leap:
story-strength {vivid / solid / thin} + what-would-CONFIRM (a thing to check, not a reason
to reject).

Format: **Leap N — verdict** / reason / story-strength / what-would-confirm

---

## Leap 1 — Lion's Share → Lions Head, Salisbury CT — **KEEP**

- **Reason:** Clean prize-name pun (Lion's Share → Lion's Head), no cipher, no arithmetic.
  Public AT/Mt. Riga State Park land, ~3 mi of Rt 41, walkable, real New England snow, no
  cliff scaling on the trail itself. CT is an empty single-box state on our board. Reuses
  the exact "place-name-as-flag" logic that produced the strongest NC anchor (Alexander).
  Internally consistent and constraint-clean.
- **Story-strength:** solid. (The wink is on-the-prize-itself, which is the most flag-like
  read available; docked from "vivid" only because lion/Lion-name echoes are common on the
  US map and this rests on the single pun without a second independent thread of its own —
  its convergence value comes from pairing with Leap 5, not from internal richness.)
- **What-would-confirm:** Check the AT/Lions Head trail shoulders and the Bald Peak
  junction for a hide spot that is on PUBLIC AT corridor land (not the private Mt. Riga
  inholdings) and genuinely within ~3 mi of Rt 41; verify the summit ledges aren't a
  scramble that trips the "no cliff/ledge" constraint. Look for any JCB textual nod to
  "head"/"crown"/"summit" or Aesop/lion framing in Ch5 or the postscript.

## Leap 2 — Nemean lion → Nemo, SD (Black Hills gold) — **KEEP**

- **Reason:** Associative chain Lion's Share → Nemean lion → Nemea → Nemo is clean
  wordplay, NOT a cipher (no letter/number counting). Lands on Black Hills NF — public,
  road-accessible, snow-getting. Double myth+gold resonance (Ch12 nugget). Internally
  coherent; no constraint break (keep on NF, not on any private mining parcel).
- **Story-strength:** thin. (Nemea → Nemo is a homophone-ish leap across a translation of
  Greek place to a tiny SD hamlet; the chain is clean but stretched, and the
  myth-AND-gold pull is thematic, not pinpointing. No second independent read converges on
  Nemo specifically.)
- **What-would-confirm:** A second, independent thread landing in the Black Hills (e.g., a
  Ch12 gold-rush detail or a Carnegie/Homestake tie) would lift this from thematic to
  convergent. Verify a specific Black Hills NF trail near Nemo/Boxelder Creek that is
  public + ~3 mi of road + not a private mining claim.

## Leap 3 — 1960 medal → Olympic Valley (Squaw Valley) CA — **KEEP**

- **Reason:** Self-aware caveat already in the leap correctly flags that Ch15's medal is
  1960 ROME (summer, Wilma Rudolph), and the 1960 WINTER games at Squaw Valley share only
  the year. That makes it a year-tag pun, NOT a venue match — but a year-tag pun is still
  association, not a cipher, and it is not internally self-contradictory once stated
  honestly (the leap states its own weakness rather than hiding it). Constraints clean
  (Tahoe NF, snow, road, walkable). KEEP, but explicitly downgraded.
- **Story-strength:** thin. (The bridge is a single shared digit-string "1960" pointing at
  a DIFFERENT Olympics than the book's item; the leap itself names the stronger rival read
  — Wilma Rudolph / Clarksville TN. Honesty preserved, but the CA landing is weak.)
- **What-would-confirm:** Watch for a Tennessee/Clarksville convergence (Rudolph's
  hometown, the medal's TRUE story) — if a second thread lands there, this CA read should
  yield to it. For the CA read itself, look for any "1960"/"Olympic"/"valley" textual nod
  in Ch15 beyond the year.

## Leap 4 — 1996 medal → Stone Mountain GA — **KEEP (flagged)**

- **Reason:** "Hide near the story" + actual 1996 venue is a coherent pattern read, not a
  cipher. BUT the leap self-reports that metro Atlanta rarely gets winter snow, which
  soft-fails the "gets winter snow" HARD CONSTRAINT. Per charitable rules a *soft* fail
  isn't an automatic CUT, and the leap supplies its own rescue (push to north-GA higher
  country — Brasstown Bald / Blue Ridge GA — which does snow). KEEP only in that rescued
  form; the literal Stone Mountain pin is constraint-failing on its own.
- **Story-strength:** thin. (The venue match is real and clean, but the snow constraint
  forces the hide AWAY from the actual landmark, dissolving the "near the story"
  precision that motivated the leap. Survives as a north-GA regional maybe, not a pin.)
- **What-would-confirm:** A second thread landing on Blue Ridge GA / Brasstown Bald (the
  snowy rescue zone), so the leap doesn't rely on relocating away from its own anchor.
  Confirm whether JCB ever treats venue-proximity as a rule (test jointly with Leap 3).

## Leap 5 — Thoreau's Fire + Tiffany's Furnace → Walden/Concord MA — **KEEP**

- **Reason:** The book HANDS us Thoreau + fire as a matched pair (Ch14), and Thoreau
  literally set the Concord woods on fire in 1844 — the "fire" wink is exact, not invented.
  Profiles confirm Emerson & Thoreau were JCB's actual favorite authors and he urges
  finders to journal — author-PERSONAL, not generic. Public state reservation, road
  (Rt 126 / Rt 2), walkable, New England snow, NHL. No cipher, no constraint break (camping
  ban irrelevant to a day-find; busy pond core handled by pushing to Walden Woods /
  Estabrook fringe). Strongest single non-NC leap in this lens. Internally coherent.
- **Story-strength:** vivid. (Two independent textual hooks — Thoreau AND fire — both land
  on one named place, REINFORCED by an externally-confirmed author-biography tie. This is
  the rare leap where book-text, real history, and author psychology all point the same
  way without stretching.)
- **What-would-confirm:** Identify the actual 1844 burn site / Fairhaven Bay woods west of
  the pond and confirm a public, ~3-mi-of-road, walkable spot there (off the swimming
  beach). Look for any Ch14 or postscript nod to "Walden," "Concord," "journal," or 1844.
  A second Ch7 (Amelia "Explore More") transcendentalist tie would strengthen the
  New-England cluster.

## Leap 6 — Carnegie + "Science of Giving" → Laurel Highlands PA — **KEEP**

- **Reason:** "The Science of Giving" = Carnegie's *Gospel of Wealth*; Pittsburgh's Laurel
  Highlands are the geographic body of that myth. Public land (Ohiopyle / Laurel Ridge SP),
  road-accessible, snow-getting Allegheny plateau, walkable. No cipher; chain is thematic
  association from a real chapter subject. PA empty single-box state. Coherent, constraint-
  clean.
- **Story-strength:** thin. (Carnegie → Pittsburgh → Laurel Highlands is a broad regional
  association, not a pin; "Carnegie" is stamped widely on the US map and the leap itself
  wavers between Carnegie PA the town and the Laurel Highlands the region. No second
  independent read converges here.)
- **What-would-confirm:** A specific spot tie (e.g., a Carnegie-named feature, library, or
  Frick/steel landmark on or adjacent to public Laurel Highlands land) plus any Ch18
  textual geography. A second thread (Washington Ch17 colonial axis) landing in SW PA /
  Fort Necessity area would add convergence.

## Leap 7 — Washington's Jelly Glass + "Share Your Story" → Sky Meadows / VA Blue Ridge — **KEEP**

- **Reason:** Washington literally surveyed the northern Virginia Blue Ridge front as a

  Meadows SP is public, on the AT, road-accessible, snow-getting Blue Ridge, walkable.

## Leap 8 — Moon Rocks & Meteors → Flagstaff / San Francisco Peaks AZ — **KEEP**

- **Reason:** "The Next Frontier" = space; Flagstaff is the LITERAL Apollo lunar-geology

  road-accessible, snows heavily. The leap correctly EXCLUDES privately-owned Meteor Crater

- **Story-strength:** solid. (The Flagstaff-as-Moon-training-ground tie is a real,

  stronger than a mere name-echo. Docked from vivid only because no second independent
  thread converges on AZ and the San Francisco Peaks are a large area, not a pin.)
- **What-would-confirm:** A specific Coconino NF / lower San Francisco Peaks trail that is
  public + ~3 mi of road + walkable + not on the sacred-summit zone that might be
  access-restricted. Any Ch19 textual nod to Flagstaff, Lowell, Apollo training, or
  "Arizona." Confirm the peaks' upper reaches aren't a dangerous scramble.

## Leap 9 — Picasso's Pendant "A Love Story" → Heart Mountain WY — **KEEP**

- **Reason:** "A Love Story" → heart → Heart Mountain is a direct, cipher-free placename

  is a nice coherence touch. WY empty single-box state, snowy. No constraint break if kept

- **Story-strength:** thin. (The leap wavers between Heart Mountain and Sweetwater country

- **What-would-confirm:** Resolve Heart Mtn's public-access status (Heart Mountain Ranch

  not, this trips the public-land constraint and would move toward CUT). A second thread
  (gem/jewel Ch chord) landing on a WY heart/love feature would lift it.

## Leap 10 — Lion's Share/Hercules → Hercules, CA — **KEEP (flagged)**

- **Reason:** Direct name match to the Hercules/Nemean-lion myth underlying "Lion's Share,"
  plus a genuine triple-wink (Hercules the lion-slayer + the town's real explosives/powder-
  works "furnace/fire" history + CA gold-country state). Not a cipher. BUT the leap self-
  reports Bay Area lowlands essentially DON'T get winter snow — soft-fails the snow HARD
  CONSTRAINT. The leap's own rescue (push up Mt. Diablo's higher slopes, which can dust)
  is geographically far from Hercules CA, weakening the name-wink it's built on. KEEP as a
  name-wink lead only, terrain-flagged.
- **Story-strength:** thin. (The name + powder-works "furnace" wink is clever and the
  Hercules myth is the hunt's own root, but the snow rescue relocates AWAY from the named
  town to Mt. Diablo, severing the wink from the terrain. Survives the lion/Hercules
  geographic test only by tilting that test toward CT, per the leap's own note.)
- **What-would-confirm:** Whether ANY East Bay public park near Hercules gets reliable
  winter snow (almost certainly no) — if confirmed snowless at all walkable elevations,
  this hardens toward CUT. The convergence value is mainly diagnostic: it TESTS whether the
  lion/Hercules wink is geographic at all, and (failing snow in CA) it pushes that wink
  toward Connecticut/Leap 1.

## Leap 11 — Antiquities of "Alexander" → Little Egypt / Shawnee NF IL — **KEEP**

- **Reason:** Cipher-free associative chain: Alexander → Alexandria → Egypt → America's
  literal "Little Egypt" (Thebes, Karnak, Cairo IL) in Shawnee NF, where Garden of the Gods
  adds mythic-naming bonus. Honors JCB's own confirmed guidance "look beyond ancient myths
  and consider more." Public NF, road-accessible, gets winter snow, walkable. IL empty
  single-box state. Notably, this offers itself as the Ch9 confirmation-bias COUNTER to the
  NC "Alexander" read — a coherent self-check, not a contradiction. No constraint break.
- **Story-strength:** solid. (Multiple reinforcing placenames — Thebes, Karnak, Cairo,
  Garden of the Gods — clustered in ONE public forest is a genuine mini-convergence of
  Egypt/antiquity echoes, more than a single name-pun. Its honest framing as the possible
  confirmation-bias trap warning against the NC reading is itself coherent and valuable.
  Docked from vivid because the chain's first hop, Alexander→Alexandria→Egypt, is the same
  myth-decoy JCB warns is a trap, and the cluster spans a large forest, not a pin.)
- **What-would-confirm:** A second independent thread landing in southern IL / Shawnee NF
  (e.g., a Ch3 puzzle-box/egg "Egypt" echo, or a chalice/antiquities tie) would convert
  this from a clever counter-read into a real rival to NC. Identify a specific Shawnee NF
  spot (Garden of the Gods / Bell Smith Springs) that is public + ~3 mi of road + walkable
  + not a dangerous bluff edge.

## SCRUTINEER SUMMARY

- **Verdict:** ALL 11 leaps KEEP. None is internally incoherent, none rests on a cipher,
  and none flatly breaks a hard constraint in a way the leap doesn't already handle. Two
  (Leap 4 Stone Mtn, Leap 10 Hercules CA) carry SOFT snow-constraint flags and survive only
  in their relocated/rescued forms; Leap 9 (Heart Mtn) carries an OPEN public-access
  question (Nature Conservancy land) that could harden toward CUT if foot access is barred.
- **Strongest kept (by story-strength):** Leap 5 (Walden/Concord MA) = vivid; the only leap
  where book-text + real history + confirmed author-biography converge without stretch.
- **Solid tier:** Leap 1 (Lions Head CT), Leap 8 (Flagstaff AZ), Leap 11 (Little Egypt IL).
- **Convergence note (charitable):** The NEW-ENGLAND cluster (Leap 1 + Leap 5) is the
  cleanest multi-leap convergence in this lens — two independent reads (lion-pun + Thoreau-
  fire) in the CT/MA corner, different states (allowed). Leap 11 (Little Egypt) is the most
  interesting INTERNAL convergence (4 Egypt placenames in one forest) and doubles as the
  honest confirmation-bias counterweight to the NC "Alexander" anchor — worth holding as the
  designated devil's-advocate against home-ground bias.
- **Diagnostic value:** The lion/Hercules test (Leap 1 vs Leap 10) and the Olympic-venue
  test (Leap 3 vs Leap 4) are useful EVEN in failure: snow eliminates the CA members of
  both, tilting the lion-wink toward CT and leaving the Olympic-venue "rule" unproven.
