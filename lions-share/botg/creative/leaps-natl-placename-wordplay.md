## Leap 1 — "Hiddenite": the book's own title rendered as a town name

**Leap:** The single loudest placename wink in the entire US for a book called *There's
Treasure Inside* is **Hiddenite, NC** — a town named for a HIDDEN green gem ("hidden-ite"),
where the author personally learned to dig treasure (Emerald Hollow Mine). A treasure author
who grew up beside a town literally named *Hidden*-ite is not coincidence; it is the thesis
sentence of his life turned into a place on the map. Read it literally, per his own "don't
ignore the obvious."

**Points to:** Hiddenite community, Alexander County, NC, and the publicly accessible woods /
state game land in the Hiddenite–Stony Point band around it (Emerald Hollow itself is private
pay-to-dig, so the BOX sits on nearby PUBLIC land). ~35.9 N, 81.1 W.

**Why it coheres:** Most autobiographical placename in the book's geography; puns exactly on the
title; he confirmed Alexander/Hiddenite-adjacent winks elsewhere (Ch4, Ch21); satisfies "the
final step feels obvious in hindsight."

**Resonates with:** Ch4 "Chivor Emerald / Blaze the Path" (names the Hiddenite mine outright);
Ch21 "Antiquities of ALEXANDER" ↔ Alexander County; convergence engine's #1 anchor; red-dirt
gem-digging childhood loop.

beacon.)

**Points to:** Hibriten Mountain, Caldwell County, NC (Lenoir); public-access summit, road
nearly to the top, big view. ~35.93 N, 81.50 W.

**Why it coheres:** In the western-NC escarpment corridor; the name literally means "HIGH"
(matches "how high you ever want to be"); the star/cross beacon marries his Baptist-pastor roots
(Ch6, Ch18) with the poem's starlight/sky imagery; a bright high beacon = his "where you stand
changes everything" vantage theme.

**Resonates with:** Faith roots (Ch6, Ch18); Joy's Serenade "flame of starlight," "the sky

scattering nationally. Three corridor nodes emerge, each hit by MULTIPLE independent winks:

  Spruce Pine gem towns — coincides with the box-FORGING anchor.

The genuinely-national winks (Joy, Treasure/Gold, Pisgah, Pilot/Hanging Rock) are weaker on
convergence: real puns, but they don't STACK the way the corridor nodes do. Per the pass
discipline (convergence > cleverness), the corridor nodes outrank the standalone national puns.
