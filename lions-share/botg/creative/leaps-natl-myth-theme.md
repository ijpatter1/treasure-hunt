## Leap 1 — The literal "Lion's Share" as a place, not a metaphor

- **Leap:** JCB named the *biggest* box "the Lion's Share." We've read that purely as an idiom ("the largest part," from Aesop's lion). But this is an author who turns words into flags (ALEXANDER → Alexander Co.). Read the wink literally: the largest box sits at a LION-named place. The richest US lion-name on protected, snow-getting, road-accessible mountain land is **Lions Head (Lion's Head), Salisbury, Connecticut** — a bare quartzite summit on the Appalachian Trail in the Taconics, state forest land, short walk from Rt 41.
- **Points to:** Lion's Head summit / Mt. Riga State Park area, Salisbury, NW Connecticut (Litchfield County). Sub-spot: the open ledges and the AT/Bald Peak junction below the true summit.
- **Why it coheres:** A "Lion's Share" literally hidden at a "Lion's Head" is exactly the kind of pun-on-the-prize JCB plays. It's public AT land, ~3 mi of a road, no cliff scaling on the trail, gets real New England snow, and CT has no other obvious box anchor (one box per state → CT is "open"). The lion = the largest box reuses the same naming logic that produced the strongest NC anchor.
- **Resonates with:** Aesop/"lion's share" framing of the prize itself; Thoreau/New-England intellectual axis (Leap 5); the recurring author habit of place-names-as-flags (Alexander, Hiddenite).

## Leap 2 — Nemean Lion → "Nemo" in gold country

- **Leap:** The original Lion's Share is the *Nemean* lion (Hercules' first labor). Hercules' reward/share-of-the-spoils is the lion's pelt. Spin the myth into wordplay: the Nemean lion's home is **Nemea**; the closest US echo is **Nemo, South Dakota**, tucked in the Black Hills — and the Black Hills are one of America's great GOLD regions (Homestake, the 1870s gold rush), which maps onto the book's gold-rush nugget (Ch12) and Carnegie/wealth motifs.
- **Points to:** Nemo, SD area, Black Hills National Forest (Lawrence County) — forest land along Boxelder Creek; or more broadly the Black Hills NF gold-country trails near Lead/Deadwood.
- **Why it coheres:** "Lion's Share" → Nemean lion → Nemea → Nemo is a clean associative chain (no cipher). Lands on legal public forest, road-accessible, snow-getting Black Hills. The Black Hills double as MYTH (sacred Lakota land, Mt. Rushmore) and TREASURE (gold) — two of the book's loudest chords at once.
- **Resonates with:** Massive Gold Rush Nugget (Ch12, "Make Good Choices"); Antiquities of Alexander/myth-as-decoy (Ch21); the meteor/moon "next frontier" cosmic-American-West feel (Ch19).

## Leap 3 — The 1960 Olympic gold medal points to the 1960 Olympic SITE

- **Leap:** Ch15 is a *1960 Olympic gold medal*. The most on-the-nose mythic-American place for it is the place the 1960 Games actually happened: **Olympic Valley (formerly Squaw Valley), Lake Tahoe, California** — Sierra Nevada, Tahoe National Forest, heavy snow, road-accessible. JCB hides items "near their story." The medal's story is literally a California mountain.
- **Points to:** Olympic Valley / Palisades Tahoe & adjacent Tahoe National Forest, Placer County, CA (off CA-89). Sub-spot: the Granite Chief Wilderness boundary / Shirley Canyon trail shoulders, or the meadow rim of the old Olympic site.
- **Why it coheres:** It satisfies snow + public land + ~3 mi of road, and the YEAR is the literal setting of a named book item. Placer County is also historic Gold-Rush "gold country," doubling the resonance. CA is a single-box state with no competing anchor in our work — wide-open. PROFILE-CHECK: JCB hides "near the story" and likes serendipitous doubling, supporting the read.
- **Caveat (be honest):** Ch15's medal is the **1960 ROME (Summer) Olympics** — Wilma Rudolph, his stated favorite story; the 1960 *Winter* Games at Squaw Valley are a DIFFERENT event sharing only the year "1960." So this leap rests on a year-tag pun, not a venue match (unlike Leap 4). It is admitted because the read is clean and CA is empty on our board, but it is WEAKER than I first framed it — the medal's true "story" is Wilma Rudolph + Rome + Clarksville TN (her hometown), not California. Watch for a Tennessee/Clarksville convergence as the rival read.
- **Resonates with:** 1960 Rome Olympic Gold Medal (Ch15, "Don't Give Up"); Gold Rush nugget (Ch12) and the California gold-country thread; the "1960" year-tag is the only bridge to Squaw Valley.

## Leap 5 — Thoreau's Fire & Tiffany's Furnace → Walden / Concord, Massachusetts

- **Leap:** Ch14 ("Fail Forward") literally pairs *Thoreau's fire* with *Tiffany's furnace*. Thoreau's story is rooted in one mythic American spot: **Walden Pond, Concord, MA** — and Thoreau famously *accidentally set the Concord woods on fire* in 1844 (the "fire" wink is exact). JCB's "fail forward" subtitle fits Thoreau's literal failure-by-fire that he later transmuted.
- **Points to:** Walden Pond State Reservation / Estabrook Woods / Fairhaven Bay area, Concord, MA. Sub-spot: the woodland near the actual 1844 burn site west of the pond, or the Walden Woods trails off Rt 126.
- **Why it coheres:** Public state reservation (335 acres), adjacent to Rt 126 / Rt 2, walkable, snow-getting New England, National Historic Landmark — exactly the welcoming-public-park profile JCB favors. The book hands us Thoreau + fire as a matched pair; Walden is the single most coherent landing point for that pair. PROFILE-CHECK (strong): the profiles confirm **Emerson & Thoreau were JCB's actual high-school favorite authors** and he reveres Thoreau's journals and urges finders to "journal your adventures" — so the Thoreau thread is PERSONAL to him, not generic decoration. This materially upgrades the leap. CAVEAT: overnight camping prohibited (irrelevant to a day-find); the pond core is busy/swum, so any hide would sit in the quieter Walden Woods / Estabrook fringe, not the swimming beach. MA is an open single-box state.
- **Resonates with:** Tiffany's Furnace & Thoreau's Fire (Ch14); Amelia's Autograph "Explore More" (Ch7, New-England/transcendentalist self-reliance ethos); the Lion's Head CT leap (Leap 1) forms a NEW-ENGLAND mini-cluster — two independent creative reads (lion-name + Thoreau) both land in the CT/MA corner.
