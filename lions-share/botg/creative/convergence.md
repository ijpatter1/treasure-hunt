# Creative Pass — Convergence Analysis

**Agent role:** Convergence — final synthesis of the creative / intuitive / speculative pass.

**Discipline:** A *cluster* is a place/area where independent creative leaps from **DISTINCT lenses** land together. One clever read = a maybe. Multiple independent lenses on the same small area = a lead. Weight = (number of distinct lenses) × (story-coherence), NOT provability. The signal is *different lenses* (wordplay + myth + author-psych + terrain-intuition + item-metaphor) closing on the same ground — that is the discipline against mush.

**Inputs consumed (all read in full):** the 9 kept-leap files and their 9 matching scrutiny files —
NC track: `placename-wordplay` (15 leaps), `myth-theme` (12), `author-psych` (13), `terrain-intuition` (15), `item-metaphor` (13);
National track: `placename-wordplay` (12), `myth-theme` (11), `author-psych` (11), `faith-leap` (13) — plus `profile-repo.md` / `profile-web.md`.
**Total kept leaps across the pass: 115; cuts: effectively 0** (only one sub-target, Hibriten Mtn, was cut on a verified legally-closed-private-land fact). Everything below is a **HYPOTHESIS TO TEST**, never asserted as truth.

**Counting convention:** the nine lens-files are the nine lenses. A cluster's convergence count = how many of the nine *independently* put a leap on that ground. Where the same author-home-origin drives several leaps, the scrutineers flagged it; I down-weight shared-origin stacking and say so per cluster.
