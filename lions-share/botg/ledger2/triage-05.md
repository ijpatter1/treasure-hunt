Reviewer notes / methodology:
- Item-provenance for the treasure objects in these four chapters points OVERWHELMINGLY abroad or to off-site item-history places (Mount Vernon VA, Philadelphia, Colombia's Muzo Mine, Dunfermline Scotland, Western Sahara, Costa Rica, Australia, Tanzania, Asia/Russia). The hide is in the US; these are item-origin context, classified foreign-context (or, where a US item-history place, region-pointing-but-weak for the hide).
- The genuine region-pointing biographical anchors in this batch:
  (1) Ch18 p.161 — author's PARENTS started a church in STATESVILLE, NC; local Statesville service institutions. Distinct biographical anchor = western/Piedmont NC (Statesville / Iredell County). Strongest region-pointing in batch.
  (2) Ch18 p.164 — Mount Wilson Observatory "not far from my home, just to the NORTHEAST," i.e., author lives SW of Mount Wilson = greater Los Angeles / San Gabriel area, CA. Distinct biographical anchor.
  (3) Ch19 p.167 — author grew up in rural countryside with dark Milky Way skies; now lives "near a large city." Corroborates a rural childhood (consistent with NC) + current big-city residence (consistent with LA). Dark-sky = discriminator.
- "Statesville NC" and "rural childhood dark sky" are SEPARATELY-sourced facts that both fit a western/Piedmont NC childhood — counted as convergent biographical region-pointing, but the dark-sky line is a discriminator, not itself a place name.
- Mojave Desert (ch19 p.168 "walking a grid in the Mohave") is named as a generic meteorite-hunting locale, NOT the hide; weak region-pointing at most (US Southwest) and really discriminator/method (grid-search of flat desert).
- DISCRIMINATORS recur strongly across ch19/ch20: dark-sky / Milky Way visibility, hillside/elevation vantage, "where you stand determines what you see" perspective framing, flat open ground, rose-garden/several-acres landscape vista. These compare regions; they do not pick one.
- METHOD signals are dense in ch19/ch20: "perspective is worth considering when treasure hunting," "embracing new perspectives is the essential key to finding a treasure," "a change in perspective fills the gap," "document/journal your adventures," "five boxes." Hide-pattern/solving teachings.
- No cipher/letter-extraction moves detected. "Arthur C. Clark" printed without final 'e' (ch19 p.166) is a typo in the source, NOT a cipher — held as noise.
