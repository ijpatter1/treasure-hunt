NM, Garfield Co., southern Utah, on the 1879–80 Hole-in-the-Rock Mormon trail, off the
long unpaved Hole-in-the-Rock Road, on BLM public land. Geographically plausible as a
*type* of hide; but plausibility is not evidence.)

## 4. The case FOR / the case AGAINST

### Case FOR (honest best version)
- Dance Hall Rock is a real, dramatic, NRHP-listed **public-land** formation, **<=3 mi
  off a road**, on a famous historic trail — it cleanly satisfies the literal
  hard-filters (public, not buried, not a water crossing, no cliff-scaling).
- It nails the **dark-sky** discriminator and offers slickrock sit-rocks and open vistas.
- IF the poem token "Dancers" were ever proven a deliberate in-book geographic pointer
  (it is not — held undecoded), "Dance Hall Rock" would be a strikingly literal name
  match, and JCB is a literal placer.

### Case AGAINST (why it is almost certainly illusory)
- **Zero textual support.** The 937-signal ledger never names Utah or any
  Escalante/Hole-in-the-Rock feature, and no item is from Utah. The candidate exists
  ONLY as an externally seeded prior.
- **Pure lone-association.** The entire hope rests on ONE undecoded poem token
  ("Dancers") word-matching a place NAME ("Dance Hall Rock"). The rules explicitly hold
  "Dancers" multi-sense and forbid exactly this forced single-word placename move. No
  second independent source exists.
- **Biography points elsewhere — hard.** The valid, productive biography channel lights
  up NC (Hiddenite/Alexander, Bakersville/Mitchell, Statesville, Chapel Hill) and the
  LA/CA orbit. A genuine answer should illuminate biography; Utah is dark.
- **Selective discriminators fail.** Oaks, water/pike, blue-&-pink spring blooms, snowy
  winters, and black-bear habitat all fit montane competitors better than arid Escalante.
- **Convergence = 0.** By the method's own rule (rank on convergence of independent
  signals), Utah has none.
- **Prior fieldwork:** the project's boots-on-ground / pin history (MEMORY.md;
  `botg/assumptions.md`) is entirely **North Carolina** — Rocky Face Mtn / Alexander Co.
  (searched, FAILED) and a Rendezvous-Mtn/Wilkes review, with the live text-pass lead at
  Bakersville/Mitchell Co. There is **no record of any Utah boots-on-ground attempt** —
  Utah is neither corroborated nor field-refuted; it simply never accumulated the textual
  support that would justify a trip.
