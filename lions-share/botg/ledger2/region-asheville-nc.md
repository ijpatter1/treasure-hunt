## 4. The case FOR and the case AGAINST

### Case FOR (honest best argument)
1. Asheville is the only Buncombe-area placename JCB writes by name, and he writes it in the book's *strongest* geodetic sentence (a literal bearing + distance). A determined reader could argue JCB "had Asheville on his map" and that the metro's enormous public-land surroundings (Pisgah NF, Blue Ridge Parkway, Mt. Mitchell, DuPont nearby) fit every Group P/Q discriminator.
2. Asheville is the recognized "gateway" hub of the WNC mountains JCB clearly favors biographically; if one widened "near Asheville" to mean the broader mountain region, the terrain fit is excellent.

### Case AGAINST (why it is illusory)
1. **It is the textbook lone-association failure — and not even a true association.** The single mention is an *anchor for a vector that points elsewhere.* The sentence literally instructs the reader to go 40 mi NE, *past* Asheville, to Bakersville. Crediting Asheville is reading the milepost instead of the destination.
2. **Double-counting risk (explicit task warning).** The orchestrator brief flagged that Asheville sits inside the NC Blue Ridge gem-belt region already in the set. AVL-1's pointing value is *already booked* to Bakersville/Mitchell within GROUP A. Counting it again here would inflate the ledger by re-using one signal in two regions.
3. **Zero independent corroboration.** No biography (JCB's NC anchors are Iredell/Alexander/Orange counties, not Buncombe), no item-provenance, no museum/display, no poem token resolves to Asheville. Convergence requires ≥2 separately-sourced anchors; Asheville has 0.
4. **Generic terrain.** Every Group P/Q "fit" above is true of the entire southern Appalachians; it discriminates nothing in Asheville's favor over Bakersville, Hiddenite, or anywhere else in the belt.
5. **Mild access tension.** The most iconic Asheville-area public lands (Blue Ridge Parkway, Pisgah NF, Mt. Mitchell SP) are federal/state units; nothing in the ledger ties the hide to Buncombe specifically, and the "small town tucked away" mood of the source sentence fits Bakersville, not a metro of ~95,000.
6. **Prior boots-on-ground note:** Repository priors (memory + assumptions.md) place prior search effort at **Rocky Face Mountain, Alexander County (FAILED)** and the strongest text lead at **Bakersville/Mitchell County** — *neither is Asheville/Buncombe.* No pin or BOTG attempt was ever aimed at Asheville itself, because nothing in the corpus aims there. So Asheville is not "refuted" so much as "never actually indicated."
