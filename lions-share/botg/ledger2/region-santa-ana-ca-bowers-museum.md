## 4. The case FOR and the case AGAINST

### Case FOR Santa Ana / Bowers Museum (honest best argument)
- The Bowers Museum is **literally named in the book** (Ch1 p.23) — a concrete, real, verifiable US place, not an inference. The author chose to mention it.
- It anchors the chapter that contains the book's strongest "the book is a map / start, directions, destination" hide-method language (Ch1 p.23), so a reader could feel the venue is being spotlighted.
- It is a real public museum (Mike Scott's minerals genuinely were exhibited there), so the fact checks out and isn't a hallucination.

### Case AGAINST (why it is almost certainly illusory)
- **Lone association, single mention.** One signal, one chapter, never repeated, never reinforced by any other channel. This is the textbook anti-pattern the independence-grouping rule exists to catch.
- **Exhibition-provenance, not a hide pointer.** "Was displayed publicly in the Bowers Museum" tells you where a *third party's mineral collection* was *shown* — exactly parallel to the Getty "similar bracelet" and Loloma murals. The role taxonomy classes this as item/exhibition provenance (e), which describes object whereabouts, not the US hide.
- **The author softened the locator.** He wrote "near Los Angeles," not "in Santa Ana." He is using the museum as biographical color for Mike Scott, not flagging Santa Ana as a place of interest. Santa Ana is the analyst's geocode, not the author's emphasis.
- **Discriminator profile is actively hostile.** Urban, no dark sky, no snow, no sit-rock, not an outdoor nature setting — contradicts the book's recurring "outdoors with a loved one / sights, smells, sounds of nature" hide description.
- **No convergence partner.** Nothing else in 937 signals points here. There is no biography anchor, no second placename, no terrain cue specific to Orange County. Compare North Carolina, where three *distinct* facts (childhood gem mine, parents' church, college years) genuinely converge.
- **Prior search status:** No boots-on-ground or pin attempt has targeted Santa Ana (per MEMORY.md, real search effort was North Carolina: Rocky Face FAILED, Bakersville/Mitchell emergent). Santa Ana is therefore "unrefuted" only in the trivial sense that no one bothered to test a single-mention exhibition reference.
