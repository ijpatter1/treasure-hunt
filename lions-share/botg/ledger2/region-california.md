# Region Dossier: California

**Region scope:** Author residence (greater Los Angeles / San Gabriel orbit); Mount Wilson Observatory; California Gold Rush corridor (American River / Sutter's Mill / Coloma / El Dorado Co. / Sacramento); San Francisco Bay (incl. emergent sub-regions: SF Bay Area, Menlo Park, Treasure Island/Alcatraz, Santa Ana/Bowers Museum).

> **Source basis:** Built from the graded triage tables `botg/ledger2/triage-01.md` … `triage-07.md` (all signal rows read in full). Every quote below is lifted from those tables with its source-unit ID. California appears more often than almost any other state in the raw signal set — but the analysis below shows the appearances split sharply between (a) ITEM-PROVENANCE (where objects came from / are displayed — NOT the US hide) and (b) a small number of genuine AUTHOR-BIOGRAPHY anchors.

## 1. Pointing signals, organized by independence-group

Grouping rule applied strictly: signals whose geographic implication flows from the SAME underlying source = ONE point regardless of repetition. Distinct, separately-sourced anchors that converge = SEPARATE points. Biography COUNTS. Item-origin abroad/out-of-region and "where the object is displayed" = does NOT point the US hide here.

### Group CA-1 — Author's primary residence: greater Los Angeles / San Gabriel area (channel: BIOGRAPHY) — **the one real anchor**
- Ch12 p.114: *"I've now called California my home for over twenty years. It's a place I love."* (region-pointing, moderate)
- Ch18 p.164: Mount Wilson Observatory *"is located not far from my home, just to the northeast"* → author lives SW of Mount Wilson = greater LA / San Gabriel foothills (region-pointing, moderate). This line is doing two jobs: it confirms the LA-orbit residence AND geo-locates it relative to a specific landmark.
- Ch19 p.167: *"Seeing our galaxy is one of the things I still miss most, living near a large city"* (region-pointing, weak) — corroborates current big-city residence, consistent with LA.
- Ch1 p.19, Ch2 p.33, Ch3 p.40: scattered LA framing — son's treasure map on *"a street map of Los Angeles"*; Angkor *"the size of Los Angeles"*; *"After we returned... to Los Angeles."*
- **Independence note:** ALL of the above collapse to ONE underlying fact — *the author lives in the greater-LA area*. Multiple mentions of the same residence do NOT multiply the count. This is ONE biographical anchor. The Ch18 "northeast of Mount Wilson" line raises its *resolution* (it places the home in the San Gabriel-adjacent LA basin) but it is still the same single fact.
- **Quality:** MODERATE as an anchor's strength, but LOW as a *hide* locator. An author's home is the *authoring* locus, not necessarily the *hide* locus (cf. Fenn: lived Santa Fe, hid in WY/MT). Counts as 1 genuine biographical region-pointer.

## 4. The case FOR and the case AGAINST

### Case FOR California
- **Frequency:** California is named more than almost any other state across the raw signals (Gold Rush nugget chapter, author residence, Mount Wilson, Loloma murals, Apple, Getty/Bowers). On a naive tally it looks like a top region.
- **A real, dual-sourced-feeling residence anchor:** the author explicitly says CA is home for 20+ years AND geo-locates that home relative to Mount Wilson ("just to the northeast"). That is unusually concrete for a residence clue.
- **Mount Wilson** is literally named, is a genuine accessible public vista (road to summit, Angeles NF), and has a science/"next frontier" story (Ch18/Ch19) — a plausible evocative site IF the home were the hide region.
- **Excellent montane discriminator profile** (water, oaks, granite sit-rock, vista, superbloom blue+pink flowers, public land near roads, bears/snakes).

### Case AGAINST California (why it is likely illusory)
- **The frequency is an item-provenance mirage.** Strip out object-origin (Gold Rush nugget), exhibition sites (Loloma murals/Alcatraz, Bowers, Getty), and story-color (Apple/Jobs), and California's apparent dominance collapses to ONE biographical fact. The grouping rule is doing exactly its job here.
- **Author-home is the authoring locus, not the hide locus.** Heavily weighting the residence is the canonical Fenn error (Santa Fe home → Wyoming hide). The book's own How-To framing puts the *clues* in the book, not at the author's doorstep.
- **Mount Wilson is named in item-history, not pointed-to.** It locates the home; it is not independently flagged as a box site, and the "northeast of my home" framing arguably says the home (and its orbit) is the reference, not the summit.
- **Internal discriminator tension:** the residence sits in light-polluted, snow-free lowlands; the dark-sky and snowy-winter filters push any actual hide into mountains that have NO independent CA pointing signal.
- **"Treasure Island" is a name-coincidence trap** with zero literal support.
- **The competing region has TRUE multi-source convergence.** North Carolina is supported by THREE *distinct* biographical anchors that the grouping rule keeps separate: Hiddenite/Alexander childhood dig site (Ch4 p.48), parents' church in **Statesville/Iredell** (Ch18 p.161), and the Chapel Hill / UNC / "grew up in NC" thread (Ch2 p.28; Ch13 p.117) — PLUS the independently-sourced Seth Gould box-forging anchor in Bakersville/Mitchell (Ch2 p.37, Ch3 p.40, strongest single region-pointer in the book with a bearing-and-distance). California has nothing comparable: its anchors collapse to one.
- **Prior boots-on-ground / pin attempts:** Per repo MEMORY, physical search and the strongest converging leads have all been in **North Carolina** (Rocky Face — FAILED; Bakersville/Mitchell — active). **No California pin or BOTG attempt is on record** — so California is *unrefuted*, but it is also *unsupported by any convergence* that would justify a trip. Unrefuted-but-thin, not unrefuted-and-promising.

## 5. Honest convergence verdict

**THIN (biography-only; one anchor).**

California's large footprint in the signal set is real but is overwhelmingly **item-provenance** (Gold Rush nugget), **exhibition/museum display** (Loloma murals, Alcatraz, Bowers, Getty), and **story illustration** (Apple/Jobs) — none of which points a treasure box to California. After strict independence-grouping, exactly **ONE** distinct source genuinely points here: the author's greater-LA residence (Ch12 p.114), whose resolution is sharpened by the Mount Wilson "northeast of my home" aside (Ch18 p.164). That is a single biographical anchor, and an author-home is a weak hide-locator by nature. There is **no convergence of distinct, separately-sourced region-pointing signals on a single California hide** — unlike North Carolina, which shows three independent biographical anchors plus the Seth Gould forging anchor.

California should be retained as a low-priority, unrefuted possibility (the residence is real and the discriminator profile of its mountains is good), but it is NOT a convergence region and does not warrant a trip ahead of the NC convergence cluster.
