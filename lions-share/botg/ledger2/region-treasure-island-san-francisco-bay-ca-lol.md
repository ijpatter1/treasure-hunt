# Treasure Island, San Francisco Bay, CA (Loloma murals; Alcatraz <3mi) — Deep Dossier

> **Origin of this region:** Emergent from triage. The label was generated because (a) a Lion's Share item is provenanced to **Charles Loloma**, the Hopi master jeweler, who as a young man painted **murals at the 1939 Golden Gate International Exposition (GGIE) on Treasure Island**, and (b) Treasure Island sits **<3 miles from Alcatraz**, which superficially satisfies JCB's "within 3 miles of a road / reasonably accessible" hard-filter framing. This dossier exists to test whether that label survives the independence-grouping and literal-only rules. Spoiler-by-honesty: it does not survive as a genuine convergence — it is a **lone single-fact biographical detour off an item whose real vector is Arizona.**

## 1. Pointing Signals by Independence-Group

### Group TI-1 — Charles Loloma's Treasure Island murals + Alcatraz proximity (item-provenance, Ch8 Loloma chapter)
**Channel:** item-provenance (history of the Loloma jewelry pieces in the Lion's Share)

**Signals — the book DOES literally name this place (correcting any impression this is my own coinage):**
- **ch08 raw ledger, Ch8 p.79 (graded `placename`, MODERATE):** "Fred invited Charles to accompany him to San Francisco. **On Treasure Island in the San Francisco Bay**, Charles was one of three Native Americans to paint installations in the Indian Court of the island's Federal Building." (= the 1939 Golden Gate International Exposition.)
- **ch08 raw ledger, Ch8 p.79 (descriptive-geography, weak):** "Over half a million people came to Treasure Island to see them." (frames it as a visited, accessible public site)
- **ch08 raw ledger, Ch8 p.83 (bearing-distance, MODERATE):** "their chief Loma-hongvoma was sent to prison in **Alcatraz. Alcatraz stands less than three miles from Treasure Island** where Charles Loloma's murals later blazed."
- **triage-02 lines 103, 104, 128** mirror these and grade Treasure Island murals MODERATE, the half-million-visitors line WEAK, and the Alcatraz "<3 miles" line MODERATE — with the explicit note: *"'three miles' is item-history distance, NOT the <=3mi-to-road hide rule."*
- The Lion's Share physically contains Loloma pieces (Ch8 p.80 ironwood bracelet "now in our treasure"; Ch8 p.81 two Loloma stone rings — blue turquoise, purple sugilite). Loloma (1921–1991) was a Hopi master jeweler from **Hotevilla, Third Mesa, Arizona**.

**Geographic implication — read honestly:** The book genuinely, literally names Treasure Island and even volunteers a "less than three miles" distance, so this is NOT pure nominative determinism on the analyst's part — it is a real in-text placename + bearing-distance signal. BUT the PRIMARY and overwhelming vector of the Loloma provenance is **Hopi / Third Mesa, Arizona** (his birthplace, home, studio, ceremonial life, and the bulk of his chapter: born Badger clan 1921, Snake Priest, Bean Dance leader, "lived out his days on the Hopi reservation," "continued to live in Hotevilla," seven straight prizes at Scottsdale). Treasure Island is one early-career resume line inside an otherwise overwhelmingly Arizona-centric item history.

**Independence-grouping ruling:** Every Treasure-Island/Alcatraz signal descends from the SAME underlying source — the single Ch8 Loloma item-provenance thread. By the strict same-source rule they collapse to **ONE anchor**. Two cautions: (1) that one anchor's dominant meaning is **Arizona/Hopi**, where it is already counted; crediting it again here risks double-counting one source and inverting its center of gravity. (2) The "less than three miles" is explicitly the distance between two *historical* sites (Alcatraz prison ↔ the 1939 mural site), NOT a statement that the hide is within 3 miles of a road — so it does not actually satisfy the hard-filter it superficially rhymes with.

**Quality:** **MODERATE as a literal in-text placename, but WEAK→NULL as an independent Treasure-Island convergence point.** It is one source, already owned by Arizona, dressed up by the seductive coincidence that the place is literally named "Treasure" Island.

## 3. Discriminator Fit (region-agnostic terrain/flora/access — Group P/Q)

These are scored to compare regions, NOT to pick one. Treasure Island is a ~400-acre **man-made flat fill island** built in 1936–37 from Bay dredge spoil for the GGIE.

| Discriminator | Fit | Notes (inference marked *) |
|---|---|---|
| Water (the hide is near water) | YES, trivially | Surrounded by SF Bay. *But "near water" is satisfied by virtually any coastline; not discriminating, and JCB's "no dangerous water crossings" still allows water-adjacent.* |
| Oaks | NO | *Man-made fill island; landscaping is ornamental/planted, not natural oak woodland.* |
| Sit-rock / boulder | NO | *Engineered flat fill; no natural rock outcrops, no glacial boulders, no granite sit-rock.* |
| Elevation / vista | PARTIAL (borrowed) | Treasure Island itself is flat & near sea level; adjacent **Yerba Buena Island** is a natural hill with vistas. *Any Bay vantage gives a skyline view — generic to the whole Bay, not specific.* |
| Blue & pink spring wildflowers | NO (natural); maybe planted | *No native wildflower meadow on engineered fill. California natives (lupine=blue, clarkia/checkerbloom=pink) occur regionally but not as a wild meadow here.* |
| Dark sky | NO — strongly fails | Sits in the **core of one of the brightest light-domes in the US** (SF + Oakland skylines, Bay Bridge lights directly overhead). This is the antithesis of a dark-sky site. |
| <=3 mi to a road, public land | MIXED | Bay Bridge/road access is excellent. BUT much of the island is **active redevelopment / former Navy parcels** with restricted access, ongoing radiological/soil remediation history, and a non-"free-roam public wildland" character. Marginal at best. |
| Snowy winters | NO — fails hard | SF Bay maritime climate; effectively never snows. *If "snowy winters" is a real discriminator from Group P/Q, this site fails it outright.* |
| Bear / snake habitat | NO | *Urban fill island; no bear range, negligible snake habitat.* |

**Discriminator summary:** Treasure Island **fails most physical discriminators** that the ledger's terrain/flora/dark-sky signals call for (no oaks, no sit-rock, no dark sky, no snow, no bear/snake, no natural wildflower meadow). It passes only the trivial "near water" and the road-access geometry. This is a poor terrain fit for the kind of natural, walk-in, dark-sky-capable, oak/sit-rock hide the discriminators describe.

## 4. Case FOR and Case AGAINST

### The honest best case FOR
- **Item-provenance touch:** A real Lion's Share item (Loloma) has a documented, verifiable early-career link to this exact place (GGIE murals on Treasure Island, 1939). JCB does weave item histories into the puzzle, and item provenance IS a sanctioned clue channel.
- **Nominative resonance:** The place is literally named "**Treasure** Island," which is the kind of cute, on-the-nose wink a puzzle author *could* enjoy.
- **Access geometry is excellent:** road-connected via the Bay Bridge, urban, easy to reach — satisfies the "reasonably accessible, within 3 miles of a road" framing on paper.

### The case AGAINST (why this is illusory)
1. **Lone association / single-source.** Every pro-Treasure-Island thread reduces to ONE fact: the Loloma item. By the independence rule that is one point — and it is a point whose dominant, primary meaning is **Arizona/Hopi**, not the Bay. Treasure Island only appears if you chase the artist's least-central resume line.
2. **Biography-of-the-item, not of the author.** Unlike NC (Hiddenite mine, Chapel Hill, Statesville — multiple *distinct* JCB-life anchors), there is **zero JCB personal/biographical connection** to Treasure Island. The user's biography ruling does not help here, because no JCB biography points here.
3. **Nominative determinism is a known trap.** "Treasure Island" sounding apt is exactly the seductive-but-empty pattern the analysis is told to reject; it requires no literal in-book signal at all.
4. **Generic / failing terrain.** Man-made flat fill, brightest-possible light dome (no dark sky), no oaks, no sit-rock, no snow, no bear/snake, no natural wildflower meadow. It contradicts most discriminators.
5. **Access character is wrong despite road geometry.** "Not buried, public, accessible" is meant for natural public land; Treasure Island is an **active redevelopment / former Naval Station** with restricted parcels and remediation history — not a free-roam walk-in search site. Alcatraz (the <3mi anchor) is a controlled-ferry federal island — even less searchable.
6. **No prior boots-on-ground here**, so nothing has *refuted* it directly — but there is also nothing to refute, because there is no real convergence to test. (Prior BOTG effort and the project's leading leads are in **North Carolina (Rocky Face — failed; Bakersville lead)**, far from this region.)

### Cross-region double-count check
The single Loloma source is **already credited to Arizona/Hopi** (its primary vector) and is acknowledged-and-discounted in the **SF Bay Area** companion dossier (Group SF-1, graded WEAK). Counting it as a positive for Treasure Island would be a triple-use of one source. It must NOT be added to a convergence total here.

## 5. Honest Convergence Verdict

**SEEDED-PRIOR-ONLY / THIN (effectively null).**

Treasure Island is an emergent label produced by chasing a single item-provenance fact (Loloma) down to its least-relevant early-career detour, amplified by the nominative coincidence of the word "Treasure" and a hard-filter geometry note ("Alcatraz <3mi"). It has **no independent second source, no JCB biographical anchor, and fails most physical discriminators (dark sky, oaks, sit-rock, snow, bear/snake, natural terrain).** The one real source it leans on points primarily to **Arizona/Hopi** and is already counted there. This region should be **dropped from serious convergence ranking** and retained only as a documented dead-end so it is not re-discovered and re-litigated.
