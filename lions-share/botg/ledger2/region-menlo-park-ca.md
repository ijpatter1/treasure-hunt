## 4. The case FOR vs. the case AGAINST

### Case FOR (honest best argument)
- It is a **concrete, real, named US placename** stated unambiguously in the text ("a garage in Menlo Park, California") — literal, no cipher needed, which satisfies the literal-only rule.
- It sits inside the book's **most California-saturated chapter** (Ch1), which also name-checks the SF Bay Area, "Californians," LA, and Oregon — so *some* California intent in Ch1 is real, and the author does live in the LA orbit, establishing CA as a plausible travel/familiarity zone.
- "Garage" + "where it all began / roadmap to a destination" gives a faint thematic resonance with hidden-origin / start-of-the-map motifs.

### Case AGAINST (why it is almost certainly illusory)
- **Lone association.** Exactly ONE mention, never repeated, never revisited. The charter's anti-lone-association guard treats a single unrepeated placename inside a biographical anecdote as the weakest possible class of pointer.
- **Not the author's biography.** The ruling that "biography counts" applies to JCB's *own* life-geography (Hiddenite, Statesville, Chapel Hill, etc.). Menlo Park is the biography of *Jobs/Wozniak/the Homebrew Club* — a third party used illustratively. JCB has no known personal tie to Menlo Park. This is item/anecdote color, not author-pointing.
- **The chapter's real payload is METHOD, not place.** Ch1's repeated, strong-graded signals are all method ("the book is your map… a place to start, directions to follow, a destination," "have a roadmap," UV-reveal / hidden-in-plain-sight). The Apple story is a *vehicle* for the roadmap lesson; the garage's town is incidental.
- **Discriminator conflict.** Flat, light-polluted, snow-free, bear-poor city — actively contradicts dark-sky, snowy-winter, vista, and bear discriminators present elsewhere in the ledger.
- **Generic-terrain trap.** Any terrain fit (oaks, wildflowers, foothill vista) only appears by sliding miles away into ordinary Coast-Range open space, which is non-distinctive and unmoored from the actual clue.
- **No corroborating channel.** No treasure item originates, resides, or was made in Menlo Park; no poem token resolves here (and poem tokens are held undecoded anyway); no second chapter echoes it.
- **Competes with and is dominated by stronger CA sub-loci.** Even within the California cluster, LA (multiple independent mentions + author home), the SF Bay generic, and Treasure Island (a treasure-item/Loloma-mural tie + the Alcatraz/Hopi-chief thread) all out-point Menlo Park.
