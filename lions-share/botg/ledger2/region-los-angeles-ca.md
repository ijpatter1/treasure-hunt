## 4. The case FOR and the case AGAINST

### Case FOR (honest best argument)
1. **The home anchor is real and is the most-repeated geographic biography in the book.** "Los Angeles" recurs across Ch1, Ch2, Ch3, Ch18, Ch19; Ch18 even gives a *direction* ("not far from my home, just to the northeast" of Mount Wilson), pinning the residence to the LA/San-Gabriel side. Under the validated biography channel, that is legitimate pointing weight.
2. **Mount Wilson is a clean, accessible, physically-compliant target IF promoted.** Public land (Angeles NF), paved road + trail (≤3 mi trivially met), iconic vista, granite sit-rock, snow at elevation, bear/snake habitat — it satisfies every hard filter. It is literally NAMED in the text (now confirmed against raw ledger), unlike most candidate landmarks.
3. **A Fenn-style hunter sometimes hides within day-trip reach of home.** The San Gabriel front is < 1 hr from the author's home and easy to revisit/maintain a hide.

### Case AGAINST (why it is likely illusory)
1. **It is a single biographical anchor (count = 1).** Every LA mention reduces to "the author lives there." The triager and the California dossier both explicitly collapse these to ONE source. This is the canonical lone-association pattern the method warns against over-ranking. There is **no convergence of distinct, separately-sourced pointers** on an LA hide locus.
2. **Home ≠ hide, and the book's emotional geography points elsewhere.** The author's *affection* and *origin* are tied to the **rural childhood** (dark Milky Way skies, the Hiddenite/Alexander-Co dig, Chapel Hill). LA is where he *currently lives* and which he frames as a place he *misses the stars from* — i.e., a contrast to the kind of place treasure-imagery is lovingly attached to. Fenn hid away from his city home in wilderness he loved.
3. **The dark-sky discriminator actively cuts against LA.** Ch19's dark-sky language is nostalgia for the rural childhood, explicitly lost "living near a large city." Pointing the hide at the light-polluted basin contradicts the imagery.
4. **Mount Wilson is Carnegie-sourced, not an independent astronomy pointer.** It appears because Carnegie funded the observatory (Ch18 is the Carnegie-emerald chapter). It is a landmark-of-convenience for locating the author's home, not a second arrow. So LA does not get to count it twice.
5. **No genuine item-provenance support.** The Getty (Ch21) is a comparison museum for an Egyptian object — NOISE. All major items originate abroad/East-Coast/Hopi.
6. **Decisively outranked.** The NC Blue Ridge gem belt stacks **multiple distinct** anchors (Hiddenite/Alexander childhood mine [Ch.4], Bakersville/Mitchell-Co Seth-Gould forging w/ a strong bearing-and-distance "40 mi NE of Asheville" [Ch.3], Chapel Hill [Ch.2]) PLUS the rural-dark-sky childhood + the Ch4 vista-onto-plains discriminators. That is real 3+ point convergence. LA has 1.
7. **Prior search history:** project memory records a **failed Rocky Face NC** trip and an active **Bakersville/Mitchell-Co NC** lead; prior vision and main-book-text passes both pointed to **western/Piedmont NC**, never LA. LA is *not refuted* by boots-on-ground, but it has never been *promoted* by any independent pass — purely triage-emergent off one biographical fact.
