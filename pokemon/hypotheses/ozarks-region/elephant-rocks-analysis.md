## Location Details

### Rock Formations
- **Age:** 1.5 billion years old
- **Type:** Pink granite
- **Size:** Some over 600 tons, 20+ feet tall
- **"Dumbo"** (largest): 680 tons, 27 feet tall, 35 feet long
- **Formation type:** "Tor" - stack of spheroidally weathered granite

### Unique Features
- **"Fat Man's Squeeze"** - narrow gap between boulders
- **"The Maze"** - 100-foot section of scattered boulders
- **Braille Trail** - accessible 1-mile interpretive trail
- Historic quarry ruins

### Accessibility
- State park with road access
- Within 3 miles of road (meets safety requirement)
- Designated trails throughout
- Visitor-friendly climbing areas
