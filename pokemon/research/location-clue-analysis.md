## Executive Summary

The Pokemon chapter contains a fantastical narrative about meeting Pokemon characters while hiding a treasure box. Despite the whimsical framing, numerous concrete location clues are embedded throughout. The most likely region is **along the author's childhood road trip route from Statesville, NC through New Orleans to Houston, TX and back via the Ozarks**.
