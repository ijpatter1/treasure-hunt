## Chapter Summary

Chapter XXV tells the story of the Pokemon Box through the lens of the author's childhood memories. At age five and a half, Jon Collins-Black embarked on a two-week family road trip from Statesville (likely North Carolina) to Houston, Texas, traveling through New Orleans and returning via the Ozarks. The chapter opens with vivid memories of New Orleans - staying in a 5th floor hotel room and discovering a jazz bar in the French Quarter, where the young author wandered in alone, mesmerized by baritone saxophone music. These formative experiences of discovery and wonder set the thematic foundation for the chapter.

The narrative then jumps to July 2016, when the author and his wife Kimberly witnessed Pokemon Go's explosive release in Portland, Maine. This transition introduces the Pokemon theme and the treasure's namesake. The chapter provides background on Pokemon creator Satoshi Tajiri (nicknamed "Mr. Bug" for his childhood insect collecting) and details the two rare Charizard cards in the treasure. The handcrafted Japanese-style puzzle box by Seth Gould is described as the most expensive of the four smaller boxes, featuring over 250,000 chisel cuts and fine silver foil inlay.

The second half of the chapter follows a fantastical narrative where the author, accompanied by a Froakie Pokemon met at a jazz bar, hikes to hide the treasure "not far from some place visited along that journey." The trail features shaded oak forests, water features, and diverse wildlife. A key clue emerges: "We left the box not far from a place where locals turn up from time to time." The chapter concludes with a literal postscript emphasizing safety rules and confirming the treasure is within 3 miles of a road, not buried, and safely accessible.
