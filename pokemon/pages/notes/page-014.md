## Puzzle Box Specifications
- Designer: **Seth Gould**
- Material: **Brass** (legs are different material)
- Inlay: **Silver foil**
- Chisel cuts: **250,000+** individual cuts
- Lid alone: **220 pieces of silver foil**
- Style: Traditional Japanese puzzle box form
- Opening: Sliding panels (precise combination required)
- Instructions included but author encourages self-solving
- **Most expensive** of the four smaller boxes

## Seth Gould Quote
> This box has over 250,000 individual chisel cuts as part of the overlay technique, and that's a pretty modest estimation-it's probably quite a bit more. On the lid alone there are 220 individual pieces of fine silver foil.
