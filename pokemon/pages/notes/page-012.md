## Key Points

### Pokemon Go (July 2016)
- First augmented reality app
- 280,000,000 downloads in first week
- Portland streets full of players
- "Largest digital treasure hunt in history"

### Satoshi Tajiri (Pokemon Creator)
- Japanese creator of Pokemon
- Nicknamed "**Mr. Bug**" as child
- Inspired by **bug/insect collecting**
- Originally wanted to be entomologist
- Game Boy sparked game idea at college
- Concept: insects engaging in battle
- Original games: **Pokemon Red & Pokemon Green**

### Pokemon Franchise Stats
- 380 million physical game copies
- 1 billion downloads
- 1000+ TV episodes across 183 countries
- 34 billion cards sold
- Some cards sell for $1 million+

### Treasure Cards Details
**2002 Shining Charizard:**
- First edition, PSA 10 rating
- Only **252** gem mint copies exist
- Dark body with shine effect

**1999 Charizard Holo:**
- First edition, PSA 9 rating
- **715** exist at this grade
- **Shadowless** variant (no drop shadow in art box)
- More valuable despite lower grade
