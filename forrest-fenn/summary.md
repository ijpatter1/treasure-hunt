## The Poem

The complete 24-line poem from page 209:

```
I cruise along a road I know
and park my car just off the side,
near where I'd searched for heavy loads.
It was not there, although I'd tried.

Begin it where cool water flows
and follow through the canyon round.
Take in the rolling highs and lows
pass by a place where once was Brown.

From here you are more on your own.
The path is always drawing nigh.
The compass points now towards the home
of point and tree and seeing eye.

Stop just beyond the campers blaze.
A thinker's stone is all you need,
the spot is set with marvel gaze,
a white mark shows upon the seat.

Near here it's nestled all in dream.
I've other treasures left to hide.
I'll miss the golden bending stream,
but I must go back to my ride.

So listen well and hear me all,
your efforts will be worth the high.
If you can mark warm water's halt,
you'll bring it back full weight and dry.
```

## Author's Rules (Postscript)

Direct quotes from pages 210-211:

### Location Characteristics
> "I have hidden none of our five treasure boxes in a dangerous place."

> "No box is hidden under any body of water. You do not need to get into a raft, or canoe, or a water vessel of any kind to come to or find any of our treasure boxes."

> "No box is precariously close to a swift current or a high or dangerous ledge."

> "you will not need to scale a cliff or rock face to find a box."

> "There is no treasure hidden more than three miles from any road."

> "no treasure box is buried under the ground on public lands. So, there is no need to dig up or disturb public lands"

> "Avoid going on private property. This is a strict rule. No box is hidden on private property of any kind, and there is no need to walk through private property to arrive at the location of any box."

### On Interpreting the Postscript
> "There is no subterfuge in what I am going to say to you now. No double meanings. No misdirection. There is no subtext. No clues. No code. You should take what I say here as literally as you possibly can."

### On Insider Knowledge
> "throughout this entire process I did not give anyone I know clues to where I hid any of our five treasure boxes. My wife, my children, my parents, my siblings, none of my friends, and none of the people mentioned in this book were privy to any proprietary information."
