# Northern New Mexico Hypothesis - Summary

*Last updated: 2026-01-14*

---

## Current Status

Initial hypothesis formed based on author's explicit connection to Forrest Fenn's treasure hunt. The poem's language directly mirrors Fenn's poem and explicitly references locations the author searched for "heavy loads." Northern New Mexico is proposed as the most likely region within Fenn's search area due to its popularity among Fenn hunters and the author's extensive search experience there.

**Confidence:** Medium

---

## Strongest Evidence

1. **Direct Fenn search reference**: "near where I'd searched for heavy loads" (line 3) explicitly connects the hiding location to JCB's personal Fenn search sites

2. **Author's stated experience**: "I have traveled to all four stanzas in Forrest Fenn's treasure search area" confirms JCB searched the Rocky Mountain region extensively

3. **Fenn-style poem structure**: The 24-line, 6-stanza format mirrors Fenn's poem exactly, with parallel language ("warm water's halt," "where once was Brown")

4. **Rocky Mountains photo**: Page 206 shows JCB and Kimberly "somewhere in the Rocky Mountains" during his treasure hunting period

---

## Biggest Challenges

1. **No explicit state identification**: The poem doesn't name New Mexico; could equally apply to Colorado, Wyoming, or Montana

2. **Fenn treasure was in Wyoming**: JCB may have chosen to hide his box near where Fenn's was actually found rather than where he personally searched

3. **Unidentified landmarks**: "Thinker's stone," "campers blaze," and "marvel gaze" haven't been matched to specific NM locations yet
