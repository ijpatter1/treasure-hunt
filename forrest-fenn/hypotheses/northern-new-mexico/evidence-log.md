## 2026-01-14 - Initial Hypothesis Formation

**Source:** Chapter XXIV analysis (pages 203-211)
**Finding:** Multiple direct connections between JCB's poem and Forrest Fenn's original treasure hunt

**Key Evidence Identified:**

### Direct Evidence

1. **Page 209** - Author states: "I have traveled to all four stanzas in Forrest Fenn's treasure search area"
   - Impact: **Supports** - Confirms JCB searched Fenn's designated area (Rocky Mountains, Santa Fe to Canada)

2. **Poem line 3** - "near where I'd searched for heavy loads"
   - Impact: **Supports** - "Heavy loads" is direct quote from Fenn's poem; indicates box is near a JCB Fenn search site

3. **Page 206** - Photo caption: "Kimberly and I somewhere in the Rocky Mountains"
   - Impact: **Supports** - Visual confirmation of Rocky Mountain search activity

### Derived Evidence

4. **Poem line 8** - "pass by a place where once was Brown"
   - Impact: **Supports** - Mirrors Fenn's "Put in below the home of Brown"; uses Fenn's clue structure

5. **Poem line 23** - "If you can mark warm water's halt"
   - Impact: **Supports** - Nearly identical to Fenn's "Begin it where warm waters halt"

6. **Poem structure** - 24 lines, 6 stanzas, ABAB rhyme scheme
   - Impact: **Supports** - Deliberately mirrors Fenn's poem format

### Thematic Evidence

7. **Northern NM historical context** - Most popular Fenn search region for decade
   - Impact: **Supports** - High probability JCB searched here extensively

8. **Fenn's designated search area** - Rocky Mountains, 5,000-10,200 ft elevation
   - Impact: **Neutral** - Applies to NM but also CO, WY, MT

### Counter-Evidence Noted

9. **Fenn treasure actual location** - Found in Wyoming near Yellowstone
   - Impact: **Challenges** - JCB may have hidden box near actual Fenn site instead

10. **No explicit state reference** - Poem doesn't name New Mexico
    - Impact: **Challenges** - Theory relies on inference, not direct statement

**Analysis:**
The weight of evidence supports a Rocky Mountain location within Fenn's search area. Northern New Mexico is proposed as primary hypothesis due to:
- High search activity by Fenn hunters (including likely JCB)
- Multiple geographic features matching poem descriptions
- Author's emphasis on his personal search experience

**Confidence Adjustment:**
- Initial confidence: **Medium**
- Rationale: Strong thematic connection to Fenn, but specific NM evidence still circumstantial
