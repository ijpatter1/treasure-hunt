## Theory Statement

The Forrest Fenn Box is hidden in **Northern New Mexico**, likely in the Sangre de Cristo Mountains, Rio Grande corridor, or another location the author visited while searching for Forrest Fenn's original treasure. The treasure is accessible from a road the author knows well, near a canyon with flowing water, and features landmarks including a "thinker's stone," a "campers blaze," and a white-marked seat with a notable viewpoint.

## Supporting Analysis

### Why Northern New Mexico?

1. **Author's Personal Connection**: JCB explicitly states he searched "all four stanzas" of Fenn's search area. Northern New Mexico (around Santa Fe, the Sangre de Cristo range, and Rio Grande tributaries) was the most popular search region for Fenn hunters.

2. **"Heavy Loads" Reference**: The poem says "near where I'd searched for heavy loads" - a direct quote from Fenn's poem ("heavy loads and water high"). This indicates the box is near a location JCB personally searched for Fenn's treasure.

3. **"Where Once Was Brown"**: The phrasing "where once was Brown" (past tense) suggests a place connected to a former "Brown" - possibly a historical reference. Northern New Mexico has numerous Brown-related locations (Brown Lake, Brown Canyon, historic Brown family homesteads).

4. **Geographic Features Match**:
   - "Cool water flows" - mountain streams throughout the Sangre de Cristos
   - "Canyon round" - numerous canyons in the region
   - "Rolling highs and lows" - varied terrain typical of NM mountains
   - "Golden bending stream" - could reference the Rio Grande or tributaries

5. **Accessibility**: JCB states he "cruises along a road I know" - suggests a familiar route, likely one he drove repeatedly during his Fenn searches.

### Why NOT Wyoming/Yellowstone?

While Fenn's treasure was found in Wyoming, JCB says the area was "seemingly so well searched" and he "had no hard evidence to believe the location was illegitimate." This neutral language, combined with his emphasis on his own search experience ("where I'd searched"), suggests the box may be in a different area within Fenn's search zone that JCB personally explored.
